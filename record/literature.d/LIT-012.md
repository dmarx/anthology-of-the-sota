# LIT-012: Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization

Loshchilov et al. (2017) — [ARXIV-1711.05101](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101)

## Key takeaways

- **The inequivalence**: L2 regularization and weight decay coincide for plain
  SGD (rescaled by the learning rate) and **do not** for adaptive methods,
  because the adaptive denominator rescales the L2 gradient along with
  everything else
- Common Adam implementations use L2 "often calling it *weight decay* in what
  may be misleading due to the inequivalence we expose"
- **AdamW** applies the decay to the parameters, outside the adaptive
  rescaling
- **(i)** The optimal weight decay **decouples from the learning rate** for
  both SGD and Adam — turning a two-dimensional sweep into two
  one-dimensional ones
- **(ii)** Adam's generalization improves substantially, enough to compete
  with SGD with momentum on image classification, where it had been losing
