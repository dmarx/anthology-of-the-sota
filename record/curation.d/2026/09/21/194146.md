**The instruction was not to settle it ([#234](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/234)).** The issue asked whether
generated-corpus collapse and tool-assisted human collapse are the same
phenomenon, and argued itself into filing nothing: under the CLAUDE.md test it
would be a `THEORY`, sourced to papers none of which claim it, which is the
`DP-010` failure the session kept finding in other people's work. The owner's
answer was *we shouldn't make that judgement — file them separately, reference
each other in prose to draw attention to the potential connection.*

**That turns out to be a better shape than either alternative.** Asserting the
connection would have invented a claim. Filing nothing would have left the
observation in an issue, where it is invisible to anyone browsing either
topic. Two documents that name each other and say *we have not joined these*
put the question where the next reader will hit it, at the cost of one section
in each.

**Only one document had to be written.** Path B already had `THEORY-051`.
Path A did not — its account lived as a paragraph in `SOTA-295`'s conditions,
under the heading "They agree about the disease", noting that `SOTA-172`'s
mechanism for why generated corpora hurt is n-gram over-concentration and that
`LIT-485` measured n-gram over-concentration in a generated corpus
independently. That is a theory-shaped observation sitting inside a practice,
which is what `ADR-031` split the schemes to fix.

**The reason they stay apart got sharper while writing it.** The issue's three
arguments were about metrics, about the selection step, and about the harms
differing in kind. A fourth is stronger and was not in the issue:
`THEORY-051`'s mechanism is **scarcity** — a feature predicts partly by being
uncommon — and scarcity has no counterpart in the generated-corpus case,
because a pretraining corpus is not competing with itself for a rivalrous
outcome. The two could share a statistical signature entirely and still have
unrelated causes. That argument is now in both documents.

**`THEORY-051` goes to v2 for prose that changes nothing.** The account is
untouched; only a cross-reference was added. Bumped anyway with a `history:`
entry saying so, on the principle that the version is what tells a reader
whether the page they remember is the page they are looking at — `ADR-019`.
