# LIT-107: MiDaS v3.1 -- A Model Zoo for Robust Monocular Relative Depth Estimation

Ranftl et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2307.14460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14460)

## Key takeaways

- Improved depth estimation
- Cross-dataset generalization
- Robust performance
- Efficient architecture

## Standing in the anthology

**Carries no practice, and this is a reading rather than an assumption.**

A model zoo and a training-mixture report for monocular depth. Its transferable half — mix many datasets with an objective invariant to their differing scale conventions — is a genuine `data-pipeline` idea, but the note carries no evidence about it beyond the released models, which is the [ADR-017](../decisions.d/ADR-017.md) case: shipping is not measuring.
