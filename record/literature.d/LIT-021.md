# LIT-021: Mish: A Self Regularized Non-Monotonic Activation Function

Misra et al. (2019) — [ARXIV-1908.08681](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08681)

## Key takeaways

- Proposes `f(x) = x·tanh(softplus(x))` — smooth, **non-monotonic**, and
  explicitly positioned relative to the **Swish family** `x·σ(βx)` rather than
  as an unrelated construction
- The offered explanation is that the **first derivative's behaviour acts as a
  regularizer**; the paper calls the function "self-regularized" on that basis
- **+2.1 AP50** over Leaky ReLU on YOLOv4 / CSP-DarkNet-53 / MS-COCO, and
  **≈1% top-1** over ReLU on ResNet-50 / ImageNet-1k, *"while keeping all other
  network parameters and hyperparameters constant"* — the right comparison, and
  the one activation papers most often fail to make
- BMVC 2020, and CNN-vision throughout
