# LIT-108: 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering

Kerbl et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2308.04079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04079)

## Key takeaways

- Real-time neural rendering
- Gaussian primitive representation
- Efficient view synthesis
- Dynamic scene optimization

## Standing in the anthology

**Carries no practice, and this is a reading rather than an assumption.**

3D Gaussian splatting is a rendering representation and an optimization procedure over primitives. Hugely consequential, and the reason it carries no practice is *not* that the topics cannot express it. It is that the optimization is over explicit primitives rather than network weights — densification and pruning schedules over a growing set of Gaussians — which is a real optimization procedure the record has no comparable entry for. A gap worth naming rather than a paper worth declining.
