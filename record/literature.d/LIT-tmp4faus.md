# LIT-tmp4faus: Async-HFL: Efficient and Robust Asynchronous Federated Learning in Hierarchical IoT Networks

Yu et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2301.06646](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06646)

## What it is

Async-HFL: hierarchical federated learning over long-tailed wireless delays,
selecting devices by gradient diversity rather than by latency alone.

From the paper's own abstract:

> Federated Learning (FL) has gained increasing interest in recent years
> as a distributed on-device learning paradigm. However, multiple
> challenges remain to be addressed for deploying FL in real-world
> Internet-of-Things (IoT) networks with hierarchies. Although existing
> works have proposed various approaches to account data heterogeneity,
> system heterogeneity, unexpected stragglers and scalibility, none of
> them provides a systematic solution to address all of the challenges in
> a hierarchical and unreliable IoT network.
