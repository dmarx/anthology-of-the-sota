The instruction was to take classifier-free guidance. The measurement before
filing said something worse than "a gap": the record had spent the afternoon
*adding* dependencies on a paper it had never held.

## The gap, and how recently it was dug

`2207.12598` is named — as the technique, or as the `guidance_scale` knob — in
nineteen documents here, and was held by none. Three practices depend on the
guidance weight specifically:

| document | the dependency | when acquired |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SOTA-202` | clamping exists because high `w` pushes `x̂₀` out of range | at filing |
| `SOTA-203` | scoped to *unguided* sampling, v2 | **today** |
| `SOTA-410` | the sampler constraint that makes the clamp available | **today** |
| `SOTA-307` | search the guidance scale per cell, ±0.05 noise quantified | at filing |

Two of those four rows are hours old. The DPM-Solver++ unit found that a
`universal` practice had never named guidance, fixed it, and in fixing it made
the record's unread-paper problem worse than it was that morning. **Closing a
scope gap can deepen a sourcing gap**, and nothing in the toolchain notices,
because `luria lint` checks relations between documents and has no opinion about
a bare arXiv id in prose.

## The trap, which is in the paper's own sentence

The numbers are Table 1, ImageNet 64×64, `p_uncond = 0.1`, 50 000 samples per
point: `w = 0.1` gives FID **1.55** and IS **66.11**; `w = 4.0` gives FID
**26.22** and IS **260.2**. Seventeen-fold FID degradation against a near
quintupling of IS, from one checkpoint, with one dial.

The paper describes that as

> with FID monotonically decreasing and IS monotonically increasing with `w`

which is correct and reads backwards. FID *quality* decreases; the FID *number*
rises. Anyone who takes that clause without the table alongside it concludes that
turning the weight up improves FID, which is the exact inversion of the trade the
paper is reporting. It is quoted in `LIT-693` for that reason, and the
practice states the trade as a table rather than a sentence.

## What the source did to `SOTA-307`

`SOTA-307` is about variance in reported numbers. Its guidance items told a
reader to search the classifier-free guidance scale per cell, to report the
tolerance, and to treat ±0.05 on the scale as a noise floor — all of it with no
paper behind the technique being searched over.

The interesting part is that the source does not shore up the document's
argument, it **replaces** it with a stronger one. The document had guidance
filed under seed-and-tolerance hygiene. Against a seventeen-fold swing from one
checkpoint, a ±0.05 seed-noise floor is a rounding error: fixing the weight is
**prior to** any seed question, not a refinement of it. Recommendation, status
and consensus are unchanged at v4; what changed is which of its items is
load-bearing.

**Generalising:** a missing `source:` is usually read as a document that cannot
prove what it says. This was a document whose own reasoning was weaker than the
evidence it lacked — and it had been weaker for as long as it had been correct,
with nothing to flag it, because an unsourced *item* inside a well-sourced
practice fails no check.

## The theory, and a `promote_when` that refuses the paper's win

The paper raises a good question about the method it replaces. Classifier
guidance follows an image classifier's input gradient; FID and IS are computed
with an Inception classifier; that is mechanically what a targeted adversarial
attack does, so the predecessor's reported gains might be partly an artefact of
the scoring apparatus. Classifier-free guidance is the control: same trade, no
classifier anywhere.

And the control works — the trade survives the classifier's removal, so the
phenomenon is real. `THEORY-109` is `Proposed` anyway, and the reason is
worth keeping: classifier-free guidance *beats* classifier-guided ADM-G on FID,
and that win is scored with the same Inception network the suspicion is about.
**Beating a suspect measurement at its own game says nothing about whether the
measurement was suspect.** So the `promote_when` explicitly rejects the result
the paper actually obtained and asks for a metric with no classifier in it —
human preference, or a self-supervised feature distance.

A control can establish that an effect exists and be structurally incapable of
sizing the artefact it was built to rule out. Those are two claims, and the paper
only makes the first.

## Deliberately not in this unit

`2105.05233` — Dhariwal & Nichol, the paper classifier-free guidance is named
against — is still unheld, and this record cites it four times as an instrument
or an authority: `LIT-448` says they "ablated" a design choice, `NOTE-340` uses
their U-Net and audits a throughput claim against their run, `LIT-630`'s
controlled path comparison uses the same U-Net, and `LIT-676`'s entire ImageNet
table is run on their model. It is the next thing to take.

It is not in this one on the reasoning that kept Wettig out of the BERT unit:
filing a paper and its named antagonist together lets the sourcing of each rest
on the other's summary rather than on the paper. `THEORY-109` is a claim
*about* `2105.05233`, held with only one side read, and it says so.
