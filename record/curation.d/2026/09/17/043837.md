Finished the geometry line for [#154](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/154) by filing the matching stage — the half I
deliberately left out of the first pass, on the grounds that DUSt3R and VGGT
make matching an *output*, so the papers that made it a better *input* need
saying what they were for rather than appending to the practice that replaced
them.

Having written them, the line reads better than I expected, and the reason is
worth recording.

## It is three removals, not three improvements

- [LIT-386](../../../../literature.d/LIT-386.md) (SuperGlue, 2019) removes the **hand-designed matching
  heuristics** — the ratio test and its relatives — and learns assignment costs
  with a GNN solved by differentiable optimal transport. Keeps the detector.
- [LIT-387](../../../../literature.d/LIT-387.md) (LoFTR, 2021) removes the **detector**. Dense coarse-to-fine
  matching with cross attention.
- [LIT-385](../../../../literature.d/LIT-385.md) and [LIT-384](../../../../literature.d/LIT-384.md) (DUSt3R 2023, VGGT 2025, [SOTA-236](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-236.md)) remove **matching
  as a stage**. Predict geometry; recover correspondence from the output.

What makes it a line rather than a sequence of better numbers is that **each
removal is justified by a failure the previous framing could not state**.
SuperGlue makes matching learned and strong, which is what makes it possible
to notice that the remaining weakness is upstream. LoFTR names it: detectors
"struggle to produce repeatable interest points" in low-texture areas, and a
matcher cannot recover a correspondence that was never proposed. Once the
detector is gone, matching is a stage with nothing protecting it either.

## The transferable claim is about staged systems

LoFTR's mechanism is that descriptors become **conditioned on both images** —
self and cross attention mean a location's representation depends on the image
it is being matched to.

A detect-then-describe pipeline structurally cannot do that. Description runs
per image, before any pair exists, so a descriptor must be a summary useful
against *every* possible partner. It is conservative by construction, and
**the cost of that conservatism is invisible while the stage exists**, because
nothing in the pipeline can measure what a less conservative descriptor would
have bought.

That is the sentence worth keeping, and it is not about vision: a stage
computed before its consumer exists must be conservative, and the price only
becomes legible once the stage is removed. The record has the same shape at
[SOTA-227](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-227.md), where draft-and-verify replaced hand-tuned decoding heuristics.

## A practice filed `converged` on one source

[SOTA-237](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-237.md) rests on one paper for its recommendation, which by the usual
rule would make it `unreplicated`.

Filed `converged` on structural grounds instead: [SOTA-236](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-236.md) records systems
that remove the matching stage *entirely* and recover correspondence as an
output. That step only makes sense if the detector was already gone — the
later line **presupposes** this one. Agreement by presupposition rather than
by replication, which is a kind of evidence the consensus vocabulary does not
have a word for and which the `consensus_note` has to carry in prose.

Worth flagging as a possible gap in the vocabulary rather than quietly using
the nearest word. The reason I did not reach for `unreplicated` is that it
would be actively misleading — it implies nobody has tested the claim, when in
fact a whole subsequent literature is built on it holding.

## SuperGlue carries no practice, deliberately

Its claim was overtaken twice in eighteen months. Filing it live would
recommend the middle step of a line whose end the record holds.

But filing nothing would have left [SOTA-236](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-236.md) as a conclusion with no argument —
a reader would see "predict geometry directly" with no account of what was
tried first or why it stopped. `ADR-032`'s convention covers exactly this:
a paper admitted for what it establishes rather than for what it recommends.
