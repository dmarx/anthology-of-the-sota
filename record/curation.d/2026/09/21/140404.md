The QES paper reports three arms per cell: a quantized zeroth-order baseline,
a full-residual variant described as the oracle, and QES, which approximates
that oracle to save memory. Six cells.

In two of them QES beats the oracle. At INT8 it scores 26.35 against 22.10 on
the 1.5B model and 37.40 against 33.30 on the 3B. In one it loses by ten
points — W8A8 on 3B, 21.35 against 31.70.

An approximation cannot be better than the thing it approximates. So those
gaps are not information about the approximation; they are the measurement
noise, printed. And they are the same size as the differences the paper
spends its analysis on.

No repeats, no seeds, no error bars appear anywhere in the paper.

## Why this is worth a paragraph rather than an eye-roll

Because it gives a reader a *free* variance estimate on a paper that does not
report one, and the record can use it. The oracle-versus-approximation
comparison is the one place where the expected sign is known in advance.
Wherever the observed sign contradicts it, the magnitude of the contradiction
is a lower bound on run-to-run spread.

Here that bound is about four to ten accuracy points. Which means the one
comparison in the paper that survives it is the headline one: QuZO at 5.25%
against QES at 16.00% on INT4/1.5B. That margin is real. Most of the rest of
the table is not resolvable.

The caption says QES "was only slightly lower than with full residuals". It
is ten points lower in one of six cells and higher in two others. The
sentence is defensible on average and is not true of the row a reader
deciding on W8A8 would look at.

Two sentences in §4.2 also disagree with the table — QES is credited with
18.00% where the table gives it 16.00 and the oracle 18.05, and 14.25 → 31.85
is called a doubling of "the base model" where 14.25 is the baseline method's
number and the base model's is 2.80. Neither changes the conclusion. Both are
the kind of thing that propagates when a number is quoted from prose.

## What survives, and it is the good part

The mechanism, which does not depend on the experiments at all.

Decompose the quantizer as identity plus error and expand a `T`-step
trajectory. If every update is smaller than half the grid spacing — which is
the ordinary case in fine-tuning — the accumulated quantization loss cancels
the accumulated ideal update *term for term*. Not approximately: `w_T = w_0`.
The optimizer is stationary, and no amount of patience fixes it because there
is no residual drift to accumulate.

That is a better statement of the problem than "small updates get rounded
away", which suggests a slow leak rather than a wall. And it makes the remedy
obvious in hindsight: the thing being discarded is exactly the thing that
would have moved the weight, so keep it. Carry the remainder and the physical
weights track the high-precision trajectory within `Δ/2` for every `T` —
bounded, not growing.

`THEORY-042` is `Active` on that basis while `SOTA-283` stays
`Proposed`. A correct explanation is not evidence that a method is worth
adopting, and keeping those apart is what `ADR-031` split the schemes for.

## The line, and the ledger

`SOTA-154` — fine-tune with evolution strategies instead of policy-gradient
RL — carries a consensus note that counts which results come from inside the
Qiu line and which do not. That ledger is the practice's most valuable
property, and it only works if every new arrival gets sorted into it.

This paper's corresponding author is `LIT-211`'s first author. Inside the
line. The consensus does not move, and all four new documents say so rather
than letting "another ES paper works" do quiet work.

`THEORY-006` went further and wrote the misuse down before it happened: its
`promote_when` names "a further post-training method that works and is read
back as evidence for the account" as what would *not* satisfy it. QES is
precisely that. The account predicted its own misreading, and the only thing
left to do was not commit it.

That is what a promotion condition is for, and it is the second time this week
one has caught something — the first being `SOTA-122`'s, which says confirming
its diagnosis is not the result it needs.
