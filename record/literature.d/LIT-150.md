# LIT-150: Don't be lazy: CompleteP enables compute-efficient deep transformers

Dey et al., Cerebras with Princeton, Harvard and ETH Zürich (2025) — [ARXIV-2505.01618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01618)

## Key takeaways

- Parametrizations are rules for how model and optimizer hyperparameters
  change with size. Studying compute efficiency under different ones, the
  paper finds that some — including µP as commonly used — do not transfer
  the optimal base learning rate across changes in depth, so a value tuned
  shallow is wrong deep.
- A second failure is *lazy learning*: with the wrong depth scaling, deep
  layers barely move from initialisation and the network behaves like a
  shallower one.
- CompleteP — the parametrization with depth exponent α = 1 — achieves both
  depth-wise transfer and non-lazy learning in every layer. Against µP it
  saves 11.8% of FLOPs for optimally shaped models and 34.4% for a
  179-layer model.
- A wider range of width-to-depth ratios stays compute-efficient under it,
  which frees model shape to follow hardware constraints.
