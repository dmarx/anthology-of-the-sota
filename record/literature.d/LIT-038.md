# LIT-038: Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models

Song et al. (2020) — [ARXIV-2010.02502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02502)

## Key takeaways

- Generalises DDPM to a family of **non-Markovian** forward processes that share
  DDPM's *exact* training objective — so the sampler can be replaced on an
  already-trained network with **no retraining**. Choosing the deterministic
  member gives DDIM
- **10–50× fewer steps**: quality comparable to a 1000-step model within 20–100
  steps; on CelebA a 100-step DDPM matches a **20-step** DDIM. Sampling time
  scales linearly with trajectory length, so step count is the whole cost
- Determinism buys what stochastic sampling cannot: with `x_T` fixed, high-level
  features survive any trajectory length, so `x_T` is a **latent code** —
  interpolation in it is semantically meaningful, and samples can be **encoded
  and reconstructed** with very low error
- **This is a discrete-time paper.** Its only continuous connection is §4.3,
  relating the deterministic sampler to neural **ODEs**

**Filed a year after the reading, and the reading is why.** [NOTE-067](../notes.d/NOTE-067.md) ends
"nothing is sourced to this paper and this reading files no practice" — the
policy of a pass that deliberately filed nothing, not a judgement that there
was nothing to file. The audit in [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/121) is what came back for it.

**Two of the four takeaways this document carried were about a different
paper.** "Continuous-time formulation of diffusion models" and "connection to
SDE theory" describe Song et al.'s *Score-Based Generative Modeling through
Stochastic Differential Equations* — a separate 2020 paper. DDIM is a
discrete-time, non-Markovian reformulation with a deterministic sampler.

That is the `#114` failure mode appearing in this population, where the first
fifteen readings had found only vagueness. Worth recording: this corpus is not
uniformly "true but empty".

The reading also supplies a cause the record was carrying only the effect of.
`LIT-075`'s reading treats sampler improvements transferring to *pretrained*
networks (ImageNet-64, FID 2.07 → 1.55, no retraining) as EDM's evidence for
its design-space framing. That transfer is possible **because** of the shared
objective established here.
