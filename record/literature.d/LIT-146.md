# LIT-146: Power Scheduler: A Batch Size and Token Number Agnostic Learning Rate Scheduler

Shen et al., IBM Research (2024) — [ARXIV-2408.13359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13359)

## Key takeaways

- The optimal peak learning rate depends on batch size, training length,
  model size and more, and searching for it at the final scale is what
  makes it expensive. The paper studies these correlations under the WSD
  schedule ([LIT-144](LIT-144.md)) and finds that the optimal learning rate follows a
  power law in the number of training tokens and the batch size.
- The Power scheduler applies that law directly: the learning rate decays
  as a power of the tokens seen, capped at a maximum, with WSD's final
  decay on top — so it is agnostic to the total token count and to the
  batch size, and the same setting can be reused when either changes.
- Combined with Maximal Update Parametrization ([LIT-148](LIT-148.md)), one set of
  hyperparameters carries across model sizes as well; the paper trains its
  3B dense and MoE models this way.
