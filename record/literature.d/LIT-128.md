# LIT-128: Falcon-H1R: Pushing the Reasoning Frontiers with a Hybrid Model for Efficient Test-Time Scaling

Falcon-LLM Team (2026) — [ARXIV-2601.02346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02346)

## Key takeaways

- A 7B reasoning-optimised model built from Falcon-H1-7B by further
  supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement-learning scaling; it matches or
  outperforms state-of-the-art reasoning models two to seven times its size
  on reasoning-heavy benchmarks.
- The authors attribute the result to data curation and targeted training
  rather than size, and frame efficiency in three dimensions at once:
  accuracy, tokens per solution, and inference speed, the last from the
  parallel hybrid architecture.
- Parallel test-time scaling with DeepConf — many samples, confidence-
  weighted — is where the inference speed pays off, and where the model is
  positioned as a backbone.
