## Method

As in `#330`: delegated readers produced full-paper extractions, and I
wrote the notes. One reader answered a follow-up question about how Self
Forcing's baselines were run. The answer changed what that paper can be
cited for. It is recorded below because the first reading did not
distinguish the two.

## What the filing changed about practices already held

**The largest correction came from filing the substrate.** `SOTA-266` said
rectified flow with uniform timesteps "does not win", and glossed this as
why the straight path's advantage "had looked equivocal for two years".
The gloss had no source. Both originating papers used uniform timesteps
and won controlled comparisons against VP diffusion:

- Flow Matching, Table 1: CIFAR-10 FID 6.35 against 8.06 on the same U-Net.
- Rectified Flow, Table 1a: FID 2.58 against 3.93.

SD3's finding is real, and it is about ranking against tuned ε-prediction in
latent text-to-image at scale. The practice had generalized it. This is the
`#326` shape again. The record held the paper that carried the claim forward
and not the papers that made it, and a sentence about the absent papers had
been written without reading them.

**`SOTA-333` became `contested`, and nothing in it was refuted.** Self
Forcing argues against inference-time noised history in one uncited sentence
(§1). Its controlled table does not test that. A follow-up confirmed the
Diffusion Forcing baselines roll out on clean context. The table does test
the training half, and the result is split: per-token noise loses to teacher
forcing in the many-step rows and wins after distillation. My first draft of
the Self Forcing note said diffusion forcing "scores below teacher forcing
in both" variants. That was wrong, and I caught it by rereading the table
before running lint. The lint then required `contested_by:` once the
consensus said `contested`, which is the check doing its job.

## Unit 5: two filed, one declined

- **Filed, `Proposed` / `converged`:** full 3D attention (CogVideoX's
  ablation, whose numbers are in a figure I could not extract) and
  video-native captioning (Movie Gen Table 8b). Both have the [DP-005](../design-principles.md#dp-5) shape:
  every report adopts, one measures.
- **Declined: the text encoder.** `#331` said to file it only if the
  evidence supported a recommendation. It does not. Four reports take four
  positions:
  - T5 or umT5: Wan, CogVideoX
  - a multimodal LLM with a bidirectional refiner: HunyuanVideo
  - several encoders: Movie Gen, Step-Video
  - Wan's own ablation has Qwen-VL 0.1 FID *ahead* of the umT5 it kept

  There is no cross-test. A practice would have to say "any of these", which
  is not a practice. The disagreement stays in the LIT notes, where it is
  recorded as a disagreement.
