# LIT-142: DeepSeek-V3.2: Pushing the Frontier of Open Large Language Models

DeepSeek-AI (2025) — [ARXIV-2512.02556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02556)

## Key takeaways

- DeepSeek Sparse Attention: a *lightning indexer* with few heads,
  implementable in FP8, computes an index score for each query against
  every preceding token; a top-k selector keeps the highest-scoring KV
  entries and the attention runs only over those. Integrated with
  Multi-Head Latent Attention in its multi-query mode.
- Retrofitted by continued pretraining onto a model that already had dense
  attention — the DeepSeek-V3.1-Terminus checkpoint, itself a
  mixture-of-experts, already extended to 128K. A short warm-up initialises
  the indexer with dense attention still on and every other parameter
  frozen: 1,000 steps of 16 sequences × 128K tokens, 2.1B tokens in all;
  then the top-k selector is switched on and all parameters train.
- The report also covers V3.2's post-training and reinforcement-learning
  recipe; the anthology draws on it for the attention.
