The owner asked for a check of this journal: which readings did it name, promise
or queue, and then skip? **All 236 curation entries** (2026-08-24 to 09-25) were
read, along with every `Skimmed`, `Deferred` and `Proposed` LIT and NOTE, and issue
#290's comments, since the journal cites that issue's promoted queue without
listing it. Each candidate was checked against every LIT's `arxiv:` and
`title:`, every NOTE's `paper:`, and later entries.

About 90 candidates turned out to be done. **What remains:**

| group | works |
| --- | ---: |
| (A) promised or named to file, never done | ~40 |
| (B) held back on a trigger that has since fired | 5 |
| (C) held back, trigger not fired | ~40 |
| (D) `Skimmed` NOTEs owing a full read | 8, plus 2 read with no NOTE |

## The mechanism

**Most of (A) was never declined. It was carried forward and then dropped.**
Islamov et al. was "still unfiled" in five consecutive entries (09/20 to 09/21),
then it stopped appearing in the lists. It was never filed and never declined.
Other items went the same way:

- the dropout trio of 09/17 ("the one to file next");
- the two papers promised "a unit of its own" on 09/22;
- #290's promoted list, of which 09/24/163122 said "the next pass on #290 should
  work the promoted list".

A "next" in a journal entry is a promise nothing enforces. The next session
reads the entry, takes the first item, and the rest age out of the window.

(B) is the sharper version. **ReLoRA waited on a decision, #88. The decision
landed as `ADR-042`, and ADR-042's own status note then said ReLoRA "is still
unfiled".** The trigger fired, and the thing waiting on it was not told.

## Taken in this pass

1. **MDLM and SEDD** (LIT-tmptr16a, LIT-tmpfb0m4). Read, and added to
   `SOTA-157` as corroborating sources for its "masked" half only. They support
   choosing masked over other discrete diffusion. **Neither supports diffusion
   over autoregression.** MDLM's own retrained AR baseline wins in-domain by
   10–32% perplexity. SEDD's "beats GPT-2" is 3 of 5 zero-shot sets against a
   model trained on different data. `SOTA-157` now says so. `SOTA-254` gets their
   sampling-cost numbers, not a source.
2. **Dhariwal & Nichol** (LIT-tmpcq7qo). The record cited it four times as an
   instrument. Read, it corrects three of those citations:
   - The ImageNet classifier scale of 8.0 that `LIT-676`, `SOTA-203` and
     `SOTA-410` quote is a stress test, about three times the authors' own
     tuned value.
   - The network `THEORY-109` said is differentiated is the wrong one.
   - Its claim that large scales produce "no adversarial examples" is asserted
     and never tested.
3. **DeepSeek-V4 re-read** (LIT-139, NOTE-tmpr3mtt). The repair promised on
   09/16. **V4 keeps V3's multi-token prediction as a training objective and
   never mentions speculative decoding.** `LIT-185`'s "ship the same
   arrangement" is withdrawn, along with the claim's echoes in `SOTA-227`,
   `SOTA-162` and `LIT-163`. LIT-139's own summary is corrected in four places.
4. **ReLoRA's restart rule** (SOTA-tmppbzba, `Proposed`). Merging and
   reinitialising an adapter needs **both** an optimizer reset and a re-warm.
   Either alone diverges or does nothing, in the paper's 130M ablation from a
   random start. That ablation has never been run from a warm start, which is
   what `promote_when` asks for. `LIT-104` goes to `Active`, and `ADR-042`'s
   status note now says ReLoRA is filed.
5. **Islamov et al.** (LIT-tmp87rqx), which joins the edge-of-stability half of
   the optimizer cluster to the non-Euclidean-norm half. The edge of stability
   holds for steepest descent under other norms, but only when sharpness is
   measured **in the update's norm**. `THEORY-035` gains a section saying the
   top Hessian eigenvalue is the Euclidean case of its account, not the
   account.
