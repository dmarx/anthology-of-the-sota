title: 'Filing the trunks, and finding that one word was doing two jobs'
created: '2026-09-22T02:38:54'

**The last unit surfaced its own next unit.** Filing the self-attention
clustering paper meant writing, twice, that the record holds neither Dong et
al. nor Linformer — while four documents lean on "rank collapse" as an
established premise and two practices rest on attention being low rank. Those
are the two trunks, and this files them.

**The unplanned finding is the one worth keeping: "rank collapse" is doing two
jobs in this record.** Dong et al.'s rank collapse is token representations
converging to a rank-1 matrix through depth, in a single forward pass, at a
doubly exponential rate, and it is prevented by the residual connection every
real transformer has. `LIT-350`'s rank collapse — in its own words, "as
training progresses, weight matrices exhibit rank collapse, converging to
low-rank subspaces" — is weight matrices over training time, which is also
what `LIT-516` measures. Different object, different axis, different remedy,
same name.

**I checked whether that conflation was ours before saying anything about
it.** `NOTE-105` could have been sloppy; it is not. It reports its source,
and the source uses the term that way. So this is terminological drift in the
field, the record now holds both usages, and the fix is a table saying which
is which rather than an edit to anybody's reading. That distinction — is the
defect ours or the literature's — cost one PDF fetch and changed what the
finding is.

**The best single line in the paper is an algebraic aside.** Layer
normalization cannot mitigate rank collapse, because `LN` absorbs into
`W̃_h = W_h D_LN^{-1}` plus a rank-1 shift, and right multiplication cannot
increase rank. Two lines, no experiment, and it rules out an entire family of
would-be remedies. The record holds a large normalization cluster and had
nothing saying what normalization is *not* for.

**The status turns on the gap between what is proved and what is quoted.**
Everyone cites this paper for "skip connections prevent rank collapse". What is
proved is that *some* parameterization preserves the residual, because one path
skips every layer — and the authors say plainly that their upper bound with
skip connections is "vacuously large" and pose the tight lower bound as an open
challenge to the community. `Proposed`, with that challenge as the promotion
condition. A paper being right and being load-bearing is not the same as the
thing it is cited for being proved.

**Linformer needed the opposite care.** The record already holds a verdict on
it — `NOTE-005` has FlashAttention undermining this literature's premise by
showing the cost model was wrong. That verdict is about `O(n)` being the
right optimization target and says nothing about whether `P` is low rank,
which is the part `SOTA-184` and `SOTA-147` have been standing on without a
source. Filed `Active`, because the record retires a document when its claim
stops holding, and the measurement did not stop holding when the method was
overtaken.

**Two papers, one fact, opposite readings.** Linformer measures the attention
matrix as low rank and builds a compression method on it. Dong et al. proves
that the same structure, taken to depth, destroys the representation. Whether
low-rank `P` is an opportunity or a pathology depends on whether you are
compressing the matrix or passing information through it. Neither cites the
other on this. Recorded in both notes; not joined, because no one has made the
argument.

**And a trade nobody frames as a trade.** MLPs counteract rank collapse in
proportion to their Lipschitz constant, and Dong et al. states the cost against
its own remedy: larger Lipschitz constants make a model less robust and more
sensitive to input perturbations. `THEORY-064`, filed earlier today, holds
that transformers' robustness *is* their bias toward low-sensitivity functions.
So the standard fix for rank collapse spends the thing the other account says
robustness is made of. Three years apart, no citation either way. Into the open
questions of the new note, not into a claim.

**Neural collapse deferred rather than tacked on.** `LIT-528` names it as a
resemblance, the record holds nothing on it, and it is a different subject —
classification-layer geometry at the end of training. Nothing filed here leans
on it. It gets a unit or it gets nothing; what it does not get is three
sentences at the end of someone else's.
