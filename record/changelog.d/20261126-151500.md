### Added

- [LIT-tmplifgh](record/literature.d/LIT-tmplifgh.md), Sparse Autoencoders Trained on the Same Data Learn Different
  Features (arXiv 2501.16615): SAEs that differ only in initialization seed
  share 42% of latents at 32K on Pythia 160M and 30% at 131K on Llama 3 8B.
  About 35% of a base SAE's latents recur in none of eight other seeds.
  Read in full at arXiv v2 as [NOTE-tmpf455e](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpf455e.md). The note records that the
  abstract's "controlling for sparsity" TopK claim is not described in the
  body, and that overlap rises with training length, against the paper's
  "diverge with scale".
- [SOTA-tmprih86](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmprih86.md), `Proposed`: before resting a claim on a particular SAE
  latent, train a second seed and check that the latent recurs. It is held
  at `Proposed` until a downstream test shows the check changes
  conclusions.
