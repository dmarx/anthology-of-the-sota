# LIT-122: Muon is Scalable for LLM Training

Liu et al. (2025) — [ARXIV-2502.16982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16982)

## Key takeaways

- Muon, the orthogonalised-momentum optimizer for matrix parameters, had
  strong results at small scale and no evidence above it. Two changes carry
  it: apply weight decay, and scale each parameter's update so its RMS
  matches AdamW's, which keeps the learning rate and weight decay tuned for
  AdamW transferable without a sweep.
- Scaling-law experiments put Muon at roughly 2× the computational
  efficiency of AdamW under compute-optimal training.
- Moonlight, a 3B-active / 16B-total mixture-of-experts model trained on
  5.7T tokens, moves the Pareto frontier at that scale with fewer training
  FLOPs than prior models.
- A distributed implementation that is memory-optimal and communication-
  efficient is released; the orthogonalisation step is the part that needs
  care in a sharded setting.

## Standing in the anthology

The recipe [SOTA-121](../practices.d/SOTA-121.md) adopts, confirmed independently at tiny scale by
[LIT-119](LIT-119.md). The optimizer itself was introduced in a 2024 blog post by Jordan
and collaborators, which is not in the corpus; this paper is where the
production form and the scaling evidence live. [LIT-132](LIT-132.md) adds QK-Clip to the
same optimizer at 1T parameters ([SOTA-131](../practices.d/SOTA-131.md)), and [LIT-131](LIT-131.md) carries it to 2.8T.
