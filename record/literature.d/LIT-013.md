# LIT-013: Population Based Training of Neural Networks

Jaderberg et al. (2017) — [ARXIV-1711.09846](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09846)

## Key takeaways

- A population of models trains in parallel and asynchronously; each member
  periodically **exploits** (copies a better member's weights *and*
  hyperparameters) then **explores** (perturbs them), continuing from the
  copied weights rather than restarting
- The output is a **schedule, not a setting** — because hyperparameters change
  mid-run, PBT expresses time-varying values no fixed-hyperparameter search
  can. On WMT En-De it rediscovers a small initial learning rate, a jump of
  ~3 orders of magnitude, then exponential decay
- Beats an **already highly tuned** Transformer baseline: validation BLEU
  23.71 → 24.23, test 22.30 → 22.65, and trains faster
- **Population of 20–40** is enough for consistent improvement; ≤10 shows high
  variance and local optima, since the algorithm is greedy
- The GAN experiment deliberately **selects on CIFAR Inception score and
  reports ImageNet Inception score**, to avoid selecting on the test metric
