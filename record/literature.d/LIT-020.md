# LIT-020: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions

Wu et al. (2019) — [ARXIV-1901.10430](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.10430)

## Key takeaways

- **LightConv**: a depthwise separable convolution that additionally **shares
  weights across channel groups** and **softmax-normalizes the kernel over its
  temporal window** — the normalisation is what makes it a weighted average
  rather than a free linear map, which is the property being borrowed from
  attention
- **DynamicConv**: the same, with the kernel **predicted from the current
  token alone**. So mixing weights are conditioned on *one* position, never on
  the pair being mixed — it cannot condition on what it is attending to
- **29.7 BLEU on WMT English–German**, matching the best self-attention results
  of the time, and matching the best on English–French. Kernel sizes 3, 7, 15,
  31 across blocks: the receptive field is grown by **depth**, and 31 tokens is
  the ceiling
- The result is therefore a claim about the task: for translation at these
  lengths, the *content of the other token* apparently does not determine how
  much it should be mixed in
