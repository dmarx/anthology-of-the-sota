### Added

- **`LIT-tmpt0oos` — Child et al. (2019), *Generating Long Sequences with
  Sparse Transformers* (`ARXIV-1904.10509`).** The origin of the sliding
  window the record uses in seven other notes and never filed: causal
  attention factorized into a local window of the previous `l` positions
  and a second head that escapes it, with `l` close to `sqrt(n)`.

  Filed for provenance, not to reopen a position. Nothing is sourced to it,
  and the record's answer on approximate sparse attention stays `SOTA-086`
  — exact attention made fast.

  Two arguments in it the record did not have: the paper states the
  window-plus-escape pairing as a **connectivity requirement** rather than a
  convenience, and its enwik8 result is what a badly shaped escape costs
  (strided 1.13 bpb where dense gets 1.00, fixed 0.99). And sparse attention
  was **cheaper and lower-loss than dense** on two of three comparisons in
  2019, which its own authors decline to explain.
