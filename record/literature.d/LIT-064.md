# LIT-064: Instant Neural Graphics Primitives with a Multiresolution Hash Encoding

Müller et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2201.05989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05989)

## Key takeaways

- An **input encoding**, not an architecture: coordinates index a
  **multiresolution hash table of trainable feature vectors**, optimised by
  ordinary gradient descent alongside a network small enough to be fast —
  training in **seconds**, rendering in **tens of milliseconds**
- **Collisions are tolerated rather than resolved.** A hash smaller than the
  coordinate space must collide; the **multiresolution structure lets the network
  disambiguate them**, "making for a simple architecture", and gradient descent
  weights each entry toward whichever colliding coordinate carries more loss
- **Table size is the single dial** trading quality against performance, with no
  other structural change — which is what makes the encoding drop-in
- Explicitly **task-independent**, demonstrated across several neural graphics
  primitives rather than one
