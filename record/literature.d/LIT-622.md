# LIT-622: CogVideoX: Text-to-Video Diffusion Models with An Expert Transformer

Yang, Teng et al., Tsinghua University and Zhipu AI (2024) — [ARXIV-2408.06072](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06072)

## Key takeaways

- **The causal 3D VAE's compression was chosen by ablation.** At 8×8×4 with
  16 channels, flicker is 86.3 and PSNR 28.7, against 93.2 and 28.4 for SDXL's
  2D VAE (Table 1). 32 channels reach 30.5 PSNR. At 16×16×8, "convergence
  becomes extremely difficult". HunyuanVideo and Wan use the same 8×8×4,
  16-channel point. Movie Gen, Step-Video and LTX-Video compress further.
  The VAE is built on MAGVIT-v2's ([LIT-623](LIT-623.md)) and takes its temporally causal
  convolution, with all padding at the start of the time axis (§2.1). It
  replaces MAGVIT-v2's lookup-free quantization with a continuous latent
  trained on L1, LPIPS and KL losses.
- **Text and video share one sequence and differ only in their adaLN.** The
  "expert" adaLN is compared against SD3's MM-DiT ([LIT-449](LIT-449.md)) at equal
  parameters and at equal depth (Fig. 8). This design choice is what
  distinguishes CogVideoX. HunyuanVideo later moved to a FLUX-style
  dual-then-single-stream design, and Wan and Step-Video to cross-attention.
  The modulation itself is DiT's ([LIT-448](LIT-448.md)): following DiT, the diffusion
  timestep drives an adaLN, and CogVideoX's change is to give vision and
  text tokens separate adaLN parameters within one transformer stack.
- **Full 3D attention beats factorized 2D+1D attention and costs more.** It
  has lower FVD early in training (Fig. 8b), and the factorized variant is
  reported unstable. No values are given. One forward pass at 768×1360×5s
  takes 9.60s, against 4.17s for factorized attention, a ratio that falls to
  1.67× at 480×720×6s and 1.08× at 256×384×6s (Table 8). This is where the line
  gives up VDM's factorization.
- **Explicit uniform sampling.** Timestep ranges are split across
  data-parallel ranks, which lowers validation loss at every timestep at 40k
  steps, e.g. 0.116 against 0.119 at t=500 (Table 9).

## Where the hedges are

Per [DP-010](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-10):

- **The ablations arrived late.** The version-1 preprint's ablations were
  loss curves only, and version 3 dropped two of them. The architecture
  ablations (Figs. 8 and 10) are absent from version 1 and present in
  version 3. Version 2 was not checked. Their model size and step count are not stated in the text.
- **The released 2B model uses the rejected option.** CogVideoX-2B uses
  sinusoidal position encoding, not the RoPE the ablation favours (App.
  Table 5).
- **The claimed output grew between versions.** Version 1's introduction
  gives the open model as 720×480, 6s at 8 fps. Version 3 claims 768×1360,
  10s at 16 fps. The paper does not say
  whether a different checkpoint produced the new figures, and the Kling
  human-evaluation table is unchanged between them.
- **The reported model may have changed between versions.** Version 1 says
  the reported model's captions came from the frame-caption summarization
  pipeline, and version 3 removed the sentence. The 2B VBench row also
  changed between versions, while the 5B row and the Kling table did not.
- **The captioning claim is not ablated.** The abstract says dense
  recaptioning "significantly improves generation quality".
- **The scaling claim is two model sizes.**
- **Quality fine-tuning costs semantic ability.** Fine-tuning on the top 20%
  of data caused "a slight degradation in the model's semantic ability"
  (App. A). This appears only in the appendix.

## Standing in the anthology

This is the node the open line branches from. Its full 3D attention became
the default, and HunyuanVideo and Wan kept its VAE ratio. Its expert adaLN
did not survive, and the
successors split two ways: HunyuanVideo went to FLUX-style joint streams,
and Wan, Step-Video and LTX-Video went to cross-attention (LTX-Video calls it
"better than MM-DiT" without an ablation). The successors disagree on how
text should enter a video DiT, and none of them tests the alternatives against each other at scale.

The successors also use it as their baseline. Wan ([LIT-619](LIT-619.md)) puts
CogVideoX1.5-5B at 82.17 on VBench against Wan 14B's 86.22 and Wan 1.3B's
83.96 (Table 4), and includes CogVideoX's VAE in a reconstruction plot whose
values are not printed (Fig. 7). LTX-Video ([LIT-618](LIT-618.md)) runs CogVideoX-2B at
768×512 and 40 steps rather than its own 720×480, and its raters prefer
LTX-Video in 85% of text-to-video pairs (Table 2). CausVid ([LIT-631](LIT-631.md))
distils a teacher "similar to CogVideoX": a 10s video takes CogVideoX-5B
208.6 s at 0.6 fps, against CausVid's 1.3 s to the first frame and 9.4 fps
(Table 3), and on zero-shot image-to-video CausVid scores 92.0 / 65.0 /
28.9 on VBench temporal quality, frame quality and text alignment against
CogVideoX-5B's 87.0 / 64.9 / 28.9 (Table 6).

Read in full as [NOTE-346](../notes.d/NOTE-346.md).

UltraGen ([LIT-tmp8ew94](LIT-tmp8ew94.md)) uses CogVideoX only with super-resolution,
because it cannot generate HD video natively. It comes last on UltraGen's
HD-FVD at both 1080P (394.82) and 4K (574.10).

Pyramidal Flow Matching ([LIT-tmpqhnq9](LIT-tmpqhnq9.md)) compares against CogVideoX-2B and
-5B on VBench and in a 50-prompt user study, using CogVideoX's reported
scores. At the same 2B size it scores VBench total 81.72 against 80.91.
CogVideoX-2B leads on semantic score (75.83 against 69.62), and raters
prefer it on semantics, 57.9% to 42.1%.

Two training-free sparse attention papers use dense CogVideoX as the
reference output. Sparse VideoGen ([LIT-tmpms9qj](LIT-tmpms9qj.md)) sparsifies CogVideoX-v1.5
at 720p, each head computing about 30% of attention as a spatial or a
temporal window. With the first 25% of steps dense, it reaches PSNR 28.17
(I2V) and 29.99 (T2V) against the dense output at 2.23× and 2.28× end to
end. SpargeAttention ([LIT-tmpxwbvb](LIT-tmpxwbvb.md)) runs CogVideoX-2B at 0.46 sparsity:
CLIPSIM 0.1798 against 0.1819, VQA-a 78.28 against 80.38, and 53 s against
87 s on an RTX 4090, of which 68 s is reached by 8-bit quantization alone.
