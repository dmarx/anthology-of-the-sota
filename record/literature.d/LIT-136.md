# LIT-136: Qwen3-Next: Towards Ultimate Training & Inference Efficiency

Qwen Team, Alibaba (2025) — release post at qwen.ai
(`blog?id=4074cca80393150c`), 11 September 2025. No paper.

## Key takeaways

- The hybrid layout: 75% of layers Gated DeltaNet ([LIT-137](LIT-137.md)), 25% gated
  full attention ([LIT-138](LIT-138.md)), interleaved 3:1. The post reports Gated DeltaNet
  beating sliding-window attention and Mamba2 on in-context learning, and
  the 3:1 mix as the balance between that and efficiency. The attention
  layers add output gating, larger head dimensions and rotary embeddings on
  only part of the head.
- Ultra-sparse MoE: 512 experts, 10 routed plus 1 shared per token, about
  3.75% of parameters active — 3B of 80B.
- Native multi-token prediction, trained with the model and usable for
  speculative decoding.
- Stability measures for a hybrid at this sparsity: zero-centered
  layernorm with weight decay on the norm weights, attention output gating,
  and normalised router initialisation.
- Cost: trained on a 15T-token subset of Qwen3's 36T corpus for 9.3% of
  Qwen3-32B's training compute, matching or beating it; more than 10×
  Qwen3-32B's throughput at contexts above 32k.

## Standing in the anthology

The first shipped statement of the 3:1 hybrid that [SOTA-132](../practices.d/SOTA-132.md) records — a
month before Kimi Linear's controlled comparison ([LIT-133](LIT-133.md)) and built on the
Gated DeltaNet paper's own hybrid variants ([LIT-137](LIT-137.md)). Qwen3.5, 3.6 and 3.8
([LIT-135](LIT-135.md)) keep the design. A blog post, so its numbers are the team's own;
the practices in the chain are sourced to the papers, and this note is the
lineage.
