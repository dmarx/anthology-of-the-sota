# LIT-819: RotatE: Knowledge Graph Embedding by Relational Rotation in Complex Space

Sun, Deng, Nie, Tang, Peking University, Mila, Université de Montréal and
HEC Montréal (ICLR 2019) — [ARXIV-1902.10197](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10197)

## Key takeaways

- **The model** (§3.2): entities in ℂᵏ, each relation a vector of unit
  complex numbers e^{iθ}, distance ‖h ∘ r − t‖₁. A relation is symmetric iff
  every r_i = ±1, two relations are inverse iff conjugate, and composition
  is adding phases (Lemmas 1–3). TransE models inversion and composition but
  not symmetry; DistMult and ComplEx do not model composition (Table 2)
- **Self-adversarial negative sampling** (§3.3, Eq. 5): weight each sampled
  negative by softmax(α·score) under the current model, inside a
  negative-sampling loss −log σ(γ − d) − Σ p_i log σ(d_i − γ)
- **Main results** (Tables 4–5, filtered MRR): FB15k 0.797, WN18 0.949,
  FB15k-237 0.338, WN18RR 0.476. ConvE's reported numbers are 0.657, 0.943,
  0.325 and 0.43. DistMult as re-tuned by Kadlec et al. is 0.798 on FB15k.
  Three seeds give spreads of 0.001 or less (Table 14)
- **The ablation that matters** (Table 8, Table 13): TransE trained like
  RotatE reaches FB15k-237 MRR 0.332 (RotatE 0.338) and FB15k 0.735 (RotatE
  0.797). On FB15k-237, self-adversarial sampling lifts RotatE from 0.297 to
  0.338 and TransE from 0.297 to 0.332. The authors say their re-implemented
  TransE beats every previous state of the art on FB15k-237
- **Sampling compared** (Table 7, 50-d TransE): self-adversarial beats
  uniform and KBGAN on FB15k-237 and WN18RR MRR
- **Patterns are learned** (Figure 2): the phases of the symmetric relation
  similar_to sit at 0 or π, hypernym and hyponym phases sum to 0 or 2π, and
  a FB15k-237 composition shows up as added phases
- **Countries** (Table 6, AUC-PR): on S3, the longest composition, RotatE
  scores 0.95 against ConvE's 0.86, DistMult's 0.52 and ComplEx's 0.43, all
  three copied from Dettmers et al.

## Standing in the anthology

**Filed from the graph-coverage pass** (gap list from the record's own
review, 2026-10-22).

**It `extends` TransE ([LIT-834](LIT-834.md))**: a rotation is a translation of
phase, and RotatE with entity moduli fixed (pRotatE, or TorusE) is a
translation on a torus. It is `compared_against` TransE in two ways: against
TransE's previously reported numbers in the main tables, and against TransE
re-trained with RotatE's own loss and sampling in Table 8. The second is the
fair one, and it shrinks the FB15k-237 margin from 0.044 MRR to 0.006 while
leaving a 0.062 margin on FB15k, the symmetry-heavy set TransE cannot
represent. On Countries S3, re-trained TransE beats RotatE in AUC-ROC (0.96
against 0.95).

**It sources [SOTA-453](../practices.d/SOTA-453.md)**: train every score function with the same loss,
negative sampling and size before crediting one of them. The paper ran that
control itself, which is to its credit; the headline tables still compare
against numbers from other papers' training.

**What the headline does not say.** "Significantly outperforms" is against
baselines with weaker training, and the self-adversarial sampling is a
separate contribution that transfers to other models. The relation-pattern
account is better supported by the FB15k gap and the phase histograms than
by FB15k-237.
