# LIT-149: u-µP: The Unit-Scaled Maximal Update Parametrization

Blake et al., Graphcore and Cerebras (2024; ICLR 2025) — [ARXIV-2407.17465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17465)

## Key takeaways

- µP ([LIT-148](LIT-148.md)) makes activation scale independent of model size; Unit
  Scaling makes activations, weights and gradients begin training at a
  scale of one. Combined, the model is size-independent *and* well-scaled
  from the first step.
- u-µP models reach lower loss than comparable µP models and train in FP8
  without loss-scaling tricks, because nothing starts far from the
  representable range.
- The hyperparameters become close to independent, so a one-dimensional
  sweep replaces a joint search; a learning-rate sweep alone gets near
  the optimum.

## Standing in the anthology

A variation on [SOTA-143](../practices.d/SOTA-143.md) rather than a replacement for it: the transfer is
still across width; what changes is how cheaply the proxy sweep is done
and that it comes with FP8 for free. Filed for the chain µP → u-µP →
CompleteP ([LIT-150](LIT-150.md)); no practice is sourced to it alone.
