Continued down the revisit ranking and found a cluster that reaches back into
work filed a day ago and a week ago. Four papers, three read, one left
unfiled.

## What the ranking surfaced

Filtering the remaining worklist for optimization geometry gave a tight
group: `2605.11181` (Muon is Not That Special, six revisits), `2512.04299`
(When do spectral gradient updates help, five), `2103.00065` (Cohen et al.'s
Edge of Stability, six), and `2603.00541` (Spectral Condition for muP under
Width-Depth Scaling, nine — the highest in the whole remaining list).

The last one has no HTML on arXiv; the conversion failed and only the
abstract is retrievable. An abstract is not a reading, and `ADR-025` exists
so that the record can tell one from the other, so it is not filed. It stays
at the top of the worklist.

## The trunk that was asserted rather than held

`THEORY-030` was filed yesterday. Its `promote_when` says, of the edge of
stability, that it "is established and is the premise rather than the claim."

That sentence is true about the literature and was false about the record.
Nothing in it held Cohen et al. 2021. `LIT-453` — the Central Flows paper,
same first author, filed in the same contribution — is a model *of* the edge
of stability, and its antecedent was missing, so the `extends` edge could not
be written either.

This is the shape `#86`'s trunk detectors are for and the shape neither of
them catches: the gap was not a document citing something retired or a
relation left unbound. It was a document asserting, in prose, that the field
had settled something, with nothing in the record to point at. Both existing
detectors read `## Standing` sections. This one is in a `promote_when`.

`LIT-461` is now filed, `LIT-453` declares the edge, and
`THEORY-035` holds the account at `Active`.

## The demotion

`THEORY-024` — orthogonalising the update is dualising it, and muP and
Shampoo are two partial approximations of one duality map — was `Active`. Its
last paragraph reads:

> **And it is one group.** All three papers share authors, and `ADR-010`'s
> neighbouring caution applies: a line agreeing with itself is not
> replication. `Active` because the argument is checkable and the practices
> it explains are independently evidenced, not because anyone outside has
> confirmed the frame.

Shumaylov et al. are the first outside group to look. They built `Kaon`: an
optimizer that replaces the gradient's singular values with noise from a
chaotic recurrence, computes no linear minimization oracle, dualizes nothing,
and targets no norm. It matches Muon.

The document named its own vulnerability and the vulnerability arrived. That
is the best case for a record like this one and it is worth saying plainly:
the caveat was not decoration, it was a prediction about what would need
checking, and it paid.

`THEORY-024` goes to `Proposed`, not `Rejected`. The derivation is
mathematics and no experiment touches it. What the experiment reaches is the
sentence a reader takes away — that being the duality map is *why* Muon
works — and an account that cannot distinguish its object from a randomized
control is not, on that evidence, doing explanatory work. No text was
removed; a section was added.

## What the demotion cost

Fourteen citations in eleven files, all instantly unacknowledged. Five of
those files predate this contribution: `LIT-438`, `LIT-453`, `NOTE-189`,
`NOTE-203`, `THEORY-030`. Each needed a directive written from the lint's
report, and each needed a different reason, because a citation of the account
from the paper it was built from is not the same thing as a citation of it as
a contrast.

That is the third time in a week that a status change has turned a quiet
record into a list of chores. It is the mechanism working, and it is also a
measurable cost of demotion that nothing in the record currently accounts
for.

## What was not filed, and why

**No practice from `LIT-456`.** Its actionable residue is "retune the
learning rate for each optimizer rather than transferring it", and that is
already `SOTA-165`, sourced to `LIT-156`, which tuned ten optimizers
per-optimizer across four scales. Filing it again from one architecture at
NanoGPT scale would add a citation and no claim. `DP-006` says filing is not
endorsement; the converse discipline is that not every reading owes a
practice.

**`Kaon` is not a recommendation.** The authors call it a pedagogical
construction and the reading note says so twice. It is a control. A reader
who came away thinking the record suggests randomizing singular values would
have been failed by the filing.

**`SOTA-274` stops at measuring.** Davis and Drusvyatskiy give a
blockwise condition that predicts when a spectral update helps. The obvious
practice is to route updates by it — spectral where it holds, Euclidean
elsewhere — and that ablation is not in the paper. `ADR-017`'s question is
what the claim cannot survive losing, and a routing recipe would not survive
losing an experiment nobody ran. The practice is the measurement, and
`promote_when` asks for the ablation.

## Three accounts, held as three

The record now holds three explanations bearing on matrix preconditioning:
`THEORY-024` (it is the duality map), `THEORY-033` (it is step-size
realizability), `THEORY-032` (it is the rank structure of activations
and gradients). The second denies the first's explanatory force. The third
arrives from an unrelated group and does not cite the second, nor the second
the third.

The temptation is to merge the two new ones — both are "not geometry, but
something measurable" — and that would assert a unification nobody has shown.
They are two hypotheses about one phenomenon, from two groups, and the record
should say so rather than tidy it.
