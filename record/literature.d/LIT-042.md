# LIT-042: SGDR: Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts

Loshchilov and Hutter (2016) — [ARXIV-1608.03983](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03983)

## Key takeaways

- The schedule is a *cosine annealing* from the peak learning rate down to a
  small floor, and then a warm restart: the rate jumps back to near the peak
  and the anneal begins again, typically with each cycle longer than the one
  before.
- The motivation is escaping and exploring: a restart knocks the iterate out
  of the basin the previous cycle settled into, and the ensemble of
  end-of-cycle checkpoints is useful in its own right.
- The lasting contribution turned out to be the *cosine shape*, not the
  restarts. Every schedule in the record's modern half anneals on a cosine or
  a variant of one; almost none of them restart.
