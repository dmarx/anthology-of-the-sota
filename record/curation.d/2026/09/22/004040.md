**The instruction was to judge the batch on its own merits rather than against
`THEORY-059`'s open question.** That is `DP-004` applied to my own work: a
pass finds only defects its query is shaped like, and the first pass asked
"does this plot a weight spectrum?" — which is a good question and a narrow
one. Nine papers came back triaged out. Re-read without it, **seven merit
filing and two do not**, and three of the seven form a cluster that the first
query could not have surfaced because none of them plots a spectrum at all.

**This unit files the cluster.** Three routes to one problem: a weight
matrix's spectrum drifts during training and the drift breaks things.
σReparam rescales the weight by its spectral norm every step; the Weyl rule
caps how much one step may grow the largest singular value; Pion updates
through orthogonal equivalence transformations so the spectrum cannot move at
all. Renormalize, bound the rate, or freeze.

**The first pass's cost was concrete and worth naming.** `SOTA-192` and
`SOTA-131` have been in this record describing a failure — attention logits
grow, the softmax saturates, the run dies — and the inequality that makes that
failure inevitable was published in 2023 and was not here. A record that holds
two remedies and not the theorem behind them is not wrong, but it is missing
the thing a reader would most want when deciding between the remedies.

**The best find is a disagreement, not a result.** Zhai et al. prove attention
entropy is bounded below by a quantity decaying exponentially in the spectral
norm, and conclude entropy collapse is what breaks training. Qi et al. then
exhibit attention maps that are sparse but not low-rank — near-identity,
near-zero entropy — in runs that train perfectly well, and say in those words
that this is a counterexample. So the bound stands and the inference from it
does not.

**Getting the relation right took a check I nearly skipped.** I first wrote
`extends`, reasoning that a refinement of the same mechanism is not a
correction. Then I read the appendix, which names both Dong et al.'s rank
collapse and Zhai et al.'s entropy collapse as insufficient and offers state B
as a counterexample. That is reasoning being replaced, which is `corrects`.
The schema distinguishes them precisely and I had the wrong one.

**Three papers remove learning-rate warmup and the synthesis stays unfiled.**
σReparam trains a ViT without pre-LN, warmup, weight decay or an adaptive
optimizer. The Weyl rule replaces 60-epoch, 20-epoch and 2000-step warmups
with a plain cosine decay. Pion trains without normalization layers. The
reading that writes itself — warmup is a workaround for uncontrolled spectral
growth — is claimed by none of them, and a theory sourced to papers that do
not make its claim is exactly the `DP-010` failure this record keeps catching
in other people's work. It goes in prose in all three documents and in this
entry, where a later contributor can find it, and not into the theory
registry.

**One paper filed with no practice attached, which is the harder call.**
Pion's recommendable claim is that spectrum-preserving updates can stand in
for normalization layers, and the evidence is one 60M model, one run. Its
depth-stability number is 0.0892 against Muon's 0.0927 — a 4% difference in a
loss-variance statistic, single seed, presented as being the most stable
optimizer. And its 1.3B run wins the benchmark average while losing the
validation loss to Muon. All three are in the paper; the reading's job was to
say which of them can carry a recommendation, and the answer is none of them
yet.

**`SOTA-192` gains a condition and keeps everything else.** Its account of
its own failure mode says near-zero entropy is the problem. If the
counterexample holds, that describes a state which is sometimes harmless, and
the sharper question for someone watching a live run is whether the attention
map is also losing rank. The recommendation does not move, because bounding
the logits suppresses both modes. What moves is the instrument.

**Four of the remaining papers are still to assess and two are already
declined.** The declines, with reasons, go in the second unit so they are
recorded in one place rather than split.
