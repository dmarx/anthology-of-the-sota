**`SOTA-262` went `Proposed` → `Active` on Mixture-of-Transformers ([#180](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/180)).**
The condition it was filed with read: *a second group reporting per-modality
weights against a shared-weight backbone at matched compute, **or** an
ablation separating the separate weights from the joint attention*. Liang et
al. satisfy the first clause cleanly — Meta rather than Stability AI, an
autoregressive multi-modal LLM rather than a diffusion model, and the paper
states the control directly: "all model implementations, built upon the dense
model, maintain identical FLOPs for both training and testing." The condition
also named what would *not* count — "another multimodal transformer that
happens to use separate projections" — and this is not that.

So the promotion is straightforward, and the interesting part is what it does
**not** clear.

**The second clause is still open, and a second paper made it more open, not
less.** Mixture-of-Transformers ablates four variants — feed-forward untied,
plus projections, plus layer norms, plus the leave-one-out merges — and every
single one keeps self-attention global. Nothing varies the attention. What the
record now holds is two independent groups, in different objectives and
different labs, agreeing on a *bundle*: separate parameters plus joint
normalization. Nobody has taken the bundle apart. A reader who implemented the
per-modality weights with cross-attention conditioning instead of global
attention would not be contradicted by anything here.

That is a thing worth writing down rather than quietly dropping, because the
obvious move on promotion is to delete the satisfied `promote_when` and move
on — and the deleted text was the only place the record said the two ideas
arrive together. It is now a paragraph in the practice's Conditions, and the
`history:` entry says explicitly that it is a standing gap rather than a
reason to withhold the practice. Two different things, and the version note is
where the difference has to be recorded or it is lost.

**The bonus was worth more than the replication.** The condition asked for a
second measurement. What it got was a second measurement *plus* a component
ordering: untie the feed-forward first (the large step), the `Q`/`K`/`V` and
output projections second (about 33.3% further FLOPs saved on image, 10% on
text), and the layer norms not at all. `SOTA-262` v1 said "give each modality
its own weights" without saying *which* weights, because with one source there
was nothing to say. A practice that tells you to untie parameters and cannot
tell you which ones is most of a recommendation and not all of one.

Two reasons are offered for the ordering and they are **not** the same reason,
which matters for how far it travels. One is that at a 4,096-token context the
feed-forward is the larger share of the FLOPs — a fact about that context
length, which decays as context grows. The other is that the feed-forward is
where a transformer keeps its memory, so separate memory per modality is where
separation should pay — a fact about what the component does. The first is an
accounting argument and the second is a mechanism, and only the second would
survive a long-context run. `THEORY-049` carries both and is `Proposed`
for a related reason: the competition is inferred from the benefit of
relieving it. Nothing measures gradient conflict or capacity contention in the
shared parameters. "Separating helps most in the component with the most
parameters" is consistent with contention and equally consistent with nothing
more interesting than more effective parameters per modality.

**A caveat that the short version destroys.** "Untying layer norms is
negligible" is what the ablation shows, and the authors go out of their way to
say it is negligible *on top of* the other two untyings and establishes
nothing about untying layer norms alone. That distinction survives exactly as
long as somebody keeps restating it, so it is in the practice, the note and
the theory rather than in one of them.

**Two directives came off.** `LIT-449` and `ADR-052` both carried
`inactive-ok-file: SOTA-262 — Proposed`. A promotion makes an acknowledgement
stale, and a stale directive is a finding on the same report that the
acknowledgement was filed to quiet — which is the right design: the report
notices both directions.

**`LIT-449` also gained `multimodal-learning`.** It is where this line starts
in the record and the tag postdates the note by a day, which is the ordinary
shape of a vocabulary extension: `ADR-052` added the word, and the documents
that wanted it get it as they are next touched rather than in one retagging
pass.
