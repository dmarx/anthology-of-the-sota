# LIT-125: Qwen2.5-Coder Technical Report

Hui et al. (2024) — [ARXIV-2409.12186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12186)

## Key takeaways

- Continued pretraining of the general Qwen2.5 backbones on more than 5.5T
  code-heavy tokens, at 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B and 32B.
- Three data levers: cleaning, scalable synthetic generation, and a balanced
  mix of code, general text and math so that code gains do not cost general
  and mathematical ability.
- Fill-in-the-middle is part of pretraining, first at file level and then at
  repository level with dedicated special tokens; the FIM data also serves
  next-token prediction, which is the reading [LIT-119](LIT-119.md) confirms in [SOTA-128](../practices.d/SOTA-128.md).
- State of the art across more than ten benchmarks covering generation,
  completion, reasoning and repair at the time of release.

## Standing in the anthology

The FIM format and the reference small coder (0.5B) that [LIT-119](LIT-119.md)'s 90M
coder is measured against. Filed for the recipe rather than the scores.
