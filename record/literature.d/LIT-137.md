# LIT-137: Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule

Yang et al., NVIDIA and MIT (2024; ICLR 2025) — [ARXIV-2412.06464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464)

## Key takeaways

- Two mechanisms had been explored separately for linear transformers: a
  gate for adaptive memory control (Mamba2) and the delta update rule for
  targeted memory modification (DeltaNet). They are complementary — gating
  lets the state forget quickly, the delta rule lets it overwrite one
  association without disturbing the rest — and the gated delta rule
  combines them in one recurrence.
- A parallel, chunkwise training algorithm makes the rule efficient on
  modern accelerators, which the plain delta rule had not been.
- At 1.3B parameters on 100B tokens, Gated DeltaNet consistently beats
  Mamba2 and DeltaNet on language modelling, commonsense reasoning,
  in-context retrieval, length extrapolation and long-context understanding.
- Two hybrids, GatedDeltaNet-H1 and -H2, interleave the layer with
  sliding-window attention or Mamba2 layers and improve on the pure form —
  the seed of the layer-interleaved hybrids that followed.
