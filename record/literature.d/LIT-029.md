# LIT-029: On Layer Normalization in the Transformer Architecture

Xiong et al. (2020) — [ARXIV-2002.04745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04745)

## Standing in the anthology

Retired as a duplicate rather than for anything about the work. [LIT-114](LIT-114.md)
carries the reading.

The two notes reached the same paper from opposite directions, which is why
the duplication survived a year of lints. This one was filed correctly and
described wrongly: the identifier is right, and the single takeaway under it
names RMSNorm, which the paper never discusses. [LIT-114](LIT-114.md) was filed wrongly and
corrected: it arrived as a fabricated title against an unrelated arXiv id,
and the September repair re-sourced it to Xiong et al. Nothing compared the
two, because no check in the record compares identifiers across notes
(luria#165).

The cost was not the extra document. It was [SOTA-032](../practices.d/SOTA-032.md), which sourced this
note and inherited its conflation — recommending pre-norm *and* RMSNorm as
one practice, citing a paper that argues only the first. That practice is now
split, and the half this note misattributed is sourced to [LIT-023](LIT-023.md), where it
belonged all along.
