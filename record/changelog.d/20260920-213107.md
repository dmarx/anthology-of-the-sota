### Changed

- **`implementations:` is now a reference to `LIT`** on both schemes
  (`ADR-tmpoe5gf`). It had never been declared in `luria.yaml` and carried
  whatever anybody typed: **142 distinct spellings across 216 declarations**,
  including case duplicates (`t5` / `T5`), size variants (`SWAN-GPT-1B`,
  `SWAN-GPT-8B`, `SWAN-GPT (NVIDIA, 1B and 8B)`), and a hyperparameter value
  parenthesised into a name.
- **All 216 declarations migrated to codes**, across 153 files. **141 of the
  142 names already had a note** under a different spelling — `MMLU` is
  `LIT-390`, `RETRO` is `LIT-060`, `EDM` is `LIT-075`, `Progressive
  Distillation` is the title of `LIT-067`.

### Added

- `LIT-tmpgpqc2` — `diffusers`, the one name of 142 with no document. Filed
  as infrastructure under `ADR-032`, cited by repository URL under `ADR-009`,
  and filed unread, which the absent `NOTE` states.
- `ADR-tmpoe5gf` — the decision, and why there is **no** systems scheme:
  luria's `scheme:` is single-valued, so a field cannot accept `LIT` and
  `SYS` both, and pointing it at `SYS` would need 142 documents of which 141
  proxy a note that exists. `ADR-032` had already routed benchmarks, model
  reports and infrastructure to `LIT`; a second scheme would have been a
  competing home, not a new one.

### Removed

- Three `implementations:` entries that name nothing the record holds, all
  retained in prose at their citing site: `Nemotron-3 Super` and
  `Nemotron-3 Ultra` on `SOTA-180` — NVIDIA models known only because
  `LIT-196` mentions them — and `the 4-bit path in the major PEFT libraries`
  on `SOTA-230`, which is a description rather than a system.

### Documentation

- **The failure mode is now impossible rather than detectable.** A name that
  does not resolve to a note cannot be written. `Stable Diffusion 3` sat in
  `SOTA-188`'s `implementations:` for a month with no paper in the record and
  was found by a paper wandering past; that cannot recur.
- **The cost is stated rather than absorbed.** Variants collapse — `DiffuGPT`
  and `DiffuLLaMA` both become `LIT-381`. The mitigation is `ADR-017`'s
  existing shape: the field is typed, the prose is argument, and every
  `## Known implementations` section is unchanged.
- **The answer to "are there systems with no paper" is two, and they are
  adopters known only by somebody else's mention.** Small enough to carry in
  prose, which is what `ADR-043` says to do until it is not.
