# LIT-140: mHC: Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections

Xie et al., DeepSeek-AI (2025; revised January 2026) — [ARXIV-2512.24880](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24880)

## Key takeaways

<!-- inactive-ok: LIT-141 — the superseded paper, named as the predecessor in the chain -->
- Hyper-Connections ([LIT-141](LIT-141.md)) expand the residual stream to several parallel
  streams and let the network learn how they mix. The paper's diagnosis is
  that the free mixing gives up the identity-mapping property a residual
  connection has by construction, and that this is what makes HC unstable
  and hard to scale.
- The fix projects the residual-mixing matrix onto a manifold that
  restores identity mapping: the matrix that carries the streams forward is
  made doubly stochastic — non-negative, rows and columns summing to one —
  by Sinkhorn-Knopp iterations, which bounds its spectral norm, and the
  pre- and post-mixing maps are kept non-negative.
- Infrastructure work keeps the wider stream affordable: kernel fusion,
  recomputation and communication overlap bring the cost of four streams
  down to a reported 6.7% additional training time at expansion rate n = 4,
  with the quality gains of HC retained. That figure is the system-level
  result at 27B; the scaling study that shows the advantage holding spans
  3B, 9B and 27B, with a separate 3B run on 1T tokens for token scaling.

## Standing in the anthology

<!-- inactive-ok: SOTA-136, LIT-141 — a Proposed practice, named as part of the residual chain -->
The source of [SOTA-136](../practices.d/SOTA-136.md) and the successor of [LIT-141](LIT-141.md), which it retires in
this record: the reason to prefer it is the reason HC could not be scaled.
DeepSeek-V4 ([LIT-139](LIT-139.md)) is the production instance, and the largest model in
the record to replace the residual at all. It is one of three replacements
the record now carries — Kimi's Attention Residuals ([LIT-134](LIT-134.md)) and Qwen's
Gated Residual ([LIT-145](LIT-145.md)) are the others — and [LIT-145](LIT-145.md) is the one place all
three have been run in a single harness, where they come out level and are
separated by inference cost rather than quality. The constraint this paper
chose is separately argued against in [LIT-144](LIT-144.md), which shows the doubly
stochastic set bounds the mixing from above but not below.
