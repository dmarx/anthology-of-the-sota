# LIT-024: Fast Transformer Decoding: One Write-Head is All You Need

Shazeer (2019) — [ARXIV-1911.02150](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02150)

## Key takeaways

- **The diagnosis is the contribution.** Incremental Transformer inference "is
  limited by the memory bandwidth necessary to reload the large keys and
  values tensors which encode the state of the attention layers" — not by
  arithmetic. The paper derives this before proposing anything
- Training parallelises across sequence positions and **decoding cannot**,
  which is why the two have different bottlenecks and why an architecture
  tuned for one may be wrong for the other
- **Multi-query attention**: share one set of keys and values across all
  attention heads, keep the queries per-head. The KV tensors shrink by a
  factor of the head count, and the bandwidth requirement with them
- **Much faster to decode**, at "only minor quality degradation" from the
  multi-head baseline — reported qualitatively, not bounded, which is the
  claim GQA later found room in
