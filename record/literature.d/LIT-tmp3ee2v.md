# LIT-tmp3ee2v: Topics in propagation of chaos

Sznitman (1991) — [DOI:10.1007/BFb0085169](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0085169)

## What it is

The standard treatment of propagation of chaos: N interacting particles
become asymptotically independent as N grows, with the empirical measure
converging at rate 1/sqrt(N).

## Standing in the anthology

`Deferred`, which here means what the vocabulary says: in the corpus, not
read closely enough to place. It arrived in the imported batch, which
brought in the mean-field account of wide networks, where the object that
moves is the distribution of neurons.
