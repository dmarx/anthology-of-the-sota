# LIT-003: Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units

Sennrich et al. (2015) — [ARXIV-1508.07909](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07909)

## Key takeaways

- **Translation is an open-vocabulary problem and NMT models were not**,
  backing off to a dictionary for out-of-vocabulary words — handling them
  outside the model rather than in it
- The fix is to encode rare and unknown words as **sequences of subword
  units**, which makes the model itself open-vocabulary
- **Byte pair encoding, adapted from compression to segmentation**: start
  from characters, repeatedly merge the most frequent adjacent pair, stop at
  a chosen number of merges
- Frequent words survive as single units, rare ones fragment, and **the
  character level is always reachable** — so nothing is unrepresentable
- The merge table is **fit to a corpus**, which is where the permanent cost of
  an unrepresentative fitting distribution comes from
