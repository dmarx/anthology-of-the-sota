# LIT-051: A Unified Architecture for Accelerating Distributed DNN Training in Heterogeneous GPU/CPU Clusters

Jiang et al., Tsinghua University and ByteDance (2020) — OSDI '20,
[usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/jiang](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi20/presentation/jiang).
Known by its implementation's name, BytePS.

## Key takeaways

- All-reduce and parameter-server are not two competing architectures but
  two special cases of one communication framework, which the paper derives
  and proves optimal for a cluster holding both GPUs and spare CPU and
  network capacity.
- Reaching that optimum in practice means splitting the optimizer: a
  *Summation Service* that aggregates gradients — common to every optimizer,
  AVX-friendly, run on CPUs — and the model-dependent optimizer step, left
  on the GPUs.
- On jobs up to 256 GPUs, BytePS beats the best open-source all-reduce by up
  to 84% and the best parameter-server by up to 245%.
