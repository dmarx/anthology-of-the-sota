# LIT-073: Photorealistic Text-to-Image Diffusion Models with Deep Language Understanding

Saharia et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2205.11487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11487)

## Key takeaways

- **The finding is an allocation result, not an architecture one:** *"scaling
  the size of the frozen text encoder improves sample quality significantly more
  than scaling the size of image diffusion model"*, and frozen language models
  **trained only on text** are surprisingly effective text encoders for image
  generation
- **Diagnoses why high guidance weights wreck images.** At each step the `x̂₀`
  prediction must lie in the training range `[−1, 1]`; high guidance pushes it
  out; and because the model is applied **iteratively to its own output**, the
  excursion compounds — "produces unnatural images and sometimes even diverges"
- **Dynamic thresholding** is the fix: take `s` as a percentile of `|x̂₀|`, and
  if `s > 1` clip to `[−s, s]` and divide by `s`, pushing saturated pixels
  inward at *every* step rather than letting saturation accumulate
- **The benchmark determines the conclusion.** T5-XXL and CLIP text encoders
  perform similarly on MS-COCO; human raters prefer T5-XXL on DrawBench for both
  alignment and fidelity. DrawBench exists because COCO could not resolve it
- Zero-shot **COCO FID 7.27** without ever training on COCO, over GLIDE and
  DALL·E 2
