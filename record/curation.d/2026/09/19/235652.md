Continued filing from the reading feed by revisit count. The three papers at
the top that this anthology can express all turned out to answer the same
question, and the question is one the record had been arguing about for a
month without noticing what it was arguing over.

## What the record already held

Three practices on how far a fixed corpus may be repeated, all `Proposed` or
`contested`:

- [SOTA-124](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-124.md) — repeat freely while epoch size exceeds the model's
  memorization window. One figure from a technical blogpost, and a linear
  scaling its own source calls early.
- [SOTA-171](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-171.md) — about four epochs, from 400 runs.
- [SOTA-173](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-173.md) — repetition overfits severely, and the overfitting is a
  property of the *objective* rather than of repetition; augment to fix it.

The record's own note on all three has been that nobody has run the
experiment that tells them apart.

## What arrived

Three papers, and none of them is a fourth position:

- **Prabhudesai et al.** re-ran [SOTA-171](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-171.md)'s study with the objective swapped
  to masked diffusion. The autoregressive arm recovers `R* = 31.93` — the
  four-epoch bound, replicated. Diffusion gets `512.85`.
- **Kim et al.** left the objective alone and swept weight decay instead. The
  optimum is roughly thirty times the inherited 0.1, and with it the loss
  curve stops turning up at all.
- **Morris et al.** measured what a transformer can hold: ~3.6 bits per
  parameter, linear in parameter count, and double descent begins exactly
  where the data's information content crosses it.

## The finding

**All three agree with [SOTA-173](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-173.md)'s diagnosis and none adopts its remedy.**
The overfitting really does belong to the recipe rather than to repetition.
One paper changes the objective; the other changes one hyperparameter; the
third supplies the units in which the whole thing could be settled. A
reframing outliving the recipe it arrived with is a good outcome for a
`Proposed` practice and worth recording as one.

The sharper finding is uncomfortable. **The three old practices between them
report one setting of weight decay**, and on Kim et al.'s evidence the answer
moves with it. None of those measurements is wrong; they may all be
measurements of the under-regularized case. That is not something the record
could have found by internal query — no document was complaining, because
every document agreed about a value nobody had thought to name.

## Two places I had to be careful

**The negative result is not a refutation and I nearly wrote it as one.**
Prabhudesai et al. apply random token masking and attention dropout to their
autoregressive arm and report the gap does not close. That is two of
[SOTA-173](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-173.md)'s three families, and [SOTA-173](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-173.md) claims *delay*, not removal. It
is the first evidence in the record on that practice from outside its own
source and it points the other way — which is exactly what the body now says,
and no more. [ADR-010](../../../../decisions.d/ADR-010.md)'s rule that contrast is not support has an obverse: a
contrast that does not reach the claim is not a refutation either.

**The capacity measurement half-answers [SOTA-124](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-124.md)'s `promote_when:`, and half
is not all.** That field asks for a measurement of the memorization window at
a second model scale so the linear-in-parameters assumption can be checked.
Morris et al. check the linear scaling across three orders of magnitude — but
Falcon's window is a *token* count and this is a *bit* count, and nobody has
done the conversion. It would have been easy to call that satisfied and
promote. The status does not move, and the history entry says which half was
checked.

## On `explains:` being empty

[THEORY-tmpqd9wu](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-tmpqd9wu.md) is the most useful document in the batch and it underwrites
nothing. It supplies the common unit the repetition dispute lacks — the
dataset's information content against the model's capacity, both measurable —
and predicts that multi-epoch overfitting should begin at the crossing. That
experiment has not been run by anyone. Filling `explains:` with the three
practices it *bears on* would have been the easy move and would have been a
lie about what the paper shows.

## On the ranking

The feed ordered the list; it did not decide what belonged on it. Several
papers above these by revisit count — political exposure bias on Twitter,
channels with memory, Ricci flow for community detection — are outside what
the thirteen topics can express, and skipping them is a judgement rather than
an oversight. Worth saying plainly, because a revisit count is a record of
attention and attention is not an argument for filing.
