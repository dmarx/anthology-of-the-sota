**Unblocked by a copy-paste ([#220](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/220)).** The paper is not on arXiv and is not
readable from this environment: OpenReview answers 403 to `/pdf` and to
`api.openreview.net`, and the JSTAT page returns HTTP 200 with 386 characters
of text — a bot wall, not a paywall message. Semantic Scholar reports the
open-access status as `CLOSED`. The record's owner pasted the ICLR 2018
version. Worth saying plainly: the issue had listed the publisher route as
"untried", and trying it was worth the two minutes even though it failed.

**First framing abandoned: the issue's.** `#220` proposed this as a third
instance of "the finding was in the measurement", alongside emergence and
grokking under `SOTA-200`, and suggested three arrivals would be a
generalization worth having. Reading the rebuttal killed that. In the
emergence case the underlying quantity is real and a thresholding metric makes
a smooth curve look sharp. Here `H(h|X) = -∞` for a deterministic map, so
`I(h;X)` is **infinite** and the reported number is not a distorted
measurement of something — it is manufactured in full by a choice the analyst
made and the network never had. Same moral, different mechanism, so the count
stays a count. `DP-009`.

**Second framing abandoned: mine.** An hour before reading Saxe, this session
told the owner the dispute looked symmetric and unresolved — each side
blaming the other's estimator. That is right about one claim and wrong about
the other two. The IB theory makes three claims and they have different fates:
the two-phase claim is estimator-dependent and genuinely contested; the
compression-causes-generalization claim is refuted by the rebuttal's four-cell
dissociation **and** by `LIT-507`, which set out to defend the theory and
reports no significant correlation in hidden layers; and the SGD-diffusion
claim is refuted by full-batch gradient descent compressing just as much, and
is not contested by anyone since. Saying "the dispute is unresolved" would
have been a tidy sentence that was false about two thirds of its subject.

**The third paper turned out to argue the opposite of what was feared.**
`#220` worried that Chelombiev et al. might "materially soften" the rebuttal,
and that filing two papers would misrepresent the argument. The worry was
right and the direction was wrong. Its technical criticism lands — the
rebuttal bins ReLU over a single global range dominated by the last layer —
but its own Figure 6, averaged over the 50 initializations the original study
used, shows a ReLU network with **no distinct phase at all**. The abstract
says saturation is not required for compression; the protocol shows one
initialization out of fifty. `DP-010`, and this time in a paper defending the
theory rather than proposing it.

**What the dispute jointly establishes is something neither paper claims.**
The rebuttal changes the binning and compression disappears. The
counter-rebuttal changes the binning and compression appears. Both are correct
and neither is the truth, because for a deterministic map there is no truth of
the matter. That symmetry is the practice, and it was invisible from either
paper alone — which is the constructive form of `DP-004`, and the third time
today that holding two documents produced something neither contained.

**Filed `Active` on a dispute, deliberately.** The object-level question is
open and `THEORY-058` carries it as `Proposed`. The instrument-level
question is not: the infinity is analytic and the sensitivity is demonstrated
twice. A practice can be settled while the argument that produced it is not.

**Two consequences worth remembering past this unit.** The data processing
inequality does not apply to these estimates — the noise is added per layer
for analysis and does not propagate forward. And the estimate is not invariant
to invertible reparameterization: two linear networks computing the identical
function, one with a layer scaled by `c`, report different mutual
information. Whatever mutual information offers as a common currency for
comparing architectures, it does not survive the noise assumption that makes
it finite.
