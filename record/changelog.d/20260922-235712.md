### Added

- **CycleGAN** (`#163`), [LIT-tmpm6lfm](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpm6lfm.md), read as [NOTE-tmp36vxd](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp36vxd.md).
  **[SOTA-tmpw7xk8](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmpw7xk8.md), `Proposed`**: cycle consistency for unpaired appearance
  translation. It is scoped away from geometric change, where the authors
  report failure. The paper's own labels → photo ablation has a
  one-directional cycle beating the bidirectional one, and the practice says
  so.
- **Projected GAN** (`#163`), [LIT-tmpbzwal](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbzwal.md), read as [NOTE-tmp4h72t](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp4h72t.md).
  **[SOTA-tmptfakt](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmptfakt.md), `Proposed`, `contested`**: discriminate on frozen
  multi-scale pretrained features with fixed random mixing. It reaches
  StyleGAN2's best Church FID after 1.1M images instead of 88M. The speed-up
  is measured in FID, and the contest is over whether it is quality.
- **The Role of ImageNet Classes in FID**, [LIT-tmpcemc9](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpcemc9.md), read as
  [NOTE-tmp5m0ns](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp5m0ns.md). It is the `contested_by` of [SOTA-tmptfakt](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmptfakt.md). With a fixed
  generator, resampling to match ImageNet-class statistics cuts FFHQ FID
  5.30 → 1.78 while CLIP-space FD moves 2.76 → 2.64. At equal FID, Projected
  FastGAN loses to StyleGAN2 in CLIP space and with human raters.
  **[SOTA-tmprzpgu](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmprzpgu.md), `Active`**: when ImageNet-pretrained networks take part
  in training, confirm FID gains in a non-ImageNet feature space.
