# LIT-132: Kimi K2: Open Agentic Intelligence

Kimi Team, Moonshot AI (2025) — [ARXIV-2507.20534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20534)

## Key takeaways

- Vanilla Muon at this scale drives the maximum attention logit past 1000
  within a short run, and logits of that size bring loss spikes and
  occasional divergence. The token efficiency of Muon ([LIT-122](LIT-122.md)) was worth
  keeping; the instability was not.
- QK-Clip: after each optimizer step, if a head's maximum attention logit
  exceeds a threshold, rescale that head's query and key weights so it does
  not. It acts on the weights rather than on the activations, so inference
  is unchanged, and it only fires where the logits demand it.
- MuonClip — Muon with weight decay, RMS-matched updates and QK-Clip —
  carried a 1T/32B mixture-of-experts model through 15.5T tokens with no
  loss spike.
- Post-training is multi-stage: a large-scale pipeline synthesising
  tool-use and agentic trajectories, then a joint reinforcement-learning
  stage over real and synthetic environments. State of the art among open
  non-thinking models at release, strongest on agentic tasks.

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-131](../practices.d/SOTA-131.md) and the middle link of the Muon chain: [LIT-122](LIT-122.md) made
Muon transfer AdamW's hyperparameters; this made it survive a trillion
parameters; [LIT-131](LIT-131.md) carries the same optimizer to 2.8T. The agentic
post-training half is not drawn into a practice.
