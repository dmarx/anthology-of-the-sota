**Eight candidates, three practices.** Every earlier cluster in this sweep
converted at better than half; this one converted at three-eighths, and the
reason is worth recording because it is the first evidence that the sweep is
approaching its end rather than that it stalled.

Five of the eight are **line members**, and their notes had already said so
in detail. Mamba's selectivity is inherited by everything the record
recommends. The state-space duality is a *premise* of the hybrid practices,
not a rival to them. Gated Linear Attention is parallel invention that the
record's actual line does not run through. Griffin is the origin of the
interleave and takes the other side of a fork the practice already argues.
The gated-convolution paper is the origin of a unit `SOTA-034` recommends a
variant of.

None of those is a recommendation to file. All of them are things a reader
meeting the practice should be told, and in three of five cases the practice
already told them: `SOTA-034` had a "Where the gate came from" section,
`SOTA-132`'s Variations already carried the intra-layer layout and the
parallel-head one.

## What that says about the previous clusters

The four earlier clusters each found a defect. This one mostly found work
already done — and the difference is not that this material is better tended.
It is that **the architecture half of the record has been worked on recently
and repeatedly**, in the reference pass and the two hybrid passes, while the
data and optimizer halves had not been touched since the migration.

So the sweep's yield is a measure of when a section was last read, not of how
important it is. Worth knowing before reading a low-yield cluster as evidence
that a subject is in good shape: it is evidence that somebody was here
recently.

## The one real gap, and it was two steps back

`SOTA-132`'s `Sequence` began at the Gated DeltaNet hybrids of 2024. The
interleave starts at Griffin, and the delta-rule paper says so in as many
words — its sliding-window hybrid "follows Griffin and Samba". The practice
had been describing a line from its middle.

The fork at that first step is the part that matters and was nowhere: Griffin
puts **local** attention in the minority layers, the practice puts **global**
attention there, and only global preserves exact retrieval at arbitrary
distance — which is the entire reason the minority layers exist. A reader
taking "interleave at 3:1" from the earlier work would get the ratio and lose
the reason.

That is a fourth variety of the trunk problem: not a missing trunk, but a
line **entered halfway**, where the steps before the entry point carry the
argument for why the practice is shaped as it is.

## A small one from the lint

A directive in `SOTA-171` vouched for a citation that a file-scope directive
at the top of the same file already covered, and the lint reported it as no
longer applying. That is the guard catching a **redundant** acknowledgement
rather than a stale one — a third thing it can find, after the missing vouch
and the vouch for a document that has since gone `Active`. Cheap to fix and
worth knowing the report means three different things.
