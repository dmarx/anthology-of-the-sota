# LIT-139: DeepSeek-V4: Towards Highly Efficient Million-Token Context Intelligence

DeepSeek-AI (2026) — [ARXIV-2606.19348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19348). Weights released 24 April 2026; the
report followed on arXiv.

## Key takeaways

- Two mixture-of-experts models: DeepSeek-V4-Pro, 1.6T total and 49B
  activated, and DeepSeek-V4-Flash, 284B total and 13B activated, both with
  a one-million-token context. Pretrained on 33T and 32T tokens.
- The attention is a hybrid of two mechanisms. *Compressed Sparse Attention*
  compresses the KV cache along the sequence and then runs DeepSeek Sparse
  Attention ([LIT-142](LIT-142.md)) — a lightning indexer selecting top-k entries — over
  the compressed entries. *Heavily Compressed Attention* compresses further
  still and keeps attention dense. Two token-level latent KV streams come
  from two learned projections so that overlapping windows can be combined
  and block-boundary artefacts reduced. At 1M tokens, V4-Pro needs 27% of
  DeepSeek-V3.2's single-token inference FLOPs and 10% of its KV cache.
- Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections ([LIT-140](LIT-140.md)) replace the residual
  connection.
- Muon is the pretraining optimizer, credited with faster convergence and
  better stability. Secondary coverage reports that V4 does without
  QK-Clip ([SOTA-131](../practices.d/SOTA-131.md)) because the RMSNorm it already applies to queries and
  compressed KV entries bounds the logits; the report's own wording has not
  been read.
- Context is extended in stages — 4K, then 16K, 64K and 1M — and sparse
  attention is warmed up under dense attention for the first 1T tokens and
  introduced at the 64K stage, staying on for the rest of training.
- Multi-token prediction is kept from V3; a single fused MoE kernel
  overlaps computation and communication; FP4 quantisation-aware training
  is reported for the expert weights and the indexer's query-key path.

## Standing in the anthology

The third laboratory in the record to pretrain with Muon, after Moonshot
([LIT-122](LIT-122.md), [LIT-132](LIT-132.md), [LIT-131](LIT-131.md)) and TII ([LIT-119](LIT-119.md)), and the second model to
replace the plain residual, after Kimi K3's Attention Residuals ([LIT-134](LIT-134.md))
— by a different construction ([LIT-140](LIT-140.md)). Its sparse attention is the third
step of a line the record now carries in full: NSA ([LIT-143](LIT-143.md)) → DSA in
V3.2 ([LIT-142](LIT-142.md)) → CSA/HCA here. Source of [SOTA-139](../practices.d/SOTA-139.md); the other practices it
instantiates are sourced to the papers that introduced them.
