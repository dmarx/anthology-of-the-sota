# LIT-751: "Principal Components" Enable A New Language of Images

Wen, Zhao, Elezi, Deng and Qi (2025) — [ARXIV-2503.08685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08685) (v2 read).
Project page: visual-gen.github.io/semanticist.

## Key takeaways

- **Method.** A ViT-B/16 encoder takes patch tokens plus `K` learnable
  concept tokens under a causal mask, and outputs `K` continuous tokens.
  Their total size is fixed at 4096 floats (d16×256 by default). They
  condition a DiT decoder by cross-attention, and the decoder works in a
  frozen KL-16 VAE latent. **Nested CFG**: sample `k′ ~ U{1..K}` and replace
  tokens `k′..K` with learned null tokens, after a 50-epoch warm-up without
  it. The idea is credited to nested dropout and Matryoshka representations.
  REPA with DINOv2-B on the decoder's 8th layer is used as a regularizer.
- **The "provable" structure is a stationarity argument.** Appendix A writes
  the conditional score as the unconditional score plus per-token
  increments. At the optimum, it argues, those increments are mutually
  orthogonal and explain decreasing shares of the loss. It assumes
  convergence to that optimum. What is *measured* is Fig. 4: each token's
  share of the drop in diffusion loss falls with position.
- **Reconstruction, ImageNet 256, Table 1:** rFID **0.78** (DiT-L decoder)
  and 0.72 (DiT-XL) with 256 tokens of 16 dimensions, continuous. The next
  best is ImageFolder, 0.80 at 286×32 quantized. LlamaGen's VQ is 2.19 at
  256×8, VQGAN 7.94 at 256×16, VAR 0.90 at 680×32. PSNR 21.61 against
  LlamaGen's 20.79, but SSIM **0.626 against 0.675**, lower.
- **Generation:** ε-LlamaGen-L (LlamaGen with MAR's diffusion head for
  continuous tokens), trained and sampled on only the first **32** tokens,
  scores gFID **2.57**, IS 260.9. Rows quoted beside it include LlamaGen-L
  3.80 on 256 tokens, VAR-d16 3.30, MAR-L 1.78. "Comparable", not best. The
  appendix says later tokens gave "diminishing returns or are even harmful"
  for the generator.
- **Semantic-spectrum coupling.** In TiTok, semantic content appears only
  after half to three-quarters of the tokens, and the reconstruction's power
  spectrum only reaches the original's as tokens are added. A PCA of
  LlamaGen's VQ latent shows the same coupling. Semanticist matches the
  spectrum with a quarter of its tokens. The explanation given is that a
  diffusion decoder generates low frequencies first, so the tokens are left
  to carry semantics. **No arm swaps in a deterministic decoder**, so this is
  a contrast with other tokenizers, not an ablation.
- **What is ablated** (Fig. 6, appendix D.4): decoder scale, token count,
  decoder CFG scale, token dimension at fixed 4096 floats (lower dimension
  reconstructs better), and REPA (the full-token rFID is about the same, but
  early tokens carry more with it). Nested CFG itself has no off arm.
- Linear probing on the tokens peaks at **63.5%** top-1 using few tokens and
  *falls* as more are added. A human perception test scores first-two-token
  reconstructions near parity with captioned-then-generated distractors.

## Standing in the anthology

It belongs to the tokenizer trunk built on [LIT-497](LIT-497.md), [LIT-496](LIT-496.md) and [LIT-499](LIT-499.md), and
it bears on [SOTA-305](../practices.d/SOTA-305.md) in a specific way. The paper does state rate: it compares
at "similar-sized latent space (i.e., token count × token dimension)". But its
tokens are continuous and the rows it beats are quantized, so equal float
count is not equal rate. LlamaGen's 256×8 is 256 indices into a 16384-entry
codebook. The 0.78-versus-0.80 headline would need [SOTA-305](../practices.d/SOTA-305.md)'s question asked
in bits before it means what it seems to.

The comparisons are quoted from the original papers, except the spectrum
analysis. [LIT-497](LIT-497.md)'s LlamaGen is a Table 1 row: its VQ tokenizer at 2.19 rFID
and 256×8, and LlamaGen-L's 3.80 gFID on 256 tokens. It is also the tokenizer
whose PCA-decomposed latent Fig. 2 uses to show semantic-spectrum coupling,
and the generator Semanticist's own ε-LlamaGen is built from. [LIT-496](LIT-496.md)'s VQGAN
is the 7.94 rFID / 5.20 gFID row. Neither is retrained.

Nested CFG is the Matryoshka idea of [LIT-547](LIT-547.md) (practice [SOTA-327](../practices.d/SOTA-327.md)) moved from
embedding dimensions to token positions, with prefix decoding in place of
prefix retrieval. The paper credits it as inspiration and does not compare
against it. The decoder is a DiT ([LIT-448](LIT-448.md)).
