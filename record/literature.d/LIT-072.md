# LIT-072: Simple Open-Vocabulary Object Detection with Vision Transformers

Minderer et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2205.06230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06230)

## Key takeaways

- Open vocabulary detection
- Vision transformer architecture
- Zero-shot capabilities
- Simplified detection pipeline

## Standing in the anthology

**Carries no practice, and this is a reading rather than an assumption.**

An open-vocabulary detection architecture. The recipe is transfer a contrastively pretrained image-text backbone, then fine-tune with a detection head — a composition of two things the record already holds separately, at the altitude of a task solution rather than a training instruction.
