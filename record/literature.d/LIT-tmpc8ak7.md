# LIT-tmpc8ak7: Emergent Behavior in Flocks

Cucker and Smale (2007) — [DOI:10.1109/TAC.2007.895842](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2007.895842)

## What it is

Flocking emerges unconditionally when the interaction between agents decays
slowly enough with distance, and only conditionally when it decays faster.
