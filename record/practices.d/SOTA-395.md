# SOTA-395: Post-train an autoregressive video generator on its own rollouts, and score the whole rollout

## Source

Huang et al. (2025), [LIT-629](../literature.d/LIT-629.md) — Self Forcing, Table 2.

## The claim

A causal generator trained with teacher forcing sees clean context in
training and its own imperfect output at inference. The gap compounds with
every autoregressive step. **Close it in training.** Run the rollout during
training, build the KV cache from the model's own outputs, and apply a loss
to the generated clip as a whole.

Self Forcing compares this against the alternatives on Wan2.1-1.3B, in two
matched pairs (Table 2, VBench total, chunk-wise / frame-wise):

| Training | Chunk-wise | Frame-wise |
|---|---|---|
| Self Forcing + DMD | 84.31 | 84.26 |
| Diffusion forcing + DMD | 82.76 | 80.56 |
| Teacher forcing + DMD | 82.32 | 78.12 |
| Many-step teacher forcing | 83.58 | 80.34 |
| Many-step diffusion forcing | 82.95 | 77.24 |

The pattern is the claim. Every baseline loses quality moving from
chunk-wise to frame-wise generation, which means more autoregressive steps.
Self Forcing does not. The advantage holds with three objectives: DMD
84.31, SiD 84.07, GAN 83.88.

It is affordable. Backprop is truncated to one randomly chosen denoising step
and the KV cache is detached. Per-iteration cost is comparable to teacher
forcing, and DMD converges in about 1.5 hours on 64 H100s (Fig. 6, App. A).

## Conditions

- **Measured on short clips.** Every number is a VBench score on clips about
  5 seconds long. "Quality degradation remains observable when generating
  videos substantially longer than those seen during training" (§5). The
  practice fixes the gap inside the training window, and does not claim to
  remove it beyond.
- **The mechanism is inferred.** Exposure bias is argued from the
  chunk-to-frame degradation pattern. No quality-over-time curve is shown.
- **The table is two setups.** The many-step rows are 50×2-step models
  fine-tuned on 70K videos generated by Wan2.1-14B. The distilled rows are
  4-step students. Compare within a pair.
- **The distilled teacher is larger than the base.** The DMD rows use
  Wan2.1-14B as the score teacher. The SiD variant, with the 1.3B model as
  teacher, scores 84.07, still above every baseline.
- **Relation to [SOTA-333](SOTA-333.md).** That practice recommends per-token noise in
  training and noised history at rollout. Self Forcing argues against the
  rollout half without testing it, and [SOTA-333](SOTA-333.md) records it as `contested_by`.
  The two are alternative answers to the same train-test gap. Nothing has
  compared them head to head.
- **Beyond video, the claim is asserted.** The paper says the method "can be
  applied to other sequence domains, especially where the data is
  continuous" (§5).
