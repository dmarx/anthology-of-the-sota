## Contribution

A post-training procedure for autoregressive video diffusion models. At
training time the model generates each frame or chunk conditioned on its
*own* previous outputs, using a KV cache. A video-level
distribution-matching loss (DMD, SiD or GAN) is applied to the finished
rollout. Two devices keep this affordable: backpropagating through one
randomly chosen denoising step per frame, and detaching the KV cache. The
paper adds a rolling KV cache for extrapolation, and a training-time mask
that makes the cache work.

## Key insight

**Train on the distribution you will sample from.** Teacher forcing
conditions on clean ground truth, and diffusion forcing on noised ground
truth. Neither is what the model sees at inference, which is its own
output. Once the whole clip comes from the model, a distribution-level
loss can score it holistically, the way a GAN generator is trained, rather
than frame by frame (§3.3).

## Assumptions

- **A pretrained, ODE-initialized few-step causal model** as the starting
  point. The initialization follows CausVid's protocol with 16k ODE pairs
  from Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B (§4).
- **Four denoising steps** at [1000, 750, 500, 250] on Wan's flow-matching
  schedule with shift k = 5 (App. A).
- **A teacher score for DMD.** The DMD real-score network is
  Wan2.1-T2V-**14B**. SiD uses the 1.3B model, and the GAN trains on 70k
  videos generated by the 14B model (§4, Table 3).
- **Training length is about 5 s** at 832×480 and 16 fps. The method is
  claimed to work "within the training context length" (§5).

## Key results

- **Ablation** (Table 2), VBench total, chunk-wise / frame-wise:

  | training | chunk-wise | frame-wise |
  |---|---|---|
  | many-step (50×2) diffusion forcing | 82.95 | 77.24 |
  | many-step (50×2) teacher forcing | 83.58 | 80.34 |
  | 4-step DF + DMD ("replicates CausVid") | 82.76 | 80.56 |
  | 4-step TF + DMD | 82.32 | 78.12 |
  | Self Forcing, DMD | 84.31 | 84.26 |
  | Self Forcing, SiD | 84.07 | 83.54 |
  | Self Forcing, GAN | 83.88 | 83.27 |

- **Speed and quality** (Table 1), one H100: chunk-wise 17.0 fps, 0.69 s
  latency, VBench 84.31, quality 85.07, semantic 81.28. Frame-wise 8.9 fps,
  0.45 s, 84.26. Wan2.1-1.3B 0.78 fps, 103 s, 84.26, quality 85.30,
  semantic 80.09. The official CausVid on Wan-1.3B scores 81.20 at 17.0 fps.
- **Rolling KV cache:** recomputing the cache gives 4.6 fps on 10 s videos.
  A rolling cache trained with the first chunk masked gives 16.1 fps.
  Without that training it gives "severe" artifacts, shown qualitatively
  (§4, App. B).
- **Training cost:** DMD converges in about 1.5 h on 64 H100s, and SiD and
  GAN in 2–3 h (App. A). Per-iteration time is "comparable" to TF and DF
  (Fig. 6, figure only).
- **User study** (Fig. 4, App. E): 1,003 MovieGenBench prompts, one rater
  per prompt. Preference for Self Forcing ranges from 54.2% (MAGI-1) to
  66.1% (CausVid), with Wan2.1 at 62.7%.

## Method

For each training sample, draw a step s from {1, …, T} (Alg. 1). Generate N
frames or chunks in sequence. Each is denoised from Gaussian noise through
steps T…s, with gradients enabled only at step s. The clean prediction at
step s is kept as that frame's output. Its keys and values are computed at
t = 0 with gradients off and appended to the cache. The finished clip is
scored with DMD (reverse KL through real and fake scores), SiD (α = 1) or
a relativistic GAN with approximate R1/R2 penalties (App. A). Chunk-wise
generation uses 3 latent frames per chunk. For the rolling cache, training
stops the last chunk from attending to the first chunk. This matters
because Wan's causal VAE encodes the first latent frame without temporal
compression, so that frame's statistics differ from the rest (§3.4).

## Concepts

- **Exposure bias:** a model trained on ground-truth context and run on its
  own outputs (§1). For the term it cites Schmidt and Ning et al. [71, 60].
  For the RNN-era remedies it cites Professor Forcing, MIXER and SeqGAN
  [40, 65, 103]. Scheduled sampling is not cited.
- **Teacher forcing (TF) against diffusion forcing (DF), as defined here:**
  TF conditions on clean ground-truth context. DF samples an independent
  noise level per frame, so context is noisy ground truth. "This ensures
  the autoregressive inference scenario, where context frames are clean and
  the current frame is noisy, is covered by the training distribution"
  (§1).
- **Real time:** throughput above the playback rate *and* latency below a
  perceptual threshold (§4).
- **Parallel pre-training, sequential post-training:** the paradigm the
  paper advocates (§5). It is argued, not tested.

## Connections

It builds directly on CausVid ([LIT-631](../literature.d/LIT-631.md)): same authors in part, and the
same ODE initialization, few-step student and DMD. It changes where the
student's training inputs come from. It descends from RNN-era sequence
training (Professor Forcing, MIXER and SeqGAN are cited) and from GAN training, where
generator and sampler already coincide. It is set against Diffusion
Forcing ([LIT-554](../literature.d/LIT-554.md)) and rolling diffusion. The base model is Wan2.1
([LIT-619](../literature.d/LIT-619.md)).

## Recommendations

- **R1.** Post-train a few-step autoregressive video model on its own
  rollouts with a sequence-level distribution loss, rather than
  distribution-matching from TF or DF inputs. *Topic:* training-optimization.
  *Status:* experimental. *Strength:* moderate (C1, C2). *Conditions:* one
  base model (Wan2.1-1.3B), about 5 s clips, VBench only, gains within the
  training length.
- **R2.** If you use a rolling KV cache, train with the attention sink (the
  first chunk) masked so the model has seen that condition. *Topic:*
  inference-optimization. *Status:* experimental. *Strength:* weak (C8).
  *Conditions:* the artifact is tied to a VAE whose first latent frame is
  special.
- **R3.** Report the teacher behind a distilled student when comparing it
  with a slower model. *Topic:* analysis-and-evaluation. *Status:*
  standard. *Strength:* moderate. *Conditions:* C4 shows how a 14B teacher
  enters a 1.3B comparison.

## Limitations

- **VBench on about 5 s clips is the only quantitative quality measure.**
  There is no quality-against-time curve and no FVD, although the thesis is
  about accumulation over time.
- **One run per cell, no variance.** Several gaps (84.31 against 84.26,
  84.07 against 83.88) are small enough that the ranking is not
  established.
- **The teacher confound in Table 1.** The DMD model distills from 14B and
  is compared with 1.3B models.
- **Extrapolation still degrades** "substantially longer than those seen
  during training" (§5). Gradient truncation may limit long-range learning
  (§5).
- **The user study** has one rater per prompt, and its protocol does not
  report ties.

## Open questions

- Does self-rollout beat diffusion forcing *with* noised-history rollout,
  the configuration Diffusion Forcing recommends?
- Does the advantage grow with rollout length, as exposure bias predicts?
  The chunk-to-frame comparison is the only proxy.
- How much of Table 1's parity with Wan2.1 survives with a 1.3B DMD
  teacher?
