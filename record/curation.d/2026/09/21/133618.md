`SOTA-200` — check whether an emergent capability is a metric artefact — has
been `Active` since 2026-09-10. Its sources were `LIT-077` (BIG-bench) and
`LIT-085` (grokking). Neither is the paper that made the argument, and the
paper the argument is *against* was not in the record at all.

So the practice named a check, and a reader following its citations could not
reach either the claim being checked or the case for checking it. The
practice's own Conditions section said as much without noticing: "`LIT-077`
... puts no number on what share of observed emergence is artefactual",
"Neither source licenses 'emergence is not real'". Both true, and both
symptoms of the same absence.

## How the gap survived a coverage pass

This is `DP-004` again — a pass finds only defects its query is shaped like.
Every audit that asked "which practices have no source" found `SOTA-200`
sourced twice over. None asked "does the source say the thing the practice
says", which is a different query and a more expensive one, because answering
it means reading the sources.

The cheap signal that would have caught it: `SOTA-200` cites `LIT-077` as
`introduced_by`, and `LIT-077` is a benchmark paper. A benchmark introducing
a recommendation about how to read benchmarks is possible and is worth a
second look.

## The primary source disagrees with its own reputation

The popular version is that Wei et al. claimed emergence and Schaeffer et al.
refuted it with metrics. Reading `LIT-tmpc4fk2` §5.1 makes that version
untenable. Wei et al. raise the metric explanation themselves, quote the
exact-string-match problem, run a cross-entropy analysis over six emergent
BIG-Bench tasks, and report that cross-entropy improves smoothly where the
downstream metrics sit at chance. Then they decline it, on two grounds:

1. The quality of *intermediate reasoning steps* also jumps, which final
   answer scoring does not explain.
2. Emergence persists on "many classification tasks", which are not
   long-string partial-credit cases.

Reason 2 is wrong, and specifically wrong in a way that took three years and
a different paper to show: the classification tasks are scored by Multiple
Choice Grade, which is a *step function*. The objection assumed the metric
problem was about compounding over a long target, and a step function is a
second, independent way for a scoring rule to manufacture sharpness.
Schaeffer et al.'s >92%-under-two-metrics figure is precisely the reply, and
the two metrics are one of each kind.

Reason 1 is still standing. Nothing in `LIT-tmpgnhq2` touches it.

That is the shape worth recording: not a claim refuted, but one of two stated
defences knocked out, with the other untouched and nobody working on it.

## What actually changed in the practice

The citation was the smaller half. The larger half is that `SOTA-200` was
giving advice that was true of its sources and false in general.

It said: finding the continuous measure underneath is not cheap. That
generalises from `LIT-085`, where the progress measures are projections onto
five frequencies the network had to be reverse-engineered to reveal. In that
case it is expensive and the practice was right about it.

But `LIT-085` is the hard case, not the normal one. The normal case is that a
continuous metric over the same outputs already exists — Token Edit Distance
against a string target, Brier Score against a multiple-choice distribution,
which BIG-bench computes on the same tasks. Change the scoring function,
change nothing else, and look. The expensive route is only needed when no
continuous function of the outputs captures the thing.

A practice that tells you a check is expensive discourages the check. This one
did that for a year of record-time on the strength of one atypical example.

## A note on what was not upgraded

`SOTA-200` stays `emerging`. The case for `converged` is that the metric
argument is a NeurIPS best paper and is now the standard citation — which is
an award and a citation count, and `DP-005` is the standing reminder that
adoption is not evidence. Consensus is a judgement about a community; nobody
has measured this one, and `unassessed`-adjacent honesty is cheaper than a
guess dressed as a reading.
