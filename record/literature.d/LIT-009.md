# LIT-009: Large Batch Training of Convolutional Networks

You et al. (2017) — [ARXIV-1708.03888](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.03888)

## Key takeaways

- **The negative result is the opening move**: "the current recipe for large
  batch training (linear learning rate scaling with warm-up) **is not general
  enough and training may diverge**". Warm-up is prior art here — Goyal et
  al. ([LIT-007](LIT-007.md)) — cited, used as baseline, and argued past
- **Layer-wise Adaptive Rate Scaling**: a per-layer learning rate scaled by
  the **trust ratio** `‖w‖/‖∇w‖`, on the observation that the ratio differs
  by orders of magnitude between layers
- The reframing: warm-up manages the problem in *time*, going slowly until
  things settle, where the variation actually lives across *layers*
- **AlexNet to batch 8K** and **ResNet-50 to batch 32K**, both without loss
  in accuracy
- CNNs on ImageNet; no Transformer evidence
