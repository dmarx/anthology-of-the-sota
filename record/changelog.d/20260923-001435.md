### Added

- **Logit lens and tuned lens** (`#163`, "LogitLens"). The logit lens is
  filed as [LIT-tmpqsh8n](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpqsh8n.md), the original LessWrong post (`url:`). The tuned
  lens is [LIT-tmpebkg3](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpebkg3.md), read as [NOTE-tmpsoytn](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpsoytn.md), which `extends` it.
  **[SOTA-tmpeyp35](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmpeyp35.md), `Proposed`**: read intermediate predictions through a
  tuned lens, not the raw logit lens, which fails on BLOOM, OPT and GPT-Neo
  and is biased where it works.
- **Sparse autoencoders** (`#163`, "SAE"). Gao et al., [LIT-tmpydijh](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpydijh.md), read as
  [NOTE-tmpr5ct1](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpr5ct1.md), source two `Proposed` practices. **[SOTA-tmpmnhef](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmpmnhef.md)**: train
  with TopK, transposed-decoder initialization and an auxiliary dead-latent
  loss. **[SOTA-tmp7u4vf](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmp7u4vf.md)**: report fidelity as downstream loss in
  compute-equivalent terms, not as fraction recovered against zero ablation.
- **The case against SAE probes.** Kantamneni et al., [LIT-tmp57c8v](record/literature.d/LIT-tmp57c8v.md), read as
  [NOTE-tmptkt9y](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmptkt9y.md), is `compared_against` [LIT-tmpydijh](record/literature.d/LIT-tmpydijh.md). **[SOTA-tmpzz4sk](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmpzz4sk.md),
  `Active`**: probe with logistic regression on raw activations. On 113
  datasets, adding SAE probes changes AUC by −0.003, and it does not help
  in any hard regime.

### Documentation

- **"98.2% of loss recovered" corresponds to 10% of GPT-4's compute.** Gao et
  al.'s own footnote shows zero-ablation normalization flatters an SAE.
  This is the basis of [SOTA-tmp7u4vf](record/practices.d/SOTA-tmp7u4vf.md).
- **The tuned lens's prompt-injection result has no win against its
  baseline.** Its AUROC is near 1 on five of nine tasks, but a one-layer
  Mahalanobis detector matches or beats it on eight. The paper's version 6
  also says every lens in it was undertrained. Both are recorded on the note.
- **Not filed:** Bricken et al. and Cunningham et al., the 2023 SAE papers.
  The practices rest on the later work, which compares against them.
