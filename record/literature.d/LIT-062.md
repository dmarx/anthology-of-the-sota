# LIT-062: High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models

Rombach et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2112.10752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752)

## Key takeaways

- Latent space diffusion
- Compute efficient training
- High quality generation
- Reduced memory requirements

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-187](../practices.d/SOTA-187.md).

The transferable claim is compute allocation rather than image synthesis:
likelihood-based generative models spend most of their capacity on
imperceptible detail, so strip it with a cheap model first and let the
expensive one work on what remains. The two-stage shape — learn a compact
representation, then model *that* — is a `model-architecture` decision and
recurs well beyond images.
