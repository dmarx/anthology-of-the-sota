Asked to find a lineage containing RoPE and NoPE. The record held five of the
six documents it needed and none of the edges.

## What was already there, unconnected

`LIT-045` (RoFormer), `LIT-048` (ALiBi), `LIT-192` (Position Interpolation),
`LIT-193` (YaRN), and two practices — `SOTA-063` "use RoPE" and `SOTA-151`
"rescale it". The chain page rooted the rescaling line at Position
Interpolation, because nobody had declared the edge to the thing it
interpolates. Position Interpolation's own first sentence says it extends the
context window of "RoPE-based pretrained LLMs"; the method is a rescaling of
RoPE's position indices and is meaningless without them. One line of
frontmatter, and the line roots where it belongs.

`SOTA-151 extends SOTA-063` was missing for the same reason and is the same
shape: rescaling RoPE is what a model that used RoPE does next.

## The paper that was not there

NoPE appeared in four documents — `LIT-131`, `LIT-193`, `SOTA-132`,
`SOTA-151` — plus two changelog fragments, always as *a thing Kimi models do*.
No note, no source, no author. The anthology's own rule is that a practice
names a source; this slipped past because NoPE was never the subject of a
practice, only a paragraph inside two of them.

The document that did *not* mention it is the tell: `LIT-133`, Kimi Linear,
whose paper gives NoPE a design section, an ablation table and the citation
everything else here rests on. The note was written about KDA, so the
positional half of the paper went unrecorded, and every later document then
attributed NoPE to K3 — the model report that adopted it — rather than to the
method paper sitting in the record already. `LIT-133` gains the paragraph.

Kazemnejad et al. (2023) is the source, and Kimi Linear cites it directly
(reference [49]). Filed as `LIT-207`. The thing worth knowing about it,
which the "K3 doesn't need YaRN" framing loses: it is a *controlled* study —
five positional schemes, identical hyperparameters, one model configuration —
and it comes with a theorem saying a decoder-only transformer can represent
absolute and relative position with no encoding at all. NoPE is not a model
told less; it is a model not told which function to use. It is also 107M
parameters on synthetic tasks, which is the honest limit on it.

## What was deliberately not declared

`LIT-133 compared_against LIT-045`. Kimi Linear does run NoPE against RoPE —
Table 5, a Kimi Linear (RoPE) baseline at matched 48B configuration — and it
was tempting to declare, because it is the scale evidence the 2023 paper
lacks.

It is the wrong relation. [ADR-011](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-011.md) defines `compared_against` as "this work
evaluates itself against that one", and what Kimi Linear ran is an ablation of
its own design: itself with the encoding and itself without. That is evidence
*about* the RoPE/NoPE question, not a comparison against RoFormer, and it
belongs in prose — where it now is, in the new note and in `SOTA-132`.

Declaring it anyway was tried, to see. It collapses the page: `compared_against`
joins components, so one edge fused the positional line into the delta-rule
line, six sections became five, and the merged section was titled "From
RoFormer" with eleven members including Mamba. Worth knowing before someone
declares a cross-family sibling for a good reason — the relation is right for
a rival *within* a family and expensive for a rival that already belongs to
another one.

## Left open

There is a practice-shaped hole: *apply NoPE to the global-attention layers of
a linear-attention hybrid*. `SOTA-132` carries it as "a second benefit the
practice was filed without", which is the shape of a recommendation living
inside somebody else's document. The evidence exists — Kimi Linear's ablation,
K3 at 2.8T, Falcon-H1's near-NoPE base frequency, and SwanGPT's RoPE/NoPE
interleave, which is named in Kimi Linear's related work and has not been read
here. Not
filed here, because filing a recommendation is an editorial act with a status
and a consensus level attached, and this contribution was asked for a lineage.
