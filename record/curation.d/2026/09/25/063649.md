Filed Kanerva's *Sparse Distributed Memory and Related Models* as
`LIT-tmpwxmrr`. It was the follow-up the `LIT-666` filing left open.
Unlike the book, it can be read. NASA's Technical Reports Server has the
RIACS report version (TR 92.10, NASA-CR-190553, document 19920021480) as a
58-page PDF, and the title page says it is to appear in Hassoun's *Associative
Neural Memories* (Oxford, 1993). The report was read in full. The published
chapter was not seen. Crossref has no DOI for the chapter, so the source is
the NTRS record.

## What reading it found

`LIT-641` §1 names three optimal Hamming radii and cites this review for
them. Searching the report finds two:

- the **signal-to-noise** optimum, `p = (2MT)^(-1/3)` (§3.6);
- the **capacity** analysis, asymptotically `1/[Φ⁻¹(φ)]²` of the number
  of locations (§3.7).

**Critical distance is not there.** The report derives no convergence radius.
It shows iterated retrieval converging only in two figures, and says the
noisy-address analysis "is not carried out here". `LIT-641`'s own Appendix
B.5 gives the right source: it reproduces the critical-distance curve as
"Figure 7.3 from the SDM book". So the §1 citation is wrong and the appendix
is right, and a reader who stopped at §1 would credit the wrong document.
That is what the `LIT-666` note did at version 1, following §1 in good faith.

That makes this [DP-010](../design-principles.md#dp-10) at the scale of a citation. The body text is the
sentence that gets repeated, and the correction sits in an appendix. It was
only found because the cited source was read rather than trusted.

The text was OCR'd and garbles some words ("dislributed"), so absence by
search is weaker than absence by reading. The sections where a convergence
radius would have to appear were read directly: §3.5–3.7 on fidelity and
capacity, and the figures on noisy cues.

## What changed besides the new note

- `LIT-666` to version 2, with a history note. Critical distance is now
  credited to the book, and signal-to-noise and capacity to the review.
  One of its two open questions is answered from `LIT-641` Appendix B.1: the
  book derives the exact circle-intersection equation in its Appendix B.
  That answer is still secondhand. The book remains unread.
- `LIT-641` `extends` the review as well as the book. Its β analysis uses the
  review's signal-to-noise and capacity optima.
- `THEORY-097` says which optimum comes from which source.

## What was not done

- **`LIT-641`'s note was not edited** to record the mis-citation. Its body
  does not attribute the three criteria to anything, so it asserts nothing
  wrong. The finding lives in `LIT-tmpwxmrr` and in `LIT-666`'s history.
- **The published chapter was not compared** with the report. The two
  sources found for its page range disagree (51–75 and 50–76). The chapter
  number is garbled in the report's OCR. Neither is recorded.
- **The book is still unread.** Whether it makes the cerebellar mapping as
  the review does, discrepancies included, stays open.
