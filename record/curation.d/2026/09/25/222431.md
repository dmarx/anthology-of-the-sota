This is the second pass over the worklist in `2026/09/25/214751`. Each paper
was read in full, and each had been promised in an earlier entry and then
dropped.

**A note on how this pass came about.** The owner's request to review skipped
readings was meant to find candidates for *nucleation*: work this anthology set
aside as out of scope. It was read instead as a backlog of in-scope filings.
The first wave ([#373](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/373)) and this second wave both came from that misreading.
The owner chose to keep this wave. The survey the request actually asked for
has since been run, and its result goes to nucleation, not here.

## What was filed

The worklist promised eleven papers. The transfer-study pair turned out to be
a pair, so twelve were filed:

- **[#290](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/290)'s promoted list:**
  - Integrated Gradients and Sanity Checks for Saliency Maps
  - EDM2 and FLIP
  - Min-SNR and Primer
  - Narang et al. and the attention-only controlled study
- **The dropout cluster of 09/17:** Li et al., Gal & Ghahramani and Hara et al.

Five practices were filed, all `Proposed`:

- Put dropout after the last batch-norm layer.
- Choose the EMA length post hoc, per metric.
- Use magnitude-preserving layers in a diffusion denoiser.
- Mask half the patches in CLIP-style training and spend the saving on batch.
- Run both randomization tests before trusting an attribution map.

## The pattern: every headline is a ratio against the weakest baseline

The first wave found skims overstating papers. This wave found the papers
overstating themselves, and always the same way:

- **Min-SNR's 3.4× speedup** is against constant-weighted x0-prediction.
  Against plain ε-MSE, the usual default, FID moves 4.21 → 4.14 at 1M
  iterations, in a single run.
- **Primer's 4.2× saving** is against ReLU T5. Against SwiGLU + RMSNorm it is
  about 2×, and squared ReLU beats SwiGLU in one figure at 110M.
- **FLIP's accuracy gain** is the larger batch that masking pays for. At equal
  batch it is parity, and at ViT-B/16 it is 0.2 behind.
- **Li et al.'s "outperform their baselines"** means beating the same network
  before BN re-estimation, not the network without dropout. The real finding,
  a large measured cost of dropout upstream of BN, is sound. The headline fix
  is worth about 0.2 top-1.
- **Narang et al.** are strong evidence against the modifications that lost:
  ReZero, Fixup, Universal Transformer and parameter sharing. For the winners
  the evidence is weak. SwiGLU's gain was re-run in GLU Variants' own codebase,
  which confirms the number but does not test whether it transfers. Seven
  architecture practices now say what Narang found for them.
- **Integrated Gradients' only empirical support** is visual inspection, the
  kind of evidence Sanity Checks shows to mislead. Sanity Checks itself gives
  Integrated Gradients no verdict.

## Record changes worth knowing

- **`SOTA-034` (SwiGLU) now has a `contested_by`: Primer.** It is the only
  paper in the record that disputes SwiGLU's quality rather than its range.
  The recommendation is unchanged.
- **[LIT-020](../../record/literature.d/LIT-020.md), [LIT-023](../../record/literature.d/LIT-023.md) and [LIT-047](../../record/literature.d/LIT-047.md) gain `model-architecture` as a secondary
  tag.** Narang et al. compared against them. That joined them to a lineage
  whose other members all carry the tag, and the lint reported the line as
  sharing none. The relation was right and the tag was missing. A reader
  browsing model-architecture would expect a token mixer, a normalization
  layer and a residual reparameterization.
- **[LIT-605](../../record/literature.d/LIT-605.md) and [SOTA-372](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-372.md) gain a tag each.** The reason is the same: FLIP joined
  two lines.

## Left open

- **No practice recommends sampling from a weight average at all.** EDM2
  builds on that step, and it has no run without EMA, so it cannot source one.
- **Min-SNR** could be a `Proposed` practice if a second group shows the ε
  gain holds at the end of training.
- **Primer's per-head depthwise convolution** has no practice.
- **The topics have no word for uncertainty or calibration.** Gal &
  Ghahramani went under `analysis-and-evaluation`. That is a finding about
  the vocabulary: the fix is to add a word, not to settle for the nearest one.
- **[SOTA-337](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-337.md)'s condition may be too narrow.** EDM2 hints that it should not be
  limited to an ImageNet classifier in the pipeline.
- **[SOTA-005](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-005.md) has a cause it does not list.** Its running statistics are wrong
  because dropout changed the network, not because the data moved.

The rest of the worklist in `214751` still stands.
