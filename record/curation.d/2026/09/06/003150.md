title: 'Nine promotion conditions, written honestly, and what writing them exposed'
created: '2026-09-06T00:31:50'

The rule [ADR-014](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-014.md) adopted is that a `promote_when:` names a *kind* of
result, not a quantity of them, and the test is: could this be satisfied by a
paper that would not actually change my confidence? Writing nine of them was
supposed to be the real test of the rule. It was, and the interesting part is
how often the honest answer was narrower than the practice's own body
suggested.

Three of the nine had to be written as **exclusions**, because the evidence
that does not count had already arrived:

- [SOTA-122](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-122.md) (learnable multipliers) waits on a group *training with them*.
  [LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md) confirmed the weight-decay equilibrium the practice diagnoses, at
  1.2B, from an independent group — and adopted a different remedy. Confirming
  a diagnosis is not adopting a treatment.
- [SOTA-144](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-144.md) (CompleteP) waits on a group training *under CompleteP*. [LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md)
  again: depth transfer reached by pinning weight-matrix norms instead. Two
  routes to one property is evidence the property is reachable.
- [SOTA-136](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-136.md) (mHC) waits on an answer to the Birkhoff-polytope objection, not
  on a further independent evaluation — one of those arrived and is the
  reason this practice's condition had to be rewritten at all.

Two turned out to be waiting on something the field may never produce in the
form the practice asked for:

- [SOTA-120](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-120.md) is `Deferred` because the *claim* is settled and its adoption
  is not, so its condition asks for a report that states the choice rather
  than leaving it implicit in a config. Most reports do not.
- [SOTA-141](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-141.md) (decay to zero) had to exclude a comparison that shares one peak
  learning rate across schedules, because that compares peaks, not schedules
  — which is the whole methodological point of the source paper.

And one — [SOTA-124](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-124.md), the memorization window — turned out to be waiting on a
*second measurement of the same quantity*, which nobody has published. The
practice rests on one data point plus an assumed linear scaling in
parameters. Writing that down as a condition is the first time the record has
said so plainly.

The count that matters: before today, four of 144 practices stated a
condition anywhere, all in prose. Now every practice that is not in force
states one, in a field, and the lint will not accept a new provisional
practice without it.
