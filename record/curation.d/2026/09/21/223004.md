**Picked off the 5-revisit tier for adjacency, like the two before it.** The
hyper-connections line is the densest cluster this record holds — a trunk
(`SOTA-169`), two branches (`SOTA-136`, `SOTA-137`), five sources and two
published attacks — so a new paper in it had the best chance of being
load-bearing.

**The first thing to check was which condition it triggers, and the answer was
neither.** `SOTA-169`'s promote_when excludes "another paper proposing a
fourth constraint". That looked like a direct hit until the abstract made
clear this is not a fourth constraint: it keeps the doubly-stochastic
constraint and replaces the approximation with an exact construction. So the
exclusion does not apply — and the trunk still does not move, for the ordinary
reason that nothing here ships in a released model or isolates width.

**`SOTA-136`'s condition is the one that bites, and it is unmet for the third
time.** It asks for a measurement of stream homogenization with depth. Grep
the paper for homogenization, diversity, distinctness, collapse: zero hits.
`LIT-139` was the production report and measured no stream statistic;
`LIT-152` was the independent evaluation that did not test the constraint;
this is the third. Three documents that each looked like the one, none of
them it. That is the promote_when working exactly as `ADR-014` intends — it
could not be satisfied by a paper that would not change the record's
confidence, and three papers have now failed to satisfy it for three
different reasons.

**One check changed the reading, and it is worth recording because the first
reading was wrong.** The paper opens with a worked example where 20 SK
iterations leave column sums of 1.92/0.59/0.59 — on an input with
`α = 10⁻¹³`. That reads as adversarial, and the note nearly said so. It is
not: Figure 4 measures the distribution of `log(1/ν)` over actual SK inputs
during training and finds about **27.9%** of them at `1/ν ≥ 10¹³`. The
pathological case is a quarter of real inputs. Checking before writing the
criticism is what `DP-004` looks like when it works in the paper's favour
rather than against it.

**The sharpest thing in the unit is a missed experiment, not a result.**
`LIT-151` argues the doubly-stochastic set can only contract and must
therefore erode what distinguishes the streams. Under Sinkhorn-Knopp mHC that
argument has an escape hatch — the matrices are not actually in the set, so
whatever distinctness survives might be surviving through the gap. mHC-lite
closes the hatch by construction. It is therefore the cleanest available test
of the objection, the objection predicts it should homogenize more, and no
stream statistic was reported either way.

Naming that is the useful thing this record can do here. It is not evidence
against `LIT-151`, because the predicted outcome was never measured. It is a
two-year dispute sitting one histogram away from resolution, on a model the
authors already trained, with code already public — and it went into the
open questions of both documents rather than into a claim.
