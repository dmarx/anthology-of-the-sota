# LIT-243: Towards Crowdsourced Training of Large Neural Networks using Decentralized Mixture-of-Experts

Ryabinin and Gusev (2020) — [ARXIV-2002.04013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04013)

## What it is

Learning@home: train a mixture of experts spread over unreliable volunteer
hardware, routed through a DHT, so no worker holds the whole model.

From the paper's own abstract:

> Many recent breakthroughs in deep learning were achieved by training
> increasingly larger models on massive datasets. However, training such
> models can be prohibitively expensive. For instance, the cluster used to
> train GPT-3 costs over \$250 million.

## Standing in the anthology

`Deferred`, which here means what the vocabulary says: in the corpus, not
read closely enough to place. It arrived in the imported batch, which
brought in the volunteer- and internet-scale branch, where workers join and
leave.
