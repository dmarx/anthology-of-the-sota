# LIT-313: The Variational Formulation of the Fokker-Planck Equation

Jordan et al. (1998) — [DOI:10.1137/S0036141096303359](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0036141096303359)

## What it is

The Fokker-Planck equation is the gradient flow of free energy in the
Wasserstein metric, realized by an implicit variational scheme.
