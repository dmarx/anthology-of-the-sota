These are the eight papers the video line cited without holding. Filing
them fixed four sentences in documents that were already `Active`. None of
those sentences could have been checked before, because the paper each one
described was not in the record.

## What the sentences had in common

Each one described a paper the record did not hold:

- **"Three concurrent origins of the straight path."** This was in two
  notes and a practice. The third origin, stochastic interpolants, uses a
  cos/sin path (its eq. 5). It mentions the linear path once, in Remark
  D.5, and credits it to Liu et al.
- **"The papers that named exposure bias (scheduled sampling, Professor
  Forcing, MIXER)."** This is the usual grouping. A count of the term in
  the three texts gives 0, 0 and 11.
- **"For discrete tokens, teacher forcing does not diverge in the same
  way."** This is plausible and was unsourced. The papers support "modest
  and confounded". They neither support nor refute "does not diverge",
  because none of them measured divergence.
- **SiT as `introduced_by` for the post-hoc diffusion coefficient.** It was
  the right group and the wrong paper: the framework said it ten
  months earlier.

[DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4) applies here. A pass that checks sentences against the documents
they cite cannot find a defect when the document is missing. In each case
the sentence was the only representation of the paper the record had.
Filing the source is what makes the sentence checkable. So a note saying
"X is not held" is a debt, not a hedge: the sentences near it are
unverified by construction.

## Method that worked

Readers drafted the notes from full texts. Every claim that would change
an existing document was re-checked against the extracted text before it
was applied. This included term counts, table cells, and the version of
the paper each quote came from. The 2303.08797 v1 abstract says the latent
amplitude can also be tuned after training. By v3 only ε is post hoc. The
two versions disagree, and the note says which one it read.

One trap came from my own writing, not from the readers. While updating
CausVid, I first wrote that DMD2 "reports the same trade on ImageNet". DMD2
reports its diversity loss on SDXL, and it gives no cause. The sentence
fitted the story, and that is why it was worth checking.

## Left alone

`NOTE-197` (the SiT reading) still says the record has "nothing resembling"
R3. It is a reading, and that sentence was true of the record when it was
read. The correction sits in `LIT-447` and `SOTA-265`. Journal entries that
say DMD was not held were not edited either.

FLUX.1 has no technical report. `LIT-573` (FLUX.1 Kontext) §2 is the
nearest thing to one, and it is already filed.
