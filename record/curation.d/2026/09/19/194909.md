`#85`'s residue is read. Three notes — `LIT-004`, `LIT-055`, `LIT-095` — were
unsourced, unread and in force; all three now have readings, and the audit
that identified them is finished rather than merely accurate.

**That distinction was the correction.** The previous pass reported the audit
half "essentially done" at one bare note and three unread, and treated the
readings as residue for somebody else. An audit exists to find work. A list of
work is not the work, and a pass that produces the list and stops has
converted a content problem into a tracking problem — which is the same move
`DP-004` warns about one level up, where a query written in a devlog entry is
a query that gets reinvented rather than run.

## What the readings changed, which was less than hoped and more than nothing

**All three confirmed the determination their note already carried.** None
produced a practice. That is a real outcome and worth stating plainly, because
the alternative story — three readings, three new practices — is the one a
reader would expect from an audit's closing pass.

What each added was precision about *why*:

- **`LIT-095` (LPIPS).** Its most transferable claim is a **negative** one.
  The effect is not VGG's, not ImageNet's and not supervision's — it survives
  losing all three, including under stacked k-means initialization — but it is
  also not architecture's alone, because a randomly initialized network does
  not produce it. What survives is "train on something", which nobody needs
  told. The old standing said "the anthology is about training rather than
  evaluation"; that is true and it was not the sharpest reason.
- **`LIT-004` (gradient checkpointing).** The paper has two halves and only
  one is famous. The allocation half — liveness analysis, in-place operations,
  memory sharing, framed explicitly as register allocation in a compiler —
  deliberately gets no practice, because it is what a framework does for you
  and this record's `distributed-optimization` entries are about decisions a
  person makes. The reading also supplies limits the abstract could not: the
  segmentation has to exist, the win is over feature maps specifically, it
  composes with swapping and model parallelism *without touching the
  interconnect*, and 30% is one architecture rather than a constant.
- **`LIT-055` (P-Tuning v2).** Confirms "competitive on quality" as measured
  rather than charitable — parity from 330M to 10B and on hard sequence
  labelling at 0.1-3% of parameters — which is what makes the existing
  no-practice argument work. That argument is that the deciding property was
  deployment shape, not accuracy, and it only holds if the accuracy claim is
  real.

## The one finding that is not about any of the three papers

Reading P-Tuning v2 against the current record surfaces a convergence that
did not exist when the note was last touched, because `SOTA-231` was filed
two days ago:

| | what decides | what people tune |
|---|---|---|
| P-Tuning v2 (2021) | prompt **depth** — every layer, not the input | reparameterization — *inconsistent across datasets* |
| QLoRA (2023), `SOTA-231` | **number of adapted matrices** — all linear layers | rank `r` — *no effect across the sweep* |

Two parameter-efficient families, four years and one mechanism apart, both
concluding that **coverage decides and the knob people tune does not.**
Neither cites the other; they are not in the same literature.

**I did not record this as replication, and the reason is worth keeping.**
The temptation is to move `SOTA-231` off `unreplicated` — its
`consensus_note` says "One group, one sweep. Nobody in this record has agreed
or disagreed", and here is something that looks like agreeing. But it is a
different mechanism in a different setting on different tasks. `ADR-010` says
contrast is not support; the cousin this needs is that **structural agreement
is not measurement**. Two papers reaching the same shape of conclusion is a
reason to look, not evidence about either claim.

It is written into `LIT-055`'s standing and `NOTE-182`'s connections
instead, where a reader meets it as an observation rather than as a count.

## A query correction

`LIT-040` was in my previous pass's "bare notes" count. It is not bare: it has
carried a full determination and a reading since 2026-09-10. The regex looked
for phrases like "carries no practice" and "unfiled", and `LIT-040` says
*"nothing is filed from this reading"* — the same determination in words the
query did not anticipate. One false positive in a population of 118, from a
query written to find documents by how they phrase an absence. The lesson is
the one this session keeps meeting from different directions: a claim about
the record's own contents is not checkable, so any query over how such claims
are *phrased* is a heuristic, and its output is a candidate list rather than a
count.
