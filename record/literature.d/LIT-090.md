# LIT-090: Symbolic Discovery of Optimization Algorithms

Chen et al., Google (2023) — [ARXIV-2302.06675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06675)

## Key takeaways

- Optimizer design posed as program search, with the selection and
  simplification strategies that stop a proxy-task winner from being an
  artefact of the proxy.
- What it found is **Lion** (Evolved Sign Momentum): keep only the momentum
  — half of Adam's optimizer state — and take the *sign* of an interpolation
  between momentum and gradient, so every parameter's update has the same
  magnitude. It is a rule simple enough to read, which is the point of
  searching program space rather than tuning a parametrized family.
- Results across domains: ViT up to +2% on ImageNet and up to 5× less JFT
  pretraining compute; 88.3% zero-shot ImageNet on vision-language
  contrastive learning; a better FID with up to 2.3× less training compute
  on diffusion models; on autoregressive and masked language modelling,
  similar to or better than Adam.
- Two consequences of the sign update that are recipe, not trivia: the gain
  grows with batch size, and the learning rate must be smaller than Adam's
  because the sign function makes the update norm larger.
- The paper states its own limits — scenarios where the improvement is small
  or not statistically significant — and reports production deployment on
  Google's search-ads CTR model.

## Standing in the anthology

**The retirement here is inherited and should be re-examined.** The
migration filed this paper twice — once under its real title and once as
<!-- inactive-ok: LIT-105 — the retired duplicate, named as what was merged away -->
*Lion: Learning with Implicit Optimization* ([LIT-105](LIT-105.md), now retired as the
duplicate) — and retired this copy as "a research direction without
demonstrated practical impact". That reason was written against a
four-bullet description of the paper as automated discovery and theory. It
is not what the paper is: Lion is a concrete optimizer with cross-domain
results and a named production deployment.

The status is left as it was found rather than reversed here, because
whether the anthology wants a practice for Lion is a curation decision and
not a citation repair. What has changed is that the decision can now be made
against the paper. The record's live optimizer advice has moved elsewhere
regardless — [SOTA-121](../practices.d/SOTA-121.md) recommends Muon — and Lion's sign update is the
nearest neighbour to Muon's orthogonalised one, which is an argument for
reading them together.
