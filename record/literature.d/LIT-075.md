# LIT-075: Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models

Karras et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2206.00364](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364)

## Key takeaways

- Comprehensive design space analysis
- Sampling strategy optimization
- Architecture improvements
- Training stability insights

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-188](../practices.d/SOTA-188.md).

<!-- inactive-ok-block: ADR-020 — Proposed, named as the scope decision under which this note was read -->
Filed under the widened scope ([ADR-020](../decisions.d/ADR-020.md)): the paper's contribution is a
preconditioning scheme, a training-noise distribution and a loss weighting,
which are `training-optimization` claims that happen to have been found in
image models. Under the scope this replaced, three quarters of the corpus's
diffusion material read as ineligible rather than unread, and this note went
a year sourcing nothing.

The part worth carrying beyond diffusion: when a network is conditioned on a
variable that changes the difficulty of its task, normalize the target so the
loss means the same thing at every value of it, and sample that variable
where the signal is rather than uniformly.
