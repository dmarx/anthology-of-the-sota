# LIT-tmprrx5a: Broken Neural Scaling Laws

Caballero et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2210.14891](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14891)

## What it is

Broken neural scaling laws: a functional form with breaks that fits and
extrapolates the scaling behaviours a single power law cannot, including
non-monotone and inflected curves.

From the paper's own abstract:

> We present a smoothly broken power law functional form (that we refer to
> as a Broken Neural Scaling Law (BNSL)) that accurately models &
> extrapolates the scaling behaviors of deep neural networks (i.e. how the
> evaluation metric of interest varies as amount of compute used for
> training (or inference), number of model parameters, training dataset
> size, model input size, number of training steps, or upstream
> performance varies) for various architectures & for each of various
> tasks within a large & diverse set of upstream & downstream tasks, in
> zero-shot, prompted, & finetuned settings.
