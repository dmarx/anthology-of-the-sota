### Added

- A `domain:` axis on practices, orthogonal to the seven topics: what a
  recommendation is known to apply to, as against what kind of claim it makes.
  Many-valued, because where a practice holds is a set that accumulates, not a
  position on a scale. `domain-general` marks the practices that transfer
  regardless of modality — the answer to "what can I take?" that the record
  could not previously give.

### Changed

- The recommendations are no longer scoped to language-model training. A
  practice belongs here if it is an instruction one of the seven topics can
  express; where the work was discovered is not the test. Vision, generative
  and inference work is in scope, and the bias toward language models is now a
  bias rather than a boundary.
- [DP-008](docs/design-principles.md#dp-8)'s worked example described the old scope as a fact. Amended to
  describe it as a decision that was replaced, and why.
