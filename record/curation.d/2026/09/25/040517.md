`THEORY-097` says attention approximates Kanerva's sparse distributed
memory. It is sourced to `LIT-641`, and the record did not hold Kanerva. This
entry files the 1988 book as `LIT-tmpb41ha`. `LIT-641` `extends` it, since
that paper is a mapping onto this model and has nothing to say without it.

## The source could not be read

The book is behind MIT Press, and mitpress.mit.edu returns 403 to the build
environment, through the reader tools too. Two accounts were reachable:

- OSTI record 7055202. Its catalogue abstract lists the contents: idealized
  neurons, neurons as address decoders, the search for the best match,
  distributed storage, sequence storage and retrieval.
- `LIT-641` §1 and §5, which review the model and credit its parts. They
  credit some parts to the book and some to Kanerva's 1993 review.

The note follows that split. The write and read over a Hamming radius, the
address decoders and the cerebellar mapping are the book's, by `LIT-641`'s
credit. The three optimal-radius criteria that `LIT-641` fits β against are
credited to the 1993 review, so the note does not give them to the book. A
note that repeats the attention paper's summary under the book's name would
be [DP-010](../design-principles.md#dp-10)'s failure: the travelled sentence filed as the source's.

## Identifiers

Crossref has no DOI for the book, and neither does MIT Press's own `10.7551`
prefix. Its search does turn up Willshaw's 1990 review in *Cognitive
Neuropsychology*, `10.1080/02643299008253443`, which confirms MIT Press 1988,
176 pages, ISBN 0-262-11132-2. ACM's Guide lists it as `10.5555/534853`.
That prefix is ACM-internal and does not resolve through doi.org, so the
DOI remote would report it as unverifiable. The source is the publisher's
catalogue URL, the weakest kind the record accepts. It is the paperback
page (ISBN 9780262514699), the only one MIT Press still lists.

## What was not done

- **The book was not read.** Two questions are left open for a reading, and
  the note names both. First, is the hypersphere-intersection weighting
  derived in the book, or made explicit later? Second, which biological
  claims are the book's own?
- **Kanerva's 1993 review was not filed.** It is where `LIT-641` sources
  the optimal radii. Filing it would let `THEORY-097` cite those radii
  directly.
- **No practice moved.** `SOTA-192` recommends QK-norm on its own evidence.
