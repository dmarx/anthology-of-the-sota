### Changed

- `compared_against:` now declares itself as its `converse` on both SOTA and
  LIT. Upstream made the declaration the thing that licenses `luria link
  --fix` to write the other side of a symmetric relation, so without it the
  guard and the fixer both go quiet — silently, which is why this is here.
  Behaviour is unchanged from before: a one-sided comparison is a finding,
  and the fixer satisfies it.
- `extends:` deliberately declares no converse. An `extended_by:` field is
  derivable from the forward declaration, and this record already prefers
  deriving that direction — `superseded_by:` is stored with no `supersedes:`.
  The chain page shows what forked from a trunk today; if reading the trunk
  note *alone* and seeing its forks turns out to matter, that is the reason
  to revisit.
