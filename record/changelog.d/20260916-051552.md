### Added

- `schemes.NOTE.references.paper` declares `invariant: tags` — a reading and
  the paper it reads are one subject, so a topic they do not share is a
  disagreement about that paper. The only cross-scheme relation in this record
  that asserts one, and the first assertion of any kind across a scheme
  boundary. Four findings in 158 edges; [ADR-tmpvv2d8](record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpvv2d8.md) has the measurement over
  all six heterogeneous relations and why the other five assert nothing.

### Changed

- Pinned to luria 0.23.0, which is what makes an invariant declarable on a
  relation rather than only on a chain ([LU-#272](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/issues/272)).
- `docs/reports/unbound-lineage.md` goes from 22 unbound relations to 26 and
  gains a `Declared by` column, naming the chain or relation each finding came
  from.
