**Three checks before filing anything, and two of them changed the plan.**

**First: did today's thirty-five new documents introduce defects?** The
record's own reports say no — **0 unbound relations**, and the one unbound
line is the pre-existing `SOTA-036/037/038` one. That is the check worth
running before adding more, and it came back clean on the relations, which
were the part most likely to be wrong.

**It did not come back clean on spelling.** Three citations in a concretized
code's old spelling, all in the curation entry I wrote *about that exact
trap*. `luria link --fix` cannot reach inside backticks; the fix is by hand,
and the record had already said so — the `LIT-489` fragment from a day
earlier ends "worth knowing before the next fragment". I wrote the warning's
successor and walked into it anyway. Rewritten by hand, per the established
handling.

**Second: is neural collapse load-bearing?** The previous unit deferred it
with "it gets a unit or it gets nothing", which obliged me to actually test
it rather than default either way. Grepping for simplex, equiangular,
terminal-phase training and class-mean geometry returns the two documents I
filed yesterday that name it as a resemblance, and nothing else. It would be a
trunk with no descendants. The schema permits that — `explains` is optional —
but permitted is not warranted, and the honest justification available was
"famous and adjacent", which is the `DP-005` trap. **Not filed**, test
recorded.

**Third: what is left on [#180](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/180)?** Every one of the fourteen entries in the
issue's published ranking is filed — including the three Bernstein papers its
second comment proposed as the next unit, done on 19 September, and
`THEORY-024` which was demoted from `Active` the following day when an
outside group tested the duality frame and came back negative. The worklist is
exhausted, so it needed recomputing rather than continuing.

**The recomputation needed a correction I should flag.** My first pass matched
only bare `NNNN.NNNNN` identifiers and found 139 arXiv-resolvable papers
against the issue's reported 2,196 — a discrepancy large enough that the right
response was to distrust my extraction rather than the issue. The snapshot's
keys are mostly `arxiv.NNNN.NNNNN`, with `arxiv:` and bare variants and
`openreview.`, `nature.`, `pnas.` and `url.` prefixes for everything else.
Matching the three arXiv shapes gives 2,169, which is close to the issue's
number and reproducible. **299 papers at ≥2 distinct reading days, 83 at ≥3,
31 at ≥4; 33 absent from the record at ≥3.**

**And the top of the refreshed list held a gap I made myself.**
`ARXIV-1503.02406` — Tishby and Zaslavsky, the paper the entire
information-bottleneck line descends from — absent, while this record holds
three papers arguing about it plus a theory and a practice filed from that
argument. Issue [#220](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/220) was closed on the strength of a dispute whose trunk was
missing, and the trunk was missing because I did not go looking for it.

**Reading it changed what the dispute means.** `SOTA-312` says to state the
noise or binning assumption behind any mutual information reported for a
deterministic network; `THEORY-058` exists because `I(h;X)` is infinite for
a deterministic map, so the reported compression is manufactured by the
analyst's binning. Fatal to the 2017 measurement, and **not an objection the
founding paper failed to anticipate**: it says, in one sentence, that
approaching the IB limit requires stochastic mapping between the layers. The
condition was stated at the beginning and dropped by the work that went
looking for the picture. The two-phase claim stays refuted on its own
evidence; what moves is where the defect sits — not the frame's silence, but a
measurement applied to a regime the frame had already excluded.

**The famous figure says it is a hypothesis.** "A qualitative information
plane, with a hypothesized path of the layers in a typical DNN." Everyone went
looking for the green line; the caption says it was drawn.

**Two claims in that paper have never been examined by anyone.** Optimal depth
set by the bifurcation points of the IB tradeoff, and hierarchical
representations as structural phase transitions on the information curve.
Those are checkable architecture claims. Three papers of downstream dispute,
all about compression, none about either. `DP-004` again, and this time the
narrow query was the whole field's — including this record's, when it filed
the dispute and called it closed.
