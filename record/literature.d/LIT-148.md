# LIT-148: Tensor Programs V: Tuning Large Neural Networks via Zero-Shot Hyperparameter Transfer

Yang and Hu, Microsoft and OpenAI (2022) — [ARXIV-2203.03466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03466)

## Key takeaways

- Under the standard parametrization, the optimal learning rate shifts as a
  network gets wider, so hyperparameters found on a small model are wrong
  for a large one. Under the Maximal Update Parametrization (µP) — a
  specific scaling of initialisation variances and per-layer learning rates
  with width — many optimal hyperparameters are stable across width.
- µTransfer: parametrize the target model in µP, tune on a narrow proxy,
  and transfer the values to the full width without tuning it directly.
- Demonstrations: transferring from a 13M-parameter model beats the
  published BERT-large (350M) at a total tuning cost of one BERT-large
  pretraining; transferring from 40M beats the published GPT-3 6.7B with
  tuning that cost about 7% of that model's pretraining.
- Transfer is across width; depth, batch size and training length were
  not covered, which is what [LIT-149](LIT-149.md) and [LIT-150](LIT-150.md) take up.
