# LIT-tmpyn6yr: Classifying high-dimensional Gaussian mixtures: Where kernel methods fail and neural networks succeed

Refinetti et al. (2021) — [ARXIV-2102.11742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11742)

## What it is

A two-layer network with a few hidden units beats kernel methods on a
Gaussian-mixture classification task in the high-dimensional limit, which
separates feature learning from lazy training.

From the paper's own abstract:

> A recent series of theoretical works showed that the dynamics of neural
> networks with a certain initialisation are well-captured by kernel
> methods. Concurrent empirical work demonstrated that kernel methods can
> come close to the performance of neural networks on some image
> classification tasks. These results raise the question of whether neural
> networks only learn successfully if kernels also learn successfully,
> despite neural networks being more expressive.
