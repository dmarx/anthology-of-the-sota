Fifth unit down the reading feed, and the first where the contribution is a
synthesis rather than a filing. Also the unit where I made the mistake
`CLAUDE.md` names explicitly and caught it from the lint rather than from
care.

## The gap I went looking for

The record holds 29 explanations and almost nothing about what an optimizer's
trajectory actually does. I expected to find [THEORY-013](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-013.md) as the incumbent —
the SGD noise scale setting generalisation — and to file alongside it.

It is `Rejected`. Shallue et al. swept 35 workloads and found the
generalisation effect vanishes once metaparameters are retuned, so the
phenomenon it explained was mostly an artefact of holding the learning rate
fixed. **The record's only account of optimizer dynamics is one it does not
believe**, and edge of stability — among the most-replicated empirical
findings of the last five years — had no document at all.

## What I got wrong, and how I found out

I wrote three documents describing `THEORY-013` as "the closest antecedent
the record has", "the closest thing the record holds", and "unreconciled with
this" — all phrasings that read it as live. I wrote them from memory of
having seen the code, not from looking.

`luria lint` caught it: *"[THEORY-013](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-013.md) is Rejected, cited 5× in 3 file(s)."*

`CLAUDE.md` says to write acknowledgements "from the lint's own report, after
running it, never from memory of a document's status". I had internalised
that as a rule about *directives* and it is a rule about *prose*. A sentence
describing a document's standing is a claim about its status exactly as much
as a directive vouching for it is, and I had no more business asserting one
from memory than the other.

## What the correction turned into

The fix was more interesting than the error. Kunin et al. use the *same* SDE
machinery `THEORY-013` was built on, so the natural next move — quietly drop
the reference — would have left the record holding a rejected account and a
new paper built from its tools, with nothing saying how they relate.

So all three passages now name the rejection at length and say what it does
and does not reach. What was rejected is a claim about **test accuracy**;
Kunin et al. make no claim about test accuracy. They say where the weights go
and how fast, under an explicit modified objective. Shallue's sweep is not
evidence against that, because it did not measure it.

[ADR-034](../../../../decisions.d/ADR-034.md) is the rule that makes this statable: `Rejected` on a theory means
the *reason* is wrong. It does not retire the mathematics. Reviving a
rejected account by association with a neighbour that shares its tools is the
error available here, and three paragraphs exist to block it.

## The synthesis

Three papers, 2021, 2024 and 2025, none citing the others, each measuring a
different collapse in the same plot:

- **Time.** At the edge of stability the optimizer oscillates; the plotted
  path is the time-average of one that is not smooth.
- **Data and direction.** Transitions are frequent, abrupt and differently
  timed, and their sum is smooth. *Smoothness is what many breakthroughs look
  like added up.*
- **The tail.** Long after the curve flattens the weights keep travelling,
  distance growing as a power law.

[THEORY-031](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-031.md) states the conjunction, which no single paper does. That is
[DP-007](../../../../principles.d/DP-007.md)'s shape — agreement with no author and so no arrival event — given
one, and it is the second time this week the useful thing was a claim only
the record could make from holding several papers at once.

It also generalises [THEORY-028](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-028.md), filed two days ago: one plateau shown to be a
circuit under construction, by patching the circuit in. That was a single
transition made visible by intervention. This says the population is large.

## Where I held the line on status

[THEORY-031](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-031.md) is `Active` and [THEORY-030](../../../../theory.d/THEORY-030.md) is `Proposed`, from the same
contribution, and the asymmetry is the point.

The synthesis is `Active` because it is the *weaker* claim: not why the curve
is lossy in each case, but that it is, measured three ways by three groups.
The Central Flows account is `Proposed` despite having the better evidence in
its own frame — a predictive flow and a clean ablation — because **every
result in it is full-batch and every recipe this record holds is
stochastic**. A theory about optimization that holds only without stochastic
gradients explains a setting nobody here trains in.

No practice is filed from it for the same reason. The temptation was
"read a learning rate as a curvature penalty", which is a genuinely useful
reframing and would have asserted a transfer nobody has made.
