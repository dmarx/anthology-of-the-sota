# LIT-088: Scaling Vision Transformers to 22 Billion Parameters

Zhai et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2302.05442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05442)

## Key takeaways

- Three changes carry ViT from 4B to 22B: **parallel attention/MLP blocks**
  (`y = x + MLP(LN(x)) + Attention(LN(x))`, which lets the QKV projections fuse
  with the MLP's first linear and the out-projection with its second —
  PaLM reports 15% faster training from the same change), **QK
  normalization**, and **omitted biases**
- **The instability is diagnosed, not just fixed.** Training diverged after a
  few thousand steps at ~8B, caused by *"extremely large values in attention
  logits, which lead to (almost one-hot) attention weights with near-zero
  entropy"*. The fix follows from the mechanism: LayerNorm the queries and keys
  before the dot product, `softmax[(1/√d)·LN(XW_Q)(LN(XW_K))ᵀ]`
- Dropping biases from QKV projections and LayerNorms improved **accelerator
  utilisation by 3% with no quality loss** — but biases are **kept on the MLP
  dense layers**, explicitly departing from PaLM, for observed quality with no
  speed cost. Bias removal is not uniform
- LayerNorms applied **without bias and centering**, citing Zhang & Sennrich —
  that is RMSNorm, keeping the gain
- **54.9% model FLOPs utilisation**; 89.5% ImageNet; linear probing of ViT-22B
  can approach or exceed full fine-tuning of smaller models at high resolution

running Muon with QK-norm and no clip — but nothing in the record reads a
source establishing QK-norm. This is one of the earliest at scale, with the
mechanism named and an isolated before/after. A QK-norm practice is a candidate
for a session scoped to the registry.
