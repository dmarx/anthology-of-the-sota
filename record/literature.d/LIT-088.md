# LIT-088: Scaling Vision Transformers to 22 Billion Parameters

Zhai et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2302.05442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05442)

## Key takeaways

- Three changes carry ViT from 4B to 22B: **parallel attention/MLP blocks**
  (`y = x + MLP(LN(x)) + Attention(LN(x))`, which lets the QKV projections fuse
  with the MLP's first linear and the out-projection with its second —
  PaLM reports 15% faster training from the same change), **QK
  normalization**, and **omitted biases**
- **The instability is diagnosed, not just fixed.** Training diverged after a
  few thousand steps at ~8B, caused by *"extremely large values in attention
  logits, which lead to (almost one-hot) attention weights with near-zero
  entropy"*. The fix follows from the mechanism: LayerNorm the queries and keys
  before the dot product, `softmax[(1/√d)·LN(XW_Q)(LN(XW_K))ᵀ]`
- Dropping biases from QKV projections and LayerNorms improved **accelerator
  utilisation by 3% with no quality loss** — but biases are **kept on the MLP
  dense layers**, explicitly departing from PaLM, for observed quality with no
  speed cost. Bias removal is not uniform
- LayerNorms applied **without bias and centering**, citing Zhang & Sennrich —
  that is RMSNorm, keeping the gain
- **54.9% model FLOPs utilisation**; 89.5% ImageNet; linear probing of ViT-22B
  can approach or exceed full fine-tuning of smaller models at high resolution

## Standing in the anthology

Read — [NOTE-058](../notes.d/NOTE-058.md). Retagged to `model-stability`: the paper's own framing is
efficiency and stability at scale, and the stability diagnosis is what survives
the modality gap.

**This identified a hole, and the hole is now filled.** This note said that
nothing in the record read a source establishing QK-norm, and called this
paper one of the earliest *at scale* — a hedge that was doing real work,
because it declined to claim the origin while the practice built on it
claimed exactly that. `SOTA-192` was filed with `source: LIT-088` and
`introduced_by: LIT-088`, so the practice asserted what the note had
carefully refused to.

The origin is Henry et al. (2020), `LIT-640`, which named the technique,
and `SOTA-192`'s `introduced_by` now points there. What this paper supplies
is unchanged and is the reason it stays in `source:`: a 22B model diverging
without the normalization and converging with it, the mechanism named, an
isolated before/after. One difference is worth carrying — Henry et al.
**replace** `1/√d` with a learnable scalar, while the formula above keeps
it. Both bound the logits; only theirs makes the softmax temperature a
trained parameter.

The two arrivals do not cite each other, and `LIT-449` is a third from
diffusion transformers that cites neither — see `SOTA-192`.
