Unit 5 of `#326`. The unit was scoped as the weakest of the six — an
unadopted theory nobody depends on. It found the largest defect of the six
instead, and the reason is a defect shape the record has not written down.

## The shape

Two documents, three years apart in publication and one field apart in
scope:

`LIT-088` (ViT-22B) says, in its Standing section:

> nothing in the record reads a source establishing QK-norm. **This is one of
> the earliest at scale.**

That is careful. "One of the earliest" declines to claim primacy; "at scale"
says what kind of earliest is meant. The note names the gap and says it is
not the thing that fills it.

`SOTA-192`, the practice built on that note, carried:

    source:
    - [LIT-088](../../record/literature.d/LIT-088.md)
    introduced_by:
    - [LIT-088](../../record/literature.d/LIT-088.md)

`introduced_by` is, by `ADR-029` and `ADR-030`, *the work that first made
this recommendation*. The practice asserted flatly what the note it cites had
declined to assert. The note was right, the practice was wrong, and they sat
in the same repository naming each other.

## Why nothing catches it

Not a lint question, and not a close call.

`source:` resolved. `introduced_by:` resolved. Both pointed at a live,
`Active` note whose subject is the same technique. The relation bound on
tags. Every structural check the record has was satisfied, because
**every structural check was satisfied by the true version too** — the
defect is not in whether the edge exists, it is in which of two papers is at
the other end.

And the evidence that it was the wrong one was not missing. It was sitting in
the prose of the document at the far end of the edge. A check that could
catch this would have to read `LIT-088`'s hedge, understand that "one of the
earliest at scale" is weaker than "first stated it", and compare that against
what `SOTA-192`'s field means. That is a reading, not a check.

## The transitive-hedge pattern

Worth naming because it generalises past this instance:

**A qualification lives in the document that makes it, and the document that
cites it inherits the subject without the qualification.** `LIT-088` hedged
about primacy. `SOTA-192` cited `LIT-088` for the technique and took primacy
with it. Nothing was misquoted; the hedge simply had no way to travel along
the edge.

Three earlier findings this session have the same shape from different
directions:

- `SOTA-097`'s title states `B ∝ C^(1/4)` and its status is `Superseded`.
  I cited it from the title. **A status is not in a title**, and the citing
  document had no way to carry it.
- Xie et al. write that Pre-LN "can train effectively without" warm-up in the
  paragraph beneath their own table showing a three-point drop. **The
  sentence propagates and the table does not.**
- `SOTA-032` stated "the representation collapse argument" with a definite
  article and no citation, having inherited the claim from prose rather than
  from a source.

In all four, the careful version exists somewhere and the citing site holds
the confident version. The record's checks operate on the edges; the
qualifications live in the bodies.

## Two process findings

**The defect was already diagnosed, in the promoted queue of the same
issue.** `#290` promoted `2010.04245` as candidate #7 and stated the finding
exactly: *"named origin of QK-norm; `LIT-088` uses it with no source for the
technique."* That was correct on 2026-09-23 and nothing acted on it.

Counted rather than assumed: **21 of `#290`'s 25 promotions are unfiled.**
That is not a failure — a ranked queue is a queue — but it changes what the
last two days of work mean. Two units have now been spent re-reading `#290`'s
*declines* to find false negatives, and this unit's largest finding was
sitting in its *promotions*, already written down. **The queue nobody worked
was a better source of findings than the declines everybody re-read.**

The next pass on `#290` should work the promoted list before re-reading the
rest of `D-cluster`. `2112.05682` (the FlashAttention antecedent, 19 held
documents), `2509.26030` (Muon, 59), `1909.11942` (ALBERT, 12), `2103.14030`
(Swin, 9) and `1910.05895` (the ScaleNorm/pre-norm origin, 14) are all
substrate defects of the shape this one turned out to be, and all five carry
their diagnosis already.

**The ranking was wrong, and the correction is not "rank better".**
`#326` put this unit fifth of six and gave a defensible reason: an unadopted
theory nobody depends on. That was accurate about the theory. What it could
not see from outside the paper is that the paper's *bibliography* pointed at
a substrate defect — ranking reads the subject, and the finding was
downstream of what the paper cited.

The practical lesson is the smaller one: a ranked worklist's tail is not
safely truncatable, because the thing that makes a paper worth reading is
sometimes not a property of the paper.

## What was left

Henry et al.'s technique has two halves and the record's usage carries one.
They `ℓ₂`-normalize queries and keys **and replace `1/√d` with a learnable
scalar**; `LIT-088` and `SOTA-192` keep `1/√d` and add the normalization in
front of it. Both bound the logits. Only one makes the softmax temperature a trained parameter — and
that parameter is the `β` `LIT-tmpqahee` measures at `[10, 25]`.

Whether the learned temperature does anything beyond the bounding is
unmeasured here and is not filed as a practice, because no paper in the
record compares the two arrangements. It is written down so the next reader
knows the record recommends a simplification of the published technique
without having said so.
