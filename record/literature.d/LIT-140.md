# LIT-140: mHC: Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections

Xie et al., DeepSeek-AI (2025; revised January 2026) — [ARXIV-2512.24880](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24880)

## Key takeaways

<!-- inactive-ok: LIT-141 — the superseded paper, named as the predecessor in the chain -->
- Hyper-Connections ([LIT-141](LIT-141.md)) expand the residual stream to several parallel
  streams and let the network learn how they mix. The paper's diagnosis is
  that the free mixing gives up the identity-mapping property a residual
  connection has by construction, and that this is what makes HC unstable
  and hard to scale.
- The fix projects the residual-mixing matrix onto a manifold that
  restores identity mapping: the matrix that carries the streams forward is
  made doubly stochastic — non-negative, rows and columns summing to one —
  by Sinkhorn-Knopp iterations, which bounds its spectral norm, and the
  pre- and post-mixing maps are kept non-negative.
- Infrastructure work keeps the wider stream affordable: reported overhead
  is 6–7% in training cost at 3B, 9B and 27B parameters, with the quality
  gains of HC retained.
