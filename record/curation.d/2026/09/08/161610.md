luria 0.12.0 lets a chain name the field its relations are asserting a
shared value in. Declared `invariant = "tags"` on both chains and read the
report once, cold.

**The practice chain had one finding and it was the one predicted.**
[SOTA-085](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-085.md) (use flash attention) `extended_by` [SOTA-161](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-161.md) (keep the attention
output in FP32, because flash attention's rounding bias compounds). An
attention technique and a stability claim, joined by a relation, with
nothing in either document naming the thing they are both about. Fixed with
a `flash-attention` secondary tag on both — narrow, true of each
independently, and it is what the relation was pointing at all along.

**The trap: the fix cannot be the other primary topic.** `primary_topic` is
`require = "exactly-one"`, so giving [SOTA-161](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-161.md) a second tag from the
vocabulary would trade one violation for another. Any remedy for an unbound
relation is therefore *either* a new vocabulary word *or* a secondary tag
outside the vocabulary — never a second topic. That constraint is what makes
the check's own framing land: it says the vocabulary is short a word, and
the schema will not let you pretend otherwise.

**The reading list had five unbound lines, and they are a worklist, not
noise.** Every LIT note carries exactly one topic and none carries a
secondary, so a lineage crossing a fault line in the twelve topics is
unbound by construction. The five:

- **Positional encoding.** RoFormer and ALiBi under `model-architecture`;
  the six papers extending them — position interpolation, YaRN, length
  generalization, RoPE-to-NoPE, SWAN-GPT, "Round and Round We Go" — under
  `attention-techniques`. This is the same fault line the practice
  vocabulary already answered with `representation-and-encoding`, found by
  hand in [ADR-021](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-021.md) before the check existed. The check found it again, from
  the other scheme, unprompted. That agreement is the reason to trust the
  rest of this list.
- **Linear attention and state space.** Mamba, Mamba-2, Mamba-3 under
  `model-architecture`; Kimi Linear, Gated DeltaNet, parallel DeltaNet under
  `attention-techniques`. Whether a selective SSM is an architecture or an
  attention technique is a real question the vocabulary declines to answer.
- **Mixture of experts.** GShard under `distributed-optimization`; Sparsely-
  Gated MoE, Switch, DeepSeekMoE under `model-architecture`. GShard is
  genuinely both, and the topic it holds is the half its neighbours do not.
- **Activations.** GLU variants and gated convnets under
  `model-architecture`; PowLU under `model-stability`.
- **Fine-tuning objectives.** DeepSeekMath under `adaptation-and-tuning`;
  evolution strategies for LLM fine-tuning under `training-optimization`.

Left as findings. The remedy for each is a vocabulary judgement, and [#85](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/85) is
already the reading that would make it — pre-empting it here would be
retagging twenty-three inherited notes on the strength of a report skimmed
in one sitting.
