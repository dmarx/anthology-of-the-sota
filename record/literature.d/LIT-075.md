# LIT-075: Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models

Karras et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2206.00364](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364)

## Key takeaways

- Argues the theory and practice of diffusion models are "unnecessarily
  convoluted", and presents a **design space that separates the concrete
  design choices** rather than a new model
- Improves three of those choices independently: the **sampler**, the
  **preconditioning of the score network**, and the **distribution of
  training noise levels**
- The preconditioning is **derived, not tuned**. Writing the denoiser as
  `D(x;σ) = c_skip·x + c_out·F(c_in·x; c_noise)`, the paper solves for `c_in`
  and `c_out` by requiring unit-variance inputs and targets, for `c_skip` by
  minimising the amplification of the network's own error, and fits `c_noise`
  empirically — the only one of the four with no derivation
- **FID 1.79** class-conditional and **1.97** unconditional on CIFAR-10, at
  **35 network evaluations per image**
- **Modularity is the evidence for the framing**: a *previously trained*
  ImageNet-64 model goes from FID 2.07 to 1.55 by design changes alone, and
  to 1.36 after retraining. Most of the gain arrives without retraining
- Stochastic sampling helps the ImageNet-64 model and *hurts* CIFAR-10 once
  the network is trained under the paper's own improvements — the authors
  read it as compensating for model error

## Measured against it

EDM is the baseline or the teacher for most of the diffusion work the record
holds since:

- **Kingma and Gao ([LIT-692](LIT-692.md))** derive EDM's implied weighting,
  `N(λ; 2.4, 2.4²)·(e^{−λ} + 0.25)`, which is not monotone. They re-implement
  EDM at ImageNet-64 and tie it: 1.45 for their reproduction, 1.43 for their
  best monotone weighting. Neither reaches this paper's published 1.36.
- **EDM2 ([LIT-714](LIT-714.md))**, from the same group, re-runs EDM as its baseline. On
  ImageNet-512 latents config A scores 8.00 and the final magnitude-preserving
  config 2.56. In ImageNet-64 pixel space, EDM's 2.22 deterministic becomes
  1.58 at matched complexity. It also finds that EDM's loss weight equalises
  the noise levels only at initialization.
- **Min-SNR ([LIT-710](LIT-710.md))** multiplies EDM's loss by `min(SNR, 5)/SNR` and
  converges faster over the first 200M training images. It stops there for
  compute and does not reach EDM's full schedule.
- **DMD ([LIT-643](LIT-643.md)) and DMD2 ([LIT-646](LIT-646.md))** distil the ImageNet-64 EDM model into
  one step. DMD scores 2.62 against EDM's 2.32 at 512 passes with the ODE
  sampler. DMD2 scores 1.51, which beats the ODE teacher, and 1.28 with
  longer training, the only run that also beats EDM's stochastic 1.36.
- **DMAD ([LIT-tmpv2jpm](LIT-tmpv2jpm.md))** distils the same model into one step with
  discriminator heads in place of DMD's fake score. It reports 1.24, and
  1.04 with an ImageNet-pretrained projected discriminator, after 570K
  iterations at batch 384, a longer budget than DMD2's 1.51.
- **Warm Diffusion ([LIT-555](LIT-555.md))** keeps EDM's network, training and Heun sampler
  and adds blur. Its blur-noise ratio 0 is pure-noise EDM at 1.97 on CIFAR-10,
  and the best ratio, 0.5, gives 1.85. That is a 0.12 gain, best of three
  sampling rounds and with no variance.

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-188](../practices.d/SOTA-188.md), and **confirmed** by a full reading —
[NOTE-019](../notes.d/NOTE-019.md), Section 5 and Appendix B.6.

The reading was `Skimmed` first: ar5iv has no rendering for this identifier,
so the abstract was all that was available through the route the rest of
[#114](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/114) used, and [SOTA-188](../practices.d/SOTA-188.md) was left visibly unverified rather than
quietly assumed. dmarx supplied the full text; the note is now `Read` and the
practice checks out, with one correction — [SOTA-188](../practices.d/SOTA-188.md) had implied all four
preconditioning scalings follow from the unit-variance requirement, and two
of them follow from other criteria.

That round trip is what the `Skimmed` status is for. The gap was recorded
where the next reader would find it, and it was the note's own open question
that got closed.
