The previous entry ended with four follow-ups: Porian et al., Lin et al.,
Marigold, and Kingma and Gao. All four are now filed and read in full. Each
turned out to be a paper the record had been citing *through* another
paper. In three of the four, reading the origin changed what the citing
documents said.

## The pattern: second-hand citations drift in a consistent direction

- **Lin et al. was called the origin of trailing spacing.** `SOTA-416` said so
  and so did the previous entry. Lin credits the discretization to
  DPM-Solver. What Lin originates is the rule ("sample steps should always
  include the last timestep") and the leading, linspace and trailing names.
  "Slight improvements for image generation", which `SOTA-416` attributed to
  Lin, is Garcia et al.'s word. Lin never measures spacing alone.
- **Pearce and Song called counting "much of" the Kaplan–Chinchilla gap.**
  Porian's stepwise table makes it a plurality, 0.129 of 0.338, with warmup
  and per-size tuning each about as large. And `LIT-688`'s trap described
  Porian's steps as a chain through the decay row, when the tuned row uses a
  constant rate and decay is a side branch.
- **"Chinchilla identified the schedule" survived in three more places.**
  `LIT-028` was fixed last time, but `NOTE-017` and `NOTE-072` waited for
  Porian. Filing it refines the correction itself. Chinchilla's hypothesis
  covered the whole schedule. The *decay* half moves the exponent by 0.03.
  The *warmup* half is one of three real causes.
- **Kingma and Gao's identity has a scope the blog post dropped.** Flow
  matching's weighting equals v-MSE's under the *unshifted* cosine. The
  paper's own App. D.2.1 claims the shift does not matter, and it is wrong: a
  resolution shift reweights toward high noise by up to `(d/64)²`.
  `THEORY-106` now carries the scope.

Every one of these errors moves toward a simpler story than the origin
tells. That is `DP-010` again, applied to secondary sources. A paper quoting
another paper keeps the conclusion and drops the qualifier.

## Two statuses moved, each on its own `promote_when`

- **`SOTA-416` (trailing spacing) is now `Active`.** Its condition was "Lin et
  al. is filed and its own leading-against-trailing comparison is read next to
  this one." The reading found no contradiction. Lin's comparison is one
  same-seed image, not a number. The record's case is Garcia's single-variable
  measurement plus Marigold's own step curve. That curve was measured under
  leading spacing by authors who did not know it, and gives about 34% NYUv2
  AbsRel at one step on weights that reach 5.7 under trailing.
- **`SOTA-413` (count the head) is now `Active`** on its second clause. Its first
  clause, a head-to-head extrapolation test of the two counting conventions,
  is still unmet and still stated.

## One relation withdrawn, again on the lint's advice

Guidance rescale was filed with `compared_against: SOTA-202` (dynamic
thresholding). The lint reported the practice line sharing no topic. Reading
it again, nobody ran that comparison: Lin cites thresholding as
inspiration. `compared_against` says "somebody ran the comparison", so the
relation was wrong, and it is now prose. This is the second time in a day
that an unbound-line report found a relation stronger than its evidence.
