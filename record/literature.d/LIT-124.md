# LIT-124: Efficient Training of Language Models to Fill in the Middle

Bavarian et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2207.14255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14255)

## Key takeaways

- A data transformation, not an architecture: cut a document into prefix,
  middle and suffix, move the middle to the end with sentinel tokens, and
  the model learns to infill from the surrounding context.
- *FIM-for-free*: transforming a large fraction of the training data does
  not harm left-to-right perplexity or sampling quality across a wide range
  of scales, so infilling is an added capability rather than a trade.
- Ablations over the knobs that matter: the transformation rate, PSM
  (prefix-suffix-middle) against SPM ordering, and how the span is chosen —
  character-level random spans rather than token or line boundaries — plus
  applying the transform at the context level rather than the document
  level.
- The recommendation is to train autoregressive models with FIM by default.
