# LIT-351: SWARM Parallelism: Training Large Models Can Be Surprisingly Communication-Efficient

Ryabinin et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2301.11913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11913)

## What it is

SWARM parallelism: pipeline stages over unreliable heterogeneous workers
connected by slow links, rebalanced as workers come and go.

From the paper's own abstract:

> Many deep learning applications benefit from using large models with
> billions of parameters. Training these models is notoriously expensive
> due to the need for specialized HPC clusters. In this work, we consider
> alternative setups for training large models: using cheap "preemptible"
> instances or pooling existing resources from multiple regions.
