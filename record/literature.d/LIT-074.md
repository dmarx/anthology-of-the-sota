# LIT-074: FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness

Dao et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2205.14135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135)

## Key takeaways

- **The bottleneck is HBM traffic, not FLOPs.** Approximate-attention methods
  reduced compute complexity and often failed to produce a wall-clock
  speedup, because the cost model they optimised was the wrong one
- **Tiling to on-chip SRAM**, with an online softmax carrying a running max
  and normaliser, computes **exact** attention without materialising the
  `N × N` matrix
- **Theorem 1**: `O(N²d)` FLOPs — unchanged — and **`O(N)` additional memory**
- **Theorem 2**: standard attention needs `Θ(Nd + N²)` HBM accesses;
  FlashAttention needs `Θ(N²d²M⁻¹)`, for `d ≤ M ≤ Nd`. With `d ∈ [64,128]`
  and `M ≈ 100KB`, `d² ≪ M`
- **Proposition 3**: no algorithm computes exact attention in `o(N²d²M⁻¹)`
  HBM accesses across all `M ∈ [d, Nd]`. It is IO-optimal, not merely faster
- **Block sizes are derived, not tuned**: `B_c = ⌈M/4d⌉`,
  `B_r = min(⌈M/4d⌉, d)`
- **Backward recomputation** rather than storing the attention matrix —
  trading arithmetic for memory traffic, which is the right way round given
  Theorem 2
- 15% over the MLPerf 1.1 BERT-large record, **3× on GPT-2**, 2.4× on LRA;
  and the first Transformers above chance on Path-X (16K) and Path-256 (64K)

## What came before it

This note named no antecedent, and there are two.

**Rabe and Staats (2021), `LIT-tmp2jjuh`** — six months earlier, the same
exactness and the same avoidance of the `N × N` matrix, with `O(1)` memory per
query and a practical `O(√n)` implementation giving **59× less memory
overhead at length 16,384**. This paper's v3 acknowledges them the other way
round too: they credit Dao et al. with showing *"that the reduced memory
requirements can translate to significant speedups on GPUs."*

**Jang et al. (2019)**, unheld here — "lazy softmax", the identity that lets
the denominator be deferred. Rabe and Staats say in as many words that their
own Equation 1 is *"a rediscovery"* of it, while noting that paper discusses
neither memory complexity, numerical stability, nor backpropagation.

**What this paper contributes is unchanged, and it is the systems half.**
IO-awareness as the framing, Theorem 2's HBM-access bound, Proposition 3's
IO-optimality, derived block sizes, and a CUDA kernel. The 3× on GPT-2 is a
GPU number, and the antecedent measures the same algorithm on TPU at within a
few percent of standard attention — which is the condition `SOTA-085` now
carries.
