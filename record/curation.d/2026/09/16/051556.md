luria 0.23.0 made it possible to assert a shared field across a scheme
boundary. This record has six relations that cross one, so I measured all six
and declared `invariant: tags` on the one that earned it — `NOTE.paper`, 4
findings in 158 edges against 20-47% for the other five. The argument for
picking it was that a note and its paper are **the same object**, where the
others join two things this record files on different axes on purpose.

Review:

> given that a note and its paper are the same object, rather than specifying
> an invariant (an "at least one" similarity), I feel like it would be more
> appropriate to use a derived field.

That is my own premise, carried one step further than I carried it. If the
two are the same object, the note has no subject of its own to agree or
disagree about — it should *derive* the paper's, the way `NOTE.published`
already derived the paper's date for exactly this reason. I had the argument
and stopped at the weaker mechanism: reporting a disagreement that should not
have been representable.

The tell was available and I did not read it. `NOTE.published` was sitting
four lines above `NOTE.tags` in the config, doing the thing, with a comment
saying why.

## What it cost to find out, and what it bought

Building it turned up a luria bug worth more than the change: `derive` refused
`many` on any field, and dropping `many` to satisfy the refusal made the
derived list read as *no values at all*, which deleted all fourteen
`docs/notes/tags/*.md` pages with nothing failing. [LU-#276](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/issues/276), fixed in 0.24.0 —
found because this record was the first to want a derived list.

## The part the redesign forced

Four notes disagreed with their papers, and I had deliberately left them
standing: retagging a paper inside the commit that turns a check on is [#101](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/101)'s
mistake with better manners.

Deriving changes that. The note now adopts the paper's tag, so the
disagreement does not become a finding to work through later — it becomes
invisible. And two of the four were the only reason anyone noticed the
*paper* was wrong: `LIT-024` filing multi-query attention under
`model-architecture`, `LIT-112` filing vLLM under `attention-techniques`.
Under the invariant they could wait. Under the derivation they could not, so
they went in the same contribution.

Worth keeping: **a decision about mechanism can change what counts as scope.**
"Leave the findings standing" was right for the check and wrong for the
derivation, and nothing about the four documents changed in between.

## What the record gives up

A note can no longer say its paper is filed wrong. It was never the note's
job, it was doing it by accident, and it was the only mechanism the record had
for noticing — four in 158, all four real. What comes back is that retagging a
paper now moves its reading for free, so fixing one costs less than noticing
one used to.
