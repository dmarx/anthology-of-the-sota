### Added

- **The dropout cluster is complete.** `LIT-tmppkthl` (Li et al. 2018, variance
  shift), `LIT-tmpffawr` (Gal and Ghahramani 2015, the Bayesian reading) and
  `LIT-tmpr4xyx` (Hara et al. 2017) close the three papers [#169](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/169) named.
  `THEORY-tmpr6ead` and `THEORY-tmpre9uh` state the two accounts that were
  new; Hara et al. added neither and its note says so, which was the question
  it was filed to answer.
- `SOTA-tmpjjsir` (`Active`, `converged`) — do not place dropout before a
  batch-normalization layer. The record's first practice about an *interaction*
  between two practices. The mechanism predicts its own escape routes and both
  hold: a small rate, and a wide layer — Wide ResNet beats its baseline at 0.3
  and 0.5 where narrower networks do not.
- `LIT-tmpn2vpg` (Liu et al. 2025) — **the experiment `SOTA-240` said nobody
  had run.** Single-epoch pretraining of BERT and Pythia 160M/1.4B at varying
  dropout rates: every capability measure is worse with dropout than without,
  monotonically in the rate, with "early dropout" no better and editability
  worse.
- `LIT-tmp0qipg` (Zuhri et al. 2025) — softpick. Drops the sum-to-one
  constraint outright and gets a 0.00% sink rate at both 340M and 1.8B, with
  kurtosis from 33511 to 341 and attention matrices 95-99% exact zeros — and a
  model that is worse at 1.8B on every benchmark. The mechanism confirmed and
  the remedy failing, in one paper.

### Changed

- **`SOTA-240`'s open question is closed.** The practice said that reading
  Srivastava §7.4's right edge forward to large-scale pretraining was "the
  anthology reading a 2014 curve forward rather than a claim anybody has
  checked at scale". `LIT-tmpn2vpg` checked it and the answer is stronger than
  the inference: in the single-epoch regime dropout is not merely unnecessary
  against a 2-3x training cost, it is a cost on both axes. The hedge is gone,
  the paper is a source, and the `consensus_note` no longer claims nobody has
  looked.
- `SOTA-240` also now points at `SOTA-tmpjjsir` and says the two answers must
  not be merged: this practice is about the model-to-data ratio, that one is
  about layer ordering, and dropout left convolutional architectures for the
  second reason rather than the first.
- `THEORY-tmp1rmwn` gains `LIT-tmp0qipg` as a source. Removing the
  normalization removes the sink completely, which is the most direct test of
  the account available — and the same paper shows the sink-free model is the
  worse model at 1.8B, which is why the document's claim that sinks are not a
  defect is now evidenced rather than argued.
