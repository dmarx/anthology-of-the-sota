# LIT-137: Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule

Yang et al., NVIDIA and MIT (2024; ICLR 2025) — [ARXIV-2412.06464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464)

## Key takeaways

- Two mechanisms had been explored separately for linear transformers: a
  gate for adaptive memory control (Mamba2) and the delta update rule for
  targeted memory modification (DeltaNet). They are complementary — gating
  lets the state forget quickly, the delta rule lets it overwrite one
  association without disturbing the rest — and the gated delta rule
  combines them in one recurrence.
- A parallel, chunkwise training algorithm makes the rule efficient on
  modern accelerators, which the plain delta rule had not been.
- At 1.3B parameters on 100B tokens, Gated DeltaNet consistently beats
  Mamba2 and DeltaNet on language modelling, commonsense reasoning,
  in-context retrieval, length extrapolation and long-context understanding.
- Two hybrids, GatedDeltaNet-H1 and -H2, interleave the layer with
  sliding-window attention or Mamba2 layers and improve on the pure form —
  the seed of the layer-interleaved hybrids that followed.

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-135](../practices.d/SOTA-135.md) and the module three layers in four of the Qwen
line are built from ([LIT-136](LIT-136.md), [LIT-135](LIT-135.md)). Kimi Delta Attention ([LIT-133](LIT-133.md))
extends it with a channel-wise forgetting gate; the sequence of
linear-attention recurrences in the record now reads Mamba2 and DeltaNet →
this → KDA.

The recurrence is `S_t = S_{t−1}·α_t(I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ`. From
Mamba2 ([LIT-162](LIT-162.md)) it takes the data-dependent decay `α_t`, with Mamba2's
parameterization, which can clear the state quickly but decays every
association at the same rate. From DeltaNet ([LIT-195](LIT-195.md)) it takes the delta
update, which overwrites the value stored under one key, and the WY-based
chunkwise parallel algorithm. The paper extends that algorithm to carry the
gate. It then beats both parents at 1.3B / 100B tokens. In its
single-needle tests Mamba2 degrades past 2K tokens because it forgets too
fast, and DeltaNet degrades at longer lengths on the real-essay needle tests
because it clears memory poorly.

Two 2026 papers build on it. Gated DeltaNet-2 ([LIT-tmpsiw5k](LIT-tmpsiw5k.md)), from two of
its three authors, splits β into a channel-wise erase gate and a
channel-wise write gate on top of KDA's channel-wise decay, and reduces to
this recurrence when all three are tied to scalars. It retrains this model as
a baseline under one 1.3B / 100B-token recipe. Gated DeltaNet averages 52.07
against 53.11, but it still holds the word-needle task at 4K, 60.6 against
31.8. Olmo Hybrid ([LIT-tmpax1wi](LIT-tmpax1wi.md)) uses the recurrence with the
negative-eigenvalue extension (β → 2β) for three layers in four of a 7B
model trained to 6T tokens. In its ablations from 60M to 1B, Gated DeltaNet
beats Mamba-2 at every scale, as a pure model and in a 3:1 hybrid.
