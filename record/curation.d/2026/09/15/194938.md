Two entries ago this record prescribed a fix for the gap that let it file a
reply before the claim it answers: **after filing a paper, list its references
that the record lacks.** The next entry observed that the prescription had not
been carried out and called that "its own small demonstration that a process
note in a journal entry is not a process." Here it is, run.

## The query, stated so the next person can run it again

For each of the twelve papers in the evolution-strategies line, parse the
bibliography out of the arXiv HTML (`<li id="bib.bibNN">` list items),
extract every arXiv identifier appearing in each entry, and difference the
union against every `arxiv:` field in `record/literature.d/`.

- **532 bibliography entries** across the twelve.
- **144 distinct arXiv identifiers** extracted.
- **227 arXiv identifiers** currently in the record.
- **117 of the 144 are not in the record.**

No script is committed for this. The project removed its helper scripts
deliberately ([#41](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/41)) and `pyproject.toml` says what remains is the frozen
migration; a sweep that runs four times a year does not earn a maintained
artifact. The paragraph above is the artifact.

## What the query cannot see, which is most of it

**Only 45% of the bibliography entries carry an arXiv identifier at all** —
245 of 532 did; the rest are journal and conference references. Everything
classical in this line is therefore invisible to this sweep: Rechenberg (1973),
Schwefel (1977), Hansen and Ostermeier (2001) on CMA-ES, McCloskey and Cohen
(1989) on catastrophic forgetting, Kirkpatrick et al. (2017) on elastic weight
consolidation. Several are cited by four or more of the twelve.

This is [DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4) operating on the pass that was written to fix an instance of
[DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4). The complement of "references carrying an arXiv id" is not empty,
and running the filter harder does not sample it. **The honest statement is
that this sweep found what an arXiv-shaped query can find**, and a second pass
keyed to author-year strings would return a different and older set.

## The headline, and it is worse than the gap that prompted the sweep

**`ARXIV-1703.03864` — Salimans, Ho, Chen, Sidor and Sutskever, *Evolution
Strategies as a Scalable Alternative to Reinforcement Learning* — is cited by
ten of the twelve papers and is not in this record.**

The record holds nine papers descending from it, a practice recommending the
technique it introduced ([SOTA-154](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-154.md)), two theory documents explaining why
that technique works, and not the paper. This is exactly [DP-007](../design-principles.md#dp-7)'s "trunk with
no document": every fork in the line has an entry because every fork was
somebody's contribution, and the thing all ten take for granted has none.

**`ARXIV-1707.06347` — PPO — is cited by six of the twelve and is also
absent.** That gap was noticed by hand two passes ago and written into a
curation entry as a suspected instance. The sweep confirms it and puts a
number on it.

## What this changes about how passes should run

The sweep took minutes and the gap it found is the largest single omission in
this line. Two things follow.

**Run it at filing time, not as a separate pass.** The reason it kept not
happening is that it was written down as a future task rather than as part of
what filing a paper means. A note in a journal entry is a reminder; a step in
the filing procedure is a process. This entry is still only the former.

**Count the citations rather than reading them.** The useful output was not
the 117-item list, which nobody will act on, but the ranking. *Cited by ten of
twelve and absent* is a finding; *cited once and absent* is a reading list.
The ranking is what turned a dump into the Salimans result.

## What this pass did not do

It filed nothing. Every entry above is a candidate, and the top two —
Salimans et al. and PPO — are the ones the record should not go another pass
without, on [DP-007](../design-principles.md#dp-7)'s argument rather than on any judgement about their
quality.
