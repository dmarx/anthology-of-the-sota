# LIT-115: Monarch Mixer: A Simple Sub-Quadratic GEMM-Based Architecture

Fu et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2310.12109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12109)

## Key takeaways

- Transformers scale quadratically along **two** axes — sequence length *and*
  model dimension — and almost all efficiency work addresses only the first,
  because it replaces the sequence mixer and leaves the MLP alone
- **Monarch matrices** are one sub-quadratic primitive for both: expressive
  enough to capture many linear transforms including the DFT, and
  decomposable into **GEMMs**, so they reach high FLOP utilisation where
  attention — being memory-bound — cannot, even under FlashAttention
- **Attention-free, not hybrid.** M2 replaces attention; the paper's own
  reading of its causal result is that "radically different architectures than
  Transformers may be performant on causal language modeling"
- **BERT-style**: matches BERT-base and BERT-large on GLUE with up to **27%
  fewer parameters** and up to **9.1× throughput at sequence length 4K**
- **ImageNet**: M2-ViT beats ViT-b by **1% at half the parameters**
- **Causality is the hard case**: enforcing it by masking reintroduces a
  quadratic bottleneck, so the paper develops a multivariate-polynomial view
  of Monarch matrices to parameterise causality directly
- Proof of concept, by the authors' own framing — BERT-large is the largest
  model here
