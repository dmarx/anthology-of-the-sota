# LIT-026: Advances and Open Problems in Federated Learning

Kairouz et al. (2019) — [ARXIV-1912.04977](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04977)

## Key takeaways

- Defines federated learning as a field and, most usefully, splits it in two:
  **cross-device** (very many unreliable clients with little data each, mostly
  unavailable, never individually addressable) versus **cross-silo** (few
  reliable organisational participants with a lot of data, always available,
  individually identifiable)
- Almost every practical concern differs between them — client sampling,
  communication cost, dropout handling, the threat model, whether a participant
  can be held to an agreement — and the survey contrasts both against
  traditional single-datacenter distributed learning
- Addresses **cross-device by default**, "though many of the problems apply to
  other FL settings"
- Describes the field as already deployed rather than prospective, and catalogues
  open problems across privacy, security, system design, fairness and efficiency
