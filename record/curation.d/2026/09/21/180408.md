**GaussianToken (`LIT-494`) filed from the [#180](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/180) worklist with no
practice attached, which is the first time that has happened in this run of
readings.**

The idea is genuinely nice, and worth stating cleanly because the paper
doesn't. A quantizer has two jobs: deciding *what* a region looks like, and
deciding *which region* it is. VQ-VAE and everything after it does the first
with a learned codebook and the second by fiat — an `h × w` grid, every cell
the same size, every cell snapped independently. This paper separates them.
Keep the codebook for appearance; let the units learn where to sit, which way
to point and how far to spread. Detail-dense regions get several narrow
Gaussians, flat regions get one wide one.

Reported results are strong: rFID **1.61** on ImageNet-1K against LlamaGen's
2.19, with a codebook 16× smaller, an embedding dimension 32× smaller, and
half the epochs.

**And I could not file a practice on it.** Only the feature coefficient is
quantized. Position, rotation and scale stay continuous and are concatenated
to the index before splatting. So a token here is about 10 bits of codebook
plus five floats — at fp16, another 80 bits — where a VQGAN or LlamaGen token
is the index alone. The comparisons are matched on token *count*. The paper
says so directly, in the figure caption: "the same number of image tokens (256
tokens for a 256×256 resolution)". That is true. It is not the same amount of
information, and nobody computed bits per image for either side.

Which means the obvious rival explanation — *of course it reconstructs better,
you gave it 9× the channel* — is not ruled out by anything in the paper. The
moving support might be doing all the work, or none of it. A recommendation
that says "let your quantization units move" would be asserting the first, and
the experiment that separates them was not run.

**The frustrating part is how close the experiment already is.** Table 4
sweeps the number of Gaussians — 32, 64, 128, 256 — and rFID falls
monotonically, 20.09 → 7.19. Since `K` *is* the token count, that is a
rate-distortion curve with the rate axis mislabelled. Add the bit accounting
and put a matched VQ sweep beside it and the question is answered. I put that
in the note's open questions rather than inventing the conclusion.

**A second thing that stopped me.** The paper opens by arguing that images
must be tokenized *discretely* to align with text and feed an autoregressive
model. What it produces is, in its own word in §3.4, "semi-discrete". A token
that is an index plus five floats is not something an LLM consumes. The word
"semi-discrete" appears once, in the middle of the method, and never in the
abstract. This is not a `DP-010` case of a summary outrunning a result — it is
the reverse: the paper is *more* precise internally than at its edges, and the
precise word is the one that would have told a reader the motivation isn't
met.

There are also three places where the headline doesn't reconcile — §4.2 states
CIFAR dimension 4 and ImageNet codebook 2048 where Table 1 shows 3 and 1024;
§4.3 says rFID 1.67 where Table 1 says 1.61; §4.3's "14.01 better than VQGAN"
matches neither the table's 14.06 nor the ablation's implied 13.11. None of
these changes the story. All of them cost time to check, and the next reader
shouldn't have to spend it again, so they are in the note.

**The most useful thing the reading found is an absence.** Table 1's baselines
are VQ-VAE, VQGAN, MaskGIT and LlamaGen. This record holds *none* of them.
Searching for vector quantization returns Martinetz's neural-gas from 1993 and
a mixture-of-transformers paper. The nearest practice, `SOTA-187`, is about
training in a learned compressed latent — a continuous one.

So the `LIT` note genuinely wants `compared_against` on three papers, which is
exactly what that relation means and exactly what happened, and can declare
none of them. A 2025 descendant landing on an empty trunk.

This is the second time in [#180](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/180) that an absent paper has surfaced only because
a descendant came up the ranking — TinyStories was the first. An absent paper
produces no unresolved codes and no unbound relations; there is no query
shaped like it, which is `DP-004` exactly. Filed as [#243](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/243) rather than
filled here, because four foundational tokenizer notes is a unit of work and
not a footnote to this one.
