# LIT-131: Kimi K3: Open Frontier Intelligence

Kimi Team, Moonshot AI (2026) — [ARXIV-2607.24653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653)

## Key takeaways

- 2.8T total and 104B activated parameters, 16 of 896 routed experts per
  token under what the report calls Stable LatentMoE, native visual
  understanding, and a 1,048,576-token context window. Weights released
  alongside the report.
- The attention stack is a layerwise hybrid: 69 Kimi Delta Attention layers
  ([LIT-133](LIT-133.md)) and 24 gated Multi-Head Latent Attention layers — about the
  3:1 ratio Kimi Linear settled on — so most layers carry a finite-state
  linear-attention memory and a minority keep global attention with a
  compressed KV cache.
- Attention Residuals ([LIT-134](LIT-134.md)) replace the fixed residual sum with learned
  attention over all preceding layers' representations.
- The headline claim is about the *rate* rather than the size: roughly
  2.5× the scaling efficiency of Kimi K2 ([LIT-132](LIT-132.md)) at matched compute,
  attributed to the three architectural changes together with refined
  training and data recipes.
- Optimization continues the Muon line: MuonClip from K2, with a per-head
  variant of Muon reported for the attention projections.
- Long context is reached by extending the window progressively during
  training rather than at once ([SOTA-139](../practices.d/SOTA-139.md)), and by synthesising tasks that can only be
  solved by attending across the whole million-token window.
- A multi-token-prediction layer shaped like a backbone block is trained
  with the model and later fine-tuned into an EAGLE-3-style single-layer
  draft model for speculative decoding.
