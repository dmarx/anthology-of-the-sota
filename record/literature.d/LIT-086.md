# LIT-086: K-Planes: Explicit Radiance Fields in Space, Time, and Appearance

Fridovich-Keil et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2301.10241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10241)

## Key takeaways

- Hybrid representation scheme
- Temporal coherence
- Efficient rendering
- Appearance modeling

## Standing in the anthology

**Carries no practice, and this is a reading rather than an assumption.**

A hybrid explicit representation for dynamic radiance fields. The claim is about how to factorize a signal over space, time and appearance so that an explicit representation stays tractable. **No practice yet because the vocabulary has no home for representation choices**, not because the claim is out of bounds — the same gap that leaves [LIT-064](LIT-064.md) unfiled.
