**Audit of the `LIT` documents no practice sources ([#121](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/121)).** 70 of 224 at the
start. The useful number turned out not to be 70.

## The criterion already existed

The audit needed a test for whether a note *should* be a source, and `ADR-017`
had already written one for a different purpose: **if this paper were retracted
tomorrow, would the practice need rewriting?** That decision was filed to settle
whether adopters belong in `source:`. It answers this question too, and using it
meant the audit did not invent a private rule in a commit message — which is the
specific failure `ADR-017` records having cost thirteen practices.

## What 70 decomposed into

- **12 retired or tombstoned.** Four are deduplication tombstones with the
  survivor named in `superseded_by` — `LIT-006`/`LIT-042` (SGDR),
  `LIT-029`/`LIT-114` (Pre-LN), `LIT-105`/`LIT-090` (Lion). For these the status
  *is* the reason and nothing needed writing.
- **10 cited in a practice's body but not in its `source:`.** This looked like
  the richest vein and was almost entirely a false alarm: adoption evidence and
  lineage, which `ADR-017` explicitly routes to `consensus_note` instead. The
  discipline was being followed. **One** was a real miss — see below.
- **~25 already stating why they were unsourced**, several of them well. The
  model was `LIT-110`: "Nothing in the record cites this note", followed by the
  reason.
- **24 with a Standing section that said nothing about linkage at all.** This
  was the working set, and it is where the finding is.

## The finding

A note that sources no practice is not a defect. A note that sources no
practice *and does not say why* is, and for the reason `DP-008` gives: nobody
re-reads a vocabulary, or an absence. The silence is indistinguishable from
nobody having got round to it, so every future audit pays the full cost of
finding out again.

That is what made this issue worth doing at the size it was done. Counting
unsourced notes measures nothing; **asking each one to state its own standing
is what converts a backlog into a record.**

## Two gaps the silence was hiding

**Diffusion sampling.** The record held four diffusion practices — `SOTA-187`,
`SOTA-188`, `SOTA-195`, `SOTA-202` — every one about training or
parametrization, and three unsourced papers — DDIM, DPM-Solver, Consistency
Models — every one about sampling. The record said how to train a diffusion
model and nothing about how to sample from one, while holding the three papers
that decided how everyone samples. Four practices filed.

**A convergence three readings had already noticed.** `vision-and-graphics`
held thirteen notes and zero practices. Under `ADR-026`'s filing rule an empty
domain topic is not by itself a gap — a claim merely discovered in a domain
takes its kind. But `NOTE-037` calls K-Planes "the third explicit-structure
replacement for a large coordinate network", `NOTE-042` names the same pairing
from the other side, and `NOTE-046` records NeuS2 inheriting the line. Four
papers, three mechanically unrelated structures, one conclusion, inside
eighteen months. **The convergence was stated three times in the reading list
and was invisible in the registry.** One practice, four sources.

Both had the same shape: the readings had done the work and the registry had
not been told.

## Why they were unfiled, and it is not what it looks like

Every one of those seven papers was read in the pass that deliberately filed
nothing, and all three sampling readings end with the same sentence — *"Nothing
is sourced to this paper and this reading files no practice."* That is a
**pass policy**, and a year later it reads as a judgement about the paper.

Worth stating as a rule: **a deliberate deferral must say it is one.** "Nothing
is filed" and "nothing should be filed" are different claims and were written
the same way.

## The one real link fix

`SOTA-051` had been arguing from `LIT-089`'s zero convolutions for its sharpest
content — *zero is not "near zero"* — without naming it in `source:`. Under the
retraction test that section does not survive the paper's removal. The link
existed in prose and not in data, which is the inverse of the failure the last
three contributions kept hitting, and equally invisible to the lint.

## What the audit found and did not fix

`LIT-004` — the gradient checkpointing paper — is a **generic-bullet note that
the bullet-signature pass missed**, because that pass selected on a signature
and a document can be equally empty without matching it. It sits on a gap: the
record recommends recomputation only for attention (`SOTA-087`) and has no
general activation-memory practice among its twenty-nine
`distributed-optimization` entries. Nothing was filed from it. Filing a practice
from four bullets naming the category is the failure two passes have been spent
correcting, and the fix is a reading, not a guess.

Seven notes are unsourced because **nobody has read them** — three of them a
coherent joint-embedding cluster. That is a different finding from a paper read
and declined, and the determinations say so rather than implying an assessment
that never happened.

## Afterwards

Every unsourced note now either states its standing or is retired, where the
status is the reason. 62 remain unsourced, down from 70, and the number is no
longer the interesting thing about them.
