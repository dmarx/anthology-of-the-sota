Asked to extend the vocabulary, the temptation is to name every cluster you
can see. The measurements say otherwise: eight candidates, two passed.

## The instrument worked in the negative direction, which is why to trust it

`ADR-050` set the test: count the documents on a candidate subject and measure
how concentrated their primary topics are. A subject the vocabulary can say
lands mostly in one topic; a subject it cannot scatters.

The best evidence that this test is doing something was **the physics of
learning**. 28 record documents mention mean-field analysis, free energy,
singular learning theory, random matrix theory, phase transitions. It looks
like a subject, it has a literature, and a vocabulary designer would reach
for it.

Seventeen of the twenty-eight — 61% — already take `analysis-and-evaluation`
as their primary topic.

That is the test returning "no gap", loudly, on a candidate that passed the
eyeball check. Compare numerics, which `ADR-050` added: 48 documents across
nine topics with none holding a quarter.

## Three homonyms again, and one of them is funny

`ADR-050` was inflated three times by homonyms — "alignment" returned 42
documents of which 2 were about safety, the rest gradient alignment. The same
thing happened here, three more times:

- **"policy"** — the sociotechnical probe returned 29, of which 5 were
  reinforcement learning. Policy gradients, not public policy.
- **"multimodal"** — the record probe returned 23, of which about 9 were about
  modalities. The rest were **multimodal distributions**: Kuramoto mean-field
  analysis, consensus-based sampling, stochastic gradient Langevin dynamics.
- **"harmful" and "misuse"** — the safety probe returned 20, of which about 3
  were about safety, and **five of the false positives were quantization
  papers**. A 1-bit LLM paper discussing the harm of aggressive rounding reads,
  to a regex, exactly like an alignment paper.

Every one of these was caught by reading the hits and none by the count. That
is now six documented instances across two passes, and it is starting to look
less like a caution and more like the normal case.

## The two that passed are different kinds of thing

**`multimodal-learning` is a naming gap**, and it is `ADR-050`'s numerics
story again almost word for word. `model-architecture`'s blurb ended with
"multi-modal designs" — a trailing clause, on a topic about the shape of a
network, carrying a subject about how many kinds of signal go into it. Nine
documents across six topics. Thirty-five more queued.

The clause is the tell. A subject that appears as the last item in somebody
else's list is not filed; it is parked.

**`deployment-and-society` is a scope extension**, and it is the only term in
the vocabulary that named nothing on the day it was added. One probe across
749 record documents returned one hit, and the hit was a homonym.

That is a weak position and it was taken deliberately. The incoming list holds
twenty-four such papers, led by its **single highest-revisit entry**, and the
last eleven contributions each worked down past it. `DP-009` is exact about
what that is: declining a document because no category fits is the cheaper
reading, requires no work, and is self-confirming — turn the document away and
the vocabulary keeps looking complete, because the evidence that would have
shown otherwise is gone.

A quarter of those twenty-four are methodological rather than substantive —
what happens when a platform withdraws research data access, who funds the
high-profile studies, what a benchmark for societal-impact work would need to
look like. Those are claims about how to measure, which is a kind this record
already files. The blurb's last clause exists for them.

## What would falsify it

If the first filing into `deployment-and-society` produces a literature note
and nothing else — no practice, no theory, nothing a reader could act on —
then the topic is a shelf rather than a category, and the honest move is to
reconsider it rather than to keep shelving.

That filing is the next unit. The term is on probation by construction, and
this paragraph is the condition.
