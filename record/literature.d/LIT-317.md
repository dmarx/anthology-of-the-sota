# LIT-317: PowerSGD: Practical Low-Rank Gradient Compression for Distributed Optimization

Vogels et al. (2019) — [ARXIV-1905.13727](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.13727)

## What it is

PowerSGD compresses the gradient to a rank-k factorization found by one
power-iteration step, which is all-reducible, and error feedback is what
makes it converge.

From the paper's own abstract:

> We study gradient compression methods to alleviate the communication
> bottleneck in data-parallel distributed optimization. Despite the
> significant attention received, current compression schemes either do
> not scale well or fail to achieve the target test accuracy. We propose a
> new low-rank gradient compressor based on power iteration that can i)
> compress gradients rapidly, ii) efficiently aggregate the compressed
> gradients using all-reduce, and iii) achieve test performance on par
> with SGD.
