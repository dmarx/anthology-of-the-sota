The candidates `#331` collected while working its units. Each rests on one
controlled comparison, so each is `Proposed`, and each names the comparison
that would promote it. [DP-006](../design-principles.md#dp-6) is why they are filed at all. Having a
document is a lower bar than believing it. A single-source result filed now,
with its promotion condition written down, is findable when the second
source arrives. Left in an issue it would not be.

## Every number was re-checked against the paper text before filing

Unit 6 found that numbers carried from notes into practices had drifted from
the papers. So every figure in these six was re-read from the extracted
text:

- VDM Table 6
- Video LDM Tables 3 and 11
- CausVid Table 4
- Emu Video Table 1
- Rectified Flow Table 1a

All held. Three sentences in my drafts did not, and were caught by rereading
before filing:

- **`introduced_by`.** The freeze practice named AnimateDiff. Video LDM froze
  spatial layers three months earlier, so it now names [LIT-621](../../record/literature.d/LIT-621.md).
- **An architectural overclaim.** "Freezing rules out full space-time
  attention" was too strong. Freezing rules it out only for the frozen
  layers, and new layers can still sit around them.
- **An unverified adopter.** The decoder practice listed SVD as inheriting
  Video LDM's video-tuned decoder. SVD's text says it follows Video LDM's
  temporal layers and says nothing about the decoder, so SVD is removed.

## What each one is careful about

Most of the work was in each practice's conditions section, not its claim:

- **CausVid.** The ablation cannot separate "the teacher is causal" from
  "the teacher is worse". The causal teacher is also the weaker model before
  distillation.
- **Self Forcing.** It fixes the train-test gap inside the training window
  only. The paper says so. It is filed as an alternative to `SOTA-333`, which
  it contests, and not as a replacement.
- **Freezing.** The only reason to freeze is compatibility, and the one
  controlled test does not measure what freezing protects.
- **Reconstruction guidance.** This is a practice for models you cannot
  retrain. The field now trains conditioning in explicitly.
- **Decoder fine-tuning.** It is for reusing image components. The current
  line trains 3D video autoencoders natively.
- **Reflow.** The paper's own headline needs reflow and distillation.
  Distillation alone does most of the work (378 → 6.18), and reflow costs
  many-step quality.
