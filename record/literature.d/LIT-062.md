# LIT-062: High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models

Rombach et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2112.10752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752)

## Key takeaways

- Pixel-space diffusion spends most of its capacity on **perceptually
  irrelevant detail**, which is a compression problem wearing a generative
  model's clothes
- **Split it**: a pretrained autoencoder maps images to a compact,
  perceptually equivalent latent; the diffusion model is trained there and
  asked only to model semantics
- **The autoencoder is trained once and reused** across diffusion models, so
  its cost amortises
- Conditioning is introduced into the latent-space denoiser rather than bolted
  onto pixel space
- The load-bearing assumption is **perceptual equivalence**: whatever the
  autoencoder discards is unavailable to the diffusion model no matter how
  good it is
