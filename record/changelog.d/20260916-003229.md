### Changed

- **`lint.fail_on` is no longer empty.** It gains `source-mismatch`, and only
  that — the check that verifies a document is about the paper it names, at
  zero today, and the one that would have failed the import in [#138](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/138), which
  filed three readings against identifiers belonging to other papers while
  the lint printed a clean run. It reads the committed lockfile, so it fails
  only on a disagreement that is in the diff.

- **`source-unchecked` is deliberately not promoted**, though it is the
  natural companion. Its only failure mode is an upstream outage, which a
  contributor who did nothing wrong cannot act on, and a dial people learn to
  route around is worse than a dial that is off. Every other class stays
  advice too; the ones with open findings stay advice because a dial turned
  on over a backlog fails the next contributor for somebody else's work.

### Added

- **`ADR-tmpme6k8` — turn the enforcement dial on the check that binds a
  document to its paper.** `Proposed`. Records the change and, at more length,
  a check that was proposed and **rejected on measurement**: an invariant on
  `source:` asserting that a practice shares its paper's topic.

- **`LU-#165` is the duplicate-identifier gap, already filed upstream.** Two
  documents claiming one identifier — the defect [#138](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/138) shipped 67 times — is
  not in luria's finding vocabulary and no config setting produces it. The
  upstream issue asks for a `duplicate-source` finding and a `luria merge` to
  resolve one once found, and `luria.yaml` now names it beside the dial.

### Documentation

- **"A claim inherits the topic of the document it was extracted from" does
  not survive measurement, and the sentence is withdrawn.** It was the
  summary the unbound-lineage pass put on eleven of its fifteen findings.
  Measured: **167 of 220 practices — 76% — already share their source's
  topic**, which is the ordinary correct case, and of the six practices that
  pass retagged, four had inherited their source's topic while two were wrong
  in the opposite direction. The sentence described four documents.

- **The obvious enforcement was already measured and rejected upstream, on
  this record.** `luria/invariants.py` records running the check over
  `source:` and finding 48 of 235 edges, "every one of them a cross-domain
  citation the record was changed to permit", with the conclusion that "a
  check that fires on a project for doing the thing it decided to do is worse
  than no check". Its premise — two separate vocabularies — was removed by
  `ADR-026`. Re-measured today the check reports **73 edges across 64
  practices**, worse rather than better, the largest crossing being the
  evolution-strategies line drawing practices from papers that measure rather
  than recommend.
