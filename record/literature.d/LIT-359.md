# LIT-359: A Necessary and Sufficient Condition for Consensus Over Random Networks

Tahbaz-Salehi and Jadbabaie (2008) — [DOI:10.1109/TAC.2008.917743](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2008.917743)

## What it is

Consensus over random networks holds if and only if the expected topology
has a spanning tree — the condition is on the average graph, not on each
round.

## Standing in the anthology

`Deferred`, which here means what the vocabulary says: in the corpus, not
read closely enough to place. It arrived in the imported batch, which
brought in the consensus, synchronization and flocking literature that the
decentralized-training results rest on.
