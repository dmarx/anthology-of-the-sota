The record documented two lines of work and four lines of practice. The
question was whether more were hiding in the topology. They were, but not
where I first looked.

## What the graph does not tell you

The obvious move is connected components. It does not work, for a reason
worth writing down.

Components over `source:` find **bundles, not chains**: [LIT-051](../../record/literature.d/LIT-051.md) is cited by
seven practices, [LIT-119](../../record/literature.d/LIT-119.md) by six. That is one paper fanning out into several
recommendations — co-derivation. Nothing succeeds anything.

Components over prose citations find one blob. The practice corpus has a
single connected component of 25 documents and two small ones; the literature
corpus has one of 94. Recent documents cite recent documents, and "cites" is
not "descends from". Mutual prose citation — both documents naming each other
— narrows it to 212 pairs in the literature, which is still an order of
magnitude more than the real lineages.

Topology alone cannot distinguish succession from adjacency, because the
record does not encode the difference anywhere the graph can see it.

## Where it actually was

`## Sequence`. Seven practices carry a section by that name, and it does
exactly what the relation was declared for — spells out an order, step by
step, in a paragraph:

> Muon → weight decay and RMS matching so AdamW's hyperparameters transfer
> ([SOTA-121](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-121.md)) → QK-Clip so the attention logits stay bounded at scale (this
> practice) … **Each step keeps the one before.**

One of those seven declared \`extends:\`. Six did not. The structure was in
the record the whole time, written by hand, in the one form nothing can
traverse — which is the exact failure [ADR-011](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-011.md) was filed to prevent, reopened
by accretion.

So the mineable signal was not the graph. It was **prose that describes an
edge the frontmatter does not declare**, and a grep for one section heading
found more than any component analysis did.

## What declaring costs, and what it buys

Two things fell out that prose had been able to avoid.

**It caught an error I shipped a week ago.** [LIT-194](../../record/literature.d/LIT-194.md) said GLA was one of
Gated DeltaNet's two parents. Writing \`extends:\` forces the question "which
document, exactly" — and the answer is that Gated DeltaNet's own abstract
names Mamba2's gate and DeltaNet's rule, and its title is *Improving Mamba2
with Delta Rule*. GLA is not in that line. Prose could gesture at "the
data-dependent gate"; a code cannot.

**It broke the renderer, correctly.** Declaring Gated DeltaNet's two parents
made the first real DAG in any luria record, and the chain page — which
carries nesting as indentation and ordered steps by \`(depth, code)\` —
started asserting that Kimi Linear descends from Mamba-3. Fixed upstream
before landing this, because the alternative was to drop a true edge to keep
a view honest. [LU-#206](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/issues/206).

## What I did not declare

**[SOTA-129](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-129.md) and [SOTA-130](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-130.md).** Its summary says it was filed "so its variations
([SOTA-130](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-130.md)) and its tiny-scale successor ([SOTA-123](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-123.md)) have something to be
traced back to" — an explicit invitation. But \`compared_against\` means *a
comparison was run*, and [SOTA-130](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-130.md)'s own \`promote_when\` asks for exactly the
head-to-head that does not exist. Declaring it would assert the evidence the
practice says is missing.

That is a gap in the vocabulary rather than in the record: there is no way to
say "rival answers to one question, nobody has compared them". Worth a
decision before it gets invented ad hoc.

**Model-report genealogies.** V3.2 → V4, K2 → K3, Qwen3-Next → 3.8. The
mechanisms descend; whether the *reports* do is a different question, and
answering it by hand in a mining pass would commit the record to a whole
dimension it has not decided on.
