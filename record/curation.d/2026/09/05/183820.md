**Seven papers the record was already leaning on.** After the Falcon-H1-Tiny
work merged, a pass over the corpus showed that every 2025–2026 report it
holds trains with a stable-then-decay schedule and several with µP, while
the corpus's own schedule practice was still GPT-3's single cosine cycle and
µP appeared only as a word inside other notes. The recommendation was to
file this group first, and this entry does: WSD as MiniCPM shipped it, the
Hägele et al. study that measured it against cosine, the Power scheduler
that sets its peak, Straight to Zero on how far the decay should go, and
the µP → u-µP → CompleteP line for hyperparameter transfer.

<!-- inactive-ok: SOTA-039 — the retired cosine practice, named as the predecessor in the chain -->
**The first retirement on later evidence.** [SOTA-039](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-039.md), "single cycle of cosine
decay is sufficient", was one of the 119 migrated practices. It is now
`Superseded` by the WSD practice, with the reason in its status note and a
short section in its body saying what it got right — one cycle over
several — and what moved — the cycle's shape. That makes the schedule chain
<!-- inactive-ok: LIT-042 — the retired warm-restarts paper, named as the start of the schedule chain -->
readable from warm restarts ([LIT-042](../../record/literature.d/LIT-042.md), retired at migration) through cosine
to WSD, and it is the first time a practice this record inherited has been
retired by a practice it added. [DP-003](../design-principles.md#dp-3) in action rather than in prose.

**Statuses, and why two are Proposed.** Linear decay to zero is one group's
study, and the production recipes already in the record still decay to a
floor, so the record does not yet assert it. CompleteP is one group and no
production run. Both say what would promote them. The Power scheduler is
Active on the strength of Falcon-H1-Tiny adopting it independently, which
is the kind of evidence the earlier entries kept asking for.
