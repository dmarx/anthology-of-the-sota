# LIT-833: Graph Neural Networks with Learnable Structural and Positional Representations

Dwivedi, Luu, Laurent, Bengio, Bresson, NTU Singapore, Loyola Marymount,
Mila and NUS (ICLR 2022) — [ARXIV-2110.07875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07875)

## Key takeaways

- **RWPE** (Eq. 10): `p_i = [RW_ii, RW²_ii, …, RW^k_ii]` with `RW = AD⁻¹`.
  It has no sign ambiguity. It is unique per node only when every node has
  a distinct k-hop neighbourhood. On CSL every node in a graph gets the same
  vector, yet the vector differs between the non-isomorphic graphs, so they
  are separated (§A.1)
- **LSPE** (Eqs. 7–9): a separate positional state `p` is updated by its
  own message-passing function with tanh, and concatenated into the
  structural update. An optional Laplacian-eigenvector loss on the final `p`
- **ZINC ablation** (Table 3, GatedGCN, 16 layers, about 500k parameters,
  4 seeds): no PE 0.251, LapPE at input 0.202, RWPE at input 0.122, RWPE
  with LSPE 0.100, plus concatenating the final `p` 0.093, plus the
  positional loss 0.090 (Table 1). RWPE added only at the final layer gives
  0.249, no better than none
- **Every base model gains on ZINC** (Table 1): PNA 0.141 to 0.095, SAN
  0.181 to 0.104, GraphiT 0.181 to 0.106. The sparse models beat the
  transformers and train several times faster per epoch
- **OGB molecules** (Table 1): MOLTOX21 GatedGCN 0.772 to 0.775 AUC, PNA
  0.755 to 0.761. MOLPCBA PNA 0.279 to 0.284 AP. The paper attributes the
  smaller effect to OGB features already encoding ring membership
- **Non-molecular** (Table 4): IMDB-B 66.1 to 70.0, IMDB-M 45.8 to 46.5,
  CIFAR10 superpixels 67.3 to 70.9, GatedGCN only, the CIFAR10 baseline
  copied from an earlier paper

## Standing in the anthology

**Filed from the graph-coverage pass** (gap list from the record's own
review, 2026-10-22). It is one of the two sources of [SOTA-452](../practices.d/SOTA-452.md), for the
RWPE half: on ZINC, swapping Laplacian eigenvectors for RWPE at the input
of the same GatedGCN takes test MAE from 0.202 to 0.122 (Table 3).

**The abstract's "1.79% up to 64.14%" is the whole package against no
encoding at all.** The 64.14% is GatedGCN on ZINC, 0.251 to 0.090. Table 3
is what decides what to adopt. The precomputed RWPE at the input does about
four fifths of the work (0.251 to 0.122 of the 0.251 to 0.090). The
learnable positional channel, which is the paper's named contribution,
takes 0.122 to 0.093 and raises epoch time from 9.1 s to 15.2 s. The positional loss adds 0.003 and doubles epoch time
again (33.1 s). The 1.79% is PNA on MOLPCBA. Four seeds per cell.

**The RWPE-over-LapPE explanation is the paper's guess, not its
measurement.** §3.1 attributes it to LapPE's sign ambiguity being harder to
learn than RWPE's occasional non-uniqueness. The two encodings also carry
different information, so Table 3 cannot separate the reasons. Lim et al.
([LIT-818](LIT-818.md)) test the sign half directly, and [THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md) is where
that sits. They also quote this paper's best ZINC score (0.090) against
their own 0.084, from the published number rather than a rerun.
