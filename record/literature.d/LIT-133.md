# LIT-133: Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture

Kimi Team, Moonshot AI (2025) — [ARXIV-2510.26692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692)

## Key takeaways

- Kimi Delta Attention keeps the gated delta-rule recurrence of [LIT-137](LIT-137.md) but replaces
  the scalar forgetting gate with a vector-valued one, so each channel of
  the finite-state memory decays at its own rate — a finer use of the fixed
  memory a linear-attention layer has.
- A chunkwise kernel built on a specialised Diagonal-Plus-Low-Rank
  transition keeps the layer hardware-efficient while staying close to the
  classical delta rule.
- The architecture is a layerwise hybrid: KDA layers interleaved with
  Multi-Head Latent Attention at 3:1, so one layer in four keeps global
  attention.
- On a 48B-total, 3B-activated model trained for 1.4T tokens, the hybrid
  outperforms full MLA under matched conditions across short-context,
  long-context and reinforcement-learning regimes — the paper's claim to be
  the first hybrid linear architecture to do so under a fair comparison.
- Efficiency: KV cache reduced by up to 75%; RULER at 128k reached 84.3
  with 3.98× acceleration; at 1M tokens, time per output token 1.84 ms
  against 11.48 ms for MLA, about 6.3×.
- **The global layers carry no positional encoding at all.** NoPE
  ([LIT-207](LIT-207.md)) is applied to every MLA layer, which hands the whole job of
  position and recency to the KDA layers — the paper's phrase is that KDA
  becomes "the primary position-aware operator". The ablation is the scale
  evidence [LIT-207](LIT-207.md)'s 107M study lacks: against a Kimi Linear (RoPE)
  baseline at the same 48B configuration, the NoPE variant wins on long
  context and the two are level on short, which the paper reads as a
  difference in how positional bias is distributed across depth. Two
  practical consequences it names: the MLA layers become convertible to pure
  MQA at inference, and extending the context needs no base-frequency tuning
  and no YaRN, because there is no positional parameter to retune.

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-132](../practices.d/SOTA-132.md) and the design [LIT-131](LIT-131.md) adopts at 2.8T. The other
hybrid in the record, [LIT-120](LIT-120.md), puts SSM and attention heads side by side in
every block rather than alternating layers; the two are the record's two
answers to the same question.

Gated DeltaNet-2 ([LIT-802](LIT-802.md)) keeps KDA's channel-wise decay and splits
its scalar β into a channel-wise erase gate on the key and a channel-wise
write gate on the value. Tying both gates to one scalar gives KDA back. KDA
was retrained there under a single 1.3B / 100B-token recipe and averages
52.28 against 53.11 as a recurrent model and 52.68 against 53.97 as a
hybrid with sliding-window attention. Olmo Hybrid ([LIT-799](LIT-799.md)) lists Kimi
Linear 48B-A3B in its table of open-weight models, trained on different data
and budgets: Math 68.5 against Olmo Hybrid's 55.1, RULER at 64K 90.5 against
85.0. It names this paper among the hybrids that do not use the
negative-eigenvalue extension of the delta rule.
