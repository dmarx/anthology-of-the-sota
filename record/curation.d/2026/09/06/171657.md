The new axis is only worth having if the values are defensible, so the
editorial rule was: assign where the record already carries the grounds, and
leave everything else `unassessed`. That produced ten, and the shape of the
ten is itself informative.

**Four `unreplicated`** — [SOTA-122](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-122.md), [SOTA-124](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-124.md), [SOTA-125](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-125.md), [SOTA-144](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-144.md). All four are
one group, one result, and in every case the record already said so in the
body; what it could not say is that nobody has *disagreed* either. That is the
distinction `Proposed` was hiding, and it is the difference between "waiting
for someone to look" and "people looked and differ".

**Two `contested`** — [SOTA-136](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-136.md) and [SOTA-130](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-130.md). Both have credible published
opposition: two independent critiques of mHC's constraint within a month, and
[LIT-167](../../record/literature.d/LIT-167.md) undercutting the attribution RLVR-Zero rests on. Neither is a doubt
this record invented; both are arguments happening in public.

**Two `emerging`** — [SOTA-133](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-133.md) and [SOTA-141](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-141.md). Moving, not arrived. [SOTA-141](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-141.md) is
the more interesting: nobody argues decay-to-zero is *wrong*, the frontier
recipes in this record simply still decay to a floor. "Not adopted" and
"disputed" are different states and now say so.

**Two at the top** — [SOTA-001](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-001.md) `universal` and [SOTA-120](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-120.md) `converged`.

[SOTA-120](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-120.md) is the one that justifies the whole change. It is `Deferred` — the
record declines to assert it — and `converged`, because AdamW is the default
optimizer and decoupled decay is what nearly everyone runs whether or not
they chose it. **High consensus, low editorial confidence.** One axis cannot
hold that, and the practice has been sitting in an uncomfortable status for
weeks because of it.

What I deliberately did not do: file the trunk practice. [LIT-181](../../record/literature.d/LIT-181.md) says the
record should *probably* carry "widen the residual stream, constrain the
mixing somehow" *eventually*, and turning that hedge into an assertion is a
judgement someone should make on purpose rather than a side effect of
building the mechanism to hold it. The fork renders without it.

The number to watch is 134. Most of this corpus has no consensus reading, and
the default makes that visible rather than papered over — but it also means
the axis is one person's ten judgements until somebody does the rest.
