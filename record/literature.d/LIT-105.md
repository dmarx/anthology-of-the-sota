Retired as a duplicate rather than for anything about the work. The
migration filed this paper twice: once as `Symbolic Discovery of
Optimization Algorithms` ([LIT-090](LIT-090.md)) and once as *Lion: Learning with
Implicit Optimization* — the optimizer's name, not the paper's title —
against an arXiv identifier belonging to something else entirely. Repairing
that identifier is what revealed the two notes were one paper.

Kept because the pair is the evidence for a real gap: two documents can name
one source and the lint stays clean ([luria#165](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/issues/165)). [LIT-090](LIT-090.md) carries the
reading.
