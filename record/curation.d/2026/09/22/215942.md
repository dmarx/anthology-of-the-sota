`#163` asked, "at very least", for TP-IV "for muP". The 2026-09-19 entry
measured the checklist and answered that this item was already done. It said
[LIT-148](../../record/literature.d/LIT-148.md) is Tensor Programs V, "which *is* the µP paper". That entry was
right that nothing in the record depended on TP-IV by name, and wrong about
the fact it used to dismiss the item.

TP-IV introduces µP: Definition 5.1, the name, and the uniqueness result in
Appendix C.1. TP-V takes that parametrization, carries it to Adam and
Transformers, and turns it into hyperparameter transfer. [SOTA-143](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-143.md)'s
*instruction* comes from TP-V, which is why the practice was correctly
sourced. But its *reason* is TP-IV's, and the record had no copy of it.

**Why this was worth a theory and not only a note.** [SOTA-143](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-143.md) had one
explanation, [THEORY-024](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-024.md). That is the duality frame, and it went `Proposed`
on 2026-09-20 once an outside group tested it and it came back weak. So the
record's most-used parametrization rested on a reason the record itself had
stopped believing. TP-IV's reason is a theorem, and it made a prediction that
was measured: the maximum stable learning rate scales like `1/width` under
SP and stays constant under µP. It is filed `Active` and kept to what the
theorem covers. That coverage is narrower than people assume. It is SGD, it
is the stable *ceiling* and not the optimum, and the real-task experiments
use linear networks.

**The query, for [DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4)'s sake.** "Which issue items does the record already
hold" was answered by a title grep plus a body grep for each item's name. That
finds a paper the record holds under its own title. It cannot find one the
record *believes* it holds under another paper's title, and that is exactly
what happened with TP-IV. The fix does not generalize to a check. What would
have caught it is reading TP-V's first page, which calls µP something
*earlier* work discovered.

**The rest of `#163`, in the order agreed:** Gemma 3n per-layer embeddings,
MatFormer, Matryoshka embeddings, ReFT. Each ties into something already in
the record. AlphaFold stays a scope question for the owner, as the
2026-09-21 entry left it.
