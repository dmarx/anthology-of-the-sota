# LIT-070: Hierarchical Text-Conditional Image Generation with CLIP Latents

Ramesh et al. (2022) — [ARXIV-2204.06125](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.06125)

## Key takeaways

- Two explicit stages: a **prior** mapping a caption to a **CLIP image
  embedding**, and a diffusion **decoder** rendering from that embedding. The
  explicit intermediate is what buys zero-shot language-guided manipulation,
  since CLIP's space is joint over text and images
- **The split buys diversity and costs fidelity, stated plainly:** humans
  *slightly prefer GLIDE* on photorealism — the gap is small — and *strongly
  prefer* unCLIP on diversity
- **A diffusion prior beats an autoregressive one** on both compute and sample
  quality — an early instance of diffusion displacing AR in a component role
- **Guide the decoder, not the prior**: "we found that guiding the prior hurt
  results"
- **The embedding does not carry attribute binding.** Figure 15's
  reconstructions of difficult binding problems mix up which object has which
  colour and fail on relative size — a property of the representation, not the
  decoder

## Standing in the anthology

Read — [NOTE-064](../notes.d/NOTE-064.md). Nothing is sourced here.

The reading generalises a condition the record holds in one place. `SOTA-187`
carries "the latent must be perceptually equivalent, and what the autoencoder
discards is a hard ceiling the diffusion model cannot see past". This paper
shows the same failure in a *different* representation, with a concrete
diagnostic — **reconstruct through the bottleneck and look at what breaks** —
and what breaks is attribute binding, exactly what text-to-image models were
criticised for at the time. Better-supported than the record's own source for
that condition, and it comes with a way to test it.

Of the takeaways replaced, **"improved composition ability" is backwards**: the
paper's own figure shows composition failing.

**Audited by [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/121); unsourced.** R1 is the methodological claim —
when inserting an explicit intermediate representation, measure what it fails to
carry by reconstructing through it — and it is a general instrument the record
does not hold. R2 (expect a representation bottleneck to buy diversity and cost
fidelity) is the finding, and it is one measurement. Unfiled: the instrument is
worth having and the record has no second use of it to file it from.
