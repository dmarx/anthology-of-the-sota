# LIT-287: OpenDiLoCo: An Open-Source Framework for Globally Distributed Low-Communication Training

Jaghouar et al. (2024) — [ARXIV-2407.07852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07852)

## What it is

OpenDiLoCo: a reproduction and open implementation of DiLoCo, trained across
continents at 90-95% compute utilization.

From the paper's own abstract:

> OpenDiLoCo is an open-source implementation and replication of the
> Distributed Low-Communication (DiLoCo) training method for large
> language models. We provide a reproducible implementation of the DiLoCo
> experiments, offering it within a scalable, decentralized training
> framework using the Hivemind library. We demonstrate its effectiveness
> by training a model across two continents and three countries, while
> maintaining 90-95% compute utilization.
