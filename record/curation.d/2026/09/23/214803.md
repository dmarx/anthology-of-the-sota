Unit H of `#304`, added after the worklist closed, from a question rather
than from the audit.

## The defect, and why it is the worst shape yet

**68 documents mention FID. None held the paper.** That is larger than ViT
(47) or CLIP (45), the two the whole audit was built around.

What makes it worse than a large number is the company it was keeping. The
record already held:

- `LIT-563` — FID can be *moved without improving images*, by exploiting what
  Inception features encode;
- `LIT-501` — FID *moves on its own*, by about 1.3% of its value, purely from
  the training seed;
- `SOTA-337` (`Active`) — when an ImageNet-pretrained network has touched
  training, confirm FID gains in another feature space;
- `SOTA-307` (`Active`) — report FID as an error bar over several training
  seeds;
- `SOTA-338` — a `promote_when` that turns on a non-ImageNet Fréchet
  distance.

**Two critiques, two live recommendations, and no note for the thing being
criticised.** Unit E found this shape with MAE — the winner of a named
comparison without the loser — and this is it four times larger and three
documents further along. A reader could arrive at the record's complete
theory of how FID misleads and never learn what FID is.

The generalisation: **a metric accumulates critique documents faster than it
accumulates a definition document, because critiques are what a record's
curation process rewards.** Nobody files the incumbent; everybody files the
paper that found the flaw. Worth a check of its own — for any metric the
record has an opinion about, does it hold the paper that defined it?

## A near-duplicate caught by reading before writing

I spent the first half of this unit preparing to file **The FID Lottery** as a
new note, having told the user I could not identify the paper. The record
**already holds it** as `LIT-501`, filed thoroughly, sourcing `SOTA-307` and
qualifying `SOTA-143` with its 1.7× µP window finding. I had drafted a
duplicate note, a duplicate practice (`SOTA-307` already says exactly what my
draft said, better — it carries the `2σ/√N` scaling I did not have) and a
theory that `LIT-501`'s own standing section covers.

All three were discarded before commit, on noticing `SOTA-143`'s history
entry naming `LIT-501`.

**The failure was not the misidentification, it was not re-running the check
after the identification arrived.** I searched for "The FID Lottery", found
nothing, said so, and when the user supplied the link I went straight to
reading the paper — never returning to the one question the whole exercise
was about: *does the record already hold it?* The audit habit was suspended
by the arrival of an answer to a different question.

Third instance this session of the same family — a code recalled rather than
looked up (`LIT-590` for `LIT-591`), a document attributed rather than checked
(`SOTA-338` for `SOTA-337`), and now **a lookup skipped because a different
question got answered first.** The first two were caught by habit; this one
was caught by accident, while reading `SOTA-143` for an unrelated reason.

Cheap rule, worth stating: **when a new identifier arrives from outside,
the first action is the held-check, before the reading.**

## What reading the incumbent actually supplied

Not much that is new — and that is the point of filing it anyway. Two things
every downstream document leans on without restating:

**The Gaussian fit is an assumption and the paper says so once.** "We assume
the coding units to follow a multidimensional Gaussian." Everything after
that — the Fréchet, i.e. Wasserstein-2, distance between two fitted
Gaussians — is exact given an assumption that is nowhere tested in the paper
and rarely mentioned downstream.

**The validation was monotonicity under deliberate corruption.** Six
corruption families, FID rising with each. That establishes the measure
orders *degraded* distributions correctly. It establishes nothing about
resolving small gaps between two competent models — which is the entire use
the field puts it to, and exactly what `LIT-501` later measured a floor for.

So the incumbent's own evidence and its successors' critiques fit together
cleanly once both are held. **The critiques were not overturning a
well-supported claim; they were measuring in a regime the original never
tested.** That is a much less adversarial reading of the cluster than the
record could previously state, and it required the paper nobody had filed.
