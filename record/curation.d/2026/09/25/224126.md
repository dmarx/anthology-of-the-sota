The owner asked what this journal had skipped, and meant it as a survey for
*nucleation*: work set aside as out of scope, to be triaged there. That survey
scanned all 239 entries, every ADR and issue [#290](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/290), and the papers feed. It found
**70 candidates**. Only one of them was a scope decline named by title and held
nowhere: *The Work Capacity of Channels with Memory*. The other 69 are the
social-science, physics and philosophy clusters that the 09/25 reading-time
triage named only as a group. All 70 go to nucleation, with **five exceptions**.
Those five are filed here under the owner's rule for the boundary ("when in
doubt, the anthology"), and each is `Deferred` with a `Skimmed` NOTE:

- **Burrell, *Automated decision-making as domination*** (`deployment-and-society`).
- **Kosmyna et al., *Your Brain on ChatGPT*** (`deployment-and-society`). The
  skim did not reach its EEG statistics (pp. 60–150), which is where its claim
  lives.
- **Møller et al., *The Impact of Generative AI on Social Media*** (`deployment-and-society`).
  This is a randomized experiment.
- **Roy et al., *Surya*, a heliophysics foundation model**
  (`vision-and-graphics`, `model-architecture`, `adaptation-and-tuning`).
- **Ji-An, Benna & Mattar, *Discovering cognitive strategies with tiny RNNs***
  (`analysis-and-evaluation`, `tiny-models`). First appeared as a 2023
  bioRxiv preprint. A published critique exists and has not been read.

**Two of them name vocabulary gaps. Both are recorded here, not resolved.**

- *Surya* is an ML model of physical data. [ADR-059](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-059.md) anticipated a word for
  scientific-domain models, to be added once the record holds one. It holds one
  now.
- The tiny-RNN paper is behavioural modelling. No topic says that.

The tags above are the honest nearest fits. By this record's own rule, the fix
for both is to add a word. That is the owner's call, and it is flagged here so
that it is made once.
