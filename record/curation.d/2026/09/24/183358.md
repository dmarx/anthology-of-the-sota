Unit 7 of `#331` was blocked for a day. openai.com returns 403 to the
build environment, and web.archive.org and archive.ph reset the
connection. A reader proxy got through: `r.jina.ai/<canonical URL>` returns
the page as Markdown, with the author list and references. Try it before
calling an openai.com source unreachable.

The mirror loses the embedded videos. For this report the videos are the
evidence, so the note records its video-backed claims as claims. A reader
with a browser could add what the videos show, though they would still
be curated samples.

## What was in it

Nothing measured. The report has no table, metric, sample count or
baseline. It has two comparisons. One shows one set of samples at three
compute levels. The other compares Sora with a square-crop variant. Both
are shown and neither is quantified. "Purely phenomena of scale" has no
smaller model shown failing. That is the report doing what it says it
does: "Model and implementation details are not included".

The finding is what the record had been doing with it. `NOTE-350` said
Sora uses "a separate upsampler". That came from LTX-Video §2, which
attributes to this report "an upsampler working directly in pixel space,
conditioned on the latent outputs of the base model". The report never
mentions upsampling. LTX-Video's authors may know it from elsewhere, but
they cite this report, and the report cannot support it.

This is [DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4)'s shape again, the same one the substrate filing found.
The claim sat on an unheld source, so no pass could check it. It is
[DP-010](../design-principles.md#dp-10) as well: the unsupported sentence is the specific one, and the
specific sentence is the one that gets repeated.

## What was not done

- **No practice for native-size training.** Sora's square-crop comparison
  is the only evidence the record has, and it is qualitative. NaViT
  (2307.06304, Sora's reference 18) would be the place to start, and it is
  not held.
- **Movie Gen's and Wan's Sora comparisons were not re-read.** They are
  already in `NOTE-334` and `NOTE-333`. Filing Sora did not change them.
- **The unbound line in the lineage report** (`LIT-640`, `LIT-641`,
  `LIT-651`, the QK-norm group) is already on `main`. It does not come
  from this filing and was left alone.
