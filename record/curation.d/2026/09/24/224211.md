These are the rest of `#331`'s open items. Three were searches, run by a
subagent and then re-checked here against the PDFs, and two were practices
the video readings had flagged. The largest finding was on neither list.

## [SOTA-263](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-263.md) was waiting for something published in January 2023

`SOTA-263`'s `promote_when` asked for "an ablation reporting what the
shift is worth". The practice said the shift was "derived, then
demonstrated qualitatively", and it named SD3 as its origin. All three
statements were wrong in the same way.

- **SD3 names the source.** It says its log-SNR shift is "similar to
  (Hoogeboom et al., 2023)".
- **That paper, simple diffusion, measures it.** Its Table 2 is the
  requested ablation: 7.65 → 3.76 FID at ImageNet 256, with only the
  schedule changed.
- **A concurrent paper measures it too.** Chen (2023) sweeps the same shift
  written as input scaling.
- **SD3 itself has a number.** Fig. 6 is a human-preference sweep of the
  shift value. The note on SD3 called it qualitative.

This is [DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4) again, in the shape the last three entries found:

1. The practice was written from the paper that popularized the idea.
2. That paper's related-work pointer was not followed.
3. The condition was then written to wait for evidence that already
   existed.

The origin and the evidence were both one citation away. A `promote_when`
is only as good as the search behind it, and this one had none.

The promotion also exposed a flaw in the condition itself. Its second
clause, "a second high-resolution training report that says it shifted
for this reason", counts adoption. LTX-Video and Open-Sora 2.0 had already
satisfied it, and two readings (`NOTE-350` and `NOTE-345`) flagged that to
the curator. That clause was not used, per [DP-005](../design-principles.md#dp-5). The first clause is
what moved the practice. [ADR-014](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-014.md) says a condition should name a kind of
result, not a count. This clause named a kind of *report*, and reports
are cheap.

The re-read also changed what the practice says. Every source derives a
shift from pixel count, and none uses the derived value:

- SD3 derives α = 4 and uses 3.0.
- Simple diffusion picks its reference resolution empirically.
- Chen's sweep is not monotone.
- FLUX.2's latent-channel version misses its prediction too.

So the practice now says to shift in the predicted direction and tune the
amount.

## The three searches

The W.A.L.T, FiT and Multigrid numbers below were re-extracted from the
PDFs here. The Latte, NiT, FiTv2 and SigLIP 2 descriptions are the
subagent's and were not re-checked.

- **A compute-matched test of a separate image corpus (`SOTA-386`).** Not
  found. W.A.L.T Table 5 is the only ablation with a separate corpus
  switched on and off. It is filed, and it does not state compute. Latte's
  "+IMG" arm, like VDM's, uses frames of its own videos.
- **A quantitative generative test of native aspect ratio (`SOTA-398`).**
  FiT is the best isolation, and it is filed. It shows no gain at square
  output. NiT (2506.03131) Table 4 holds the token budget fixed across data
  mixtures, but its square-only arm is not in its aspect-ratio test. It is
  not filed. FiTv2's "mixed" arm is evaluated only against centre-cropped
  references.
- **Staged against mixed resolution (`SOTA-251` against `SOTA-398`).** No
  ViT comparison exists. Multigrid (1912.00998, Table 1) is the only head
  to head, on a video CNN. Long cycles only give 74.5%, short cycles only
  74.0%, and both 76.4%, against a 75.6% baseline. It is not filed. Its
  long cycle repeats in every learning-rate stage, so it is not
  `SOTA-251`'s late raise. SigLIP 2's NaFlex against standard comparison
  changes four things at once.

## A contrary datum picked up on the way

W.A.L.T's Table 3b is the only numbered comparison against full 3D
attention in the record. Windowed hybrids match it at 1.7× the speed. It
is recorded as a condition on `SOTA-390` and does not contest it. It is
128px and 17 frames, where full attention is cheap, and it is not the
2D+1D factorization the practice argues against.

## Method note

The subagent's report was accurate on every number checked: the
W.A.L.T, FiT and Multigrid tables. It was right to flag
W.A.L.T's missing compute. It did not spot the `SOTA-390` datum in the same
paper, because its brief did not ask. The same [DP-004](../design-principles.md#dp-4) shape applies to
delegated search.
