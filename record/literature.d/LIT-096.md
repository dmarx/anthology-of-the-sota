## Key takeaways

- Universal segmentation model
- Prompt engineering
- Zero-shot capabilities
- Real-time performance

## Standing in the anthology

The source of [SOTA-186](../practices.d/SOTA-186.md).

The practice drawn from it is not about segmentation. It is the three-stage
annotation loop — assisted-manual, then semi-automatic, then fully automatic,
retraining between stages — which is a `data-pipeline` claim and the reason
this note is in the record at all under [ADR-020](../decisions.d/ADR-020.md).

The model and the dataset are built together, and each stage's labels train
the model that produces the next stage's. That is the mechanism; the 1.1B
masks are the consequence.
