**Chosen off the 5-revisit tier for adjacency, and the adjacency paid.**
Image-GS sits against the tokenizer trunk filed earlier today, and the reason
to read it next was that `NOTE-243` had left an open question about
GaussianToken: its comparisons were matched on token count while each token
carried an index plus five continuous floats, so nothing ruled out the rival
explanation that it simply had nine times the channel. Image-GS is the same
Gaussian primitives in the same year, reporting bits per pixel throughout and
matching baselines in kilobytes. It does not answer the question — nobody ran
that comparison — but it shows the accounting was available and was not done.

**No relation declared between them, deliberately.** `ADR-011` means what it
says: `compared_against` is for a comparison somebody ran. The connection is
this record's reading and lives in prose, the same handling the Duede paper
got against `LIT-487` earlier today.

**The filing decision was again "not a new practice".** Content-adaptive
densification is what 3D Gaussian Splatting already does and what `SOTA-205`
already covers at the level that matters. So this is v2 and a fifth source,
not a sixth document. That is twice in a row the grep-before-filing habit
changed the outcome — the Gemini contamination finding went to `SOTA-197` v2
for the same reason.

**What Image-GS adds to `SOTA-205` is the units.** The four existing sources
argue from rendering speed, which entangles the representation with a
rasterizer, and they are all 3D. This gives 0.3K multiply-accumulates per
pixel against C3's 3K at comparable bitrate — the claim stated in the quantity
it is actually about — and it does it in the weakest case for an explicit
structure, a single image with no viewpoint, no occlusion and no geometry.

**And an observation that is now a pair.** Table 1 shows the content-adaptive
allocation the paper is named for is worth 2.23 dB, while optimizing `1/s`
instead of the raw scale is worth 2.66 and top-K normalization is worth 2.42.
Two implementation details, each outweighing the idea in the title. `SOTA-304`
records the same shape in Genesys, where removing the symbolic checker costs
62 points of validity and removing the clever decomposition costs 19.

Two is not several. `DP-009`. What would turn this into something is a
mechanism, and the only candidate — that papers name themselves after the
interesting half — is a claim about authors rather than about methods, and
this record is not the place to evidence it. Counted in both documents,
promoted to nothing.

**The most surprising number was left as a question.** The `1/s`
reparameterization is justified in one sentence: the natural range is
`[5,10]` and the inverse lands in `[0,1]`, where gradients are smoother and
more stable. If that holds for other parameters bounded away from zero it is a
practice worth having. It is one measurement on one parameter in one paper, so
it went in the note's open questions.
