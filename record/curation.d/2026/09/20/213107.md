Asked to add a scheme for naming systems with no paper. Measured the
population first, and the measurement changed the design — twice.

## What I found instead

`implementations:` is **not declared in `luria.yaml`**. It never has been. It
sits on `SOTA` and `LIT` documents carrying whatever anybody typed: 142
distinct spellings across 216 declarations.

The spellings are the tell. `t5` and `T5`. `Kimi Linear` and `Kimi Linear
48B-A3B`. `SWAN-GPT-1B`, `SWAN-GPT-8B`, and `SWAN-GPT (NVIDIA, 1B and 8B)`
— three names for two models. And `Falcon-H1-Tiny (0.1 after the linear
projections, for a repeated FIM corpus)`, which is a hyperparameter value
parenthesised into a system name because there was no field validation to
stop it.

Then the number that reframed everything: **141 of the 142 already had a
note.** `MMLU` is [LIT-390](../../../../literature.d/LIT-390.md). `RETRO` is [LIT-060](../../../../literature.d/LIT-060.md). `EDM` is [LIT-075](../../../../literature.d/LIT-075.md).
`Progressive Distillation` is literally the first two words of [LIT-067](../../../../literature.d/LIT-067.md)'s
title.

So the field was not naming systems the record lacked. It was naming
documents the record held, in a spelling nothing could follow. The same shape
as the RETRO finding two days ago, and 141 times larger.

## Why I did not build the scheme as asked

Two independent reasons, and I found them in the wrong order — I argued from
the second before testing the first, which is backwards.

**luria's `scheme:` is single-valued.** I tried `scheme: [LIT, SYS]` and it
is refused: *"names scheme '['LIT', 'SYS']', which is not declared"*. So
`implementations:` can point at one scheme. Pointing it at `SYS` would mean
142 `SYS` documents of which 141 proxy a `LIT` note that already exists —
two documents for one object, which is what [DP-002](../../../../principles.d/DP-002.md) exists to prevent.

**And [ADR-032](../../../../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) already routed them.** Benchmarks, model reports and
training or serving infrastructure are literature and the record files them.
Every category a systems scheme would hold is a category that decision
already sends to `LIT`, and the record has notes for all of them. A second
scheme would not have been a new home; it would have been a competing one.

## The part where the premise was partly right

My first report to the user said the paperless population was **one**. That
was wrong, and I should not have said it with that confidence.

Working through the residue by hand turned up `Nemotron-3 Super` and
`Nemotron-3 Ultra` on [SOTA-180](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-180.md). These are NVIDIA models the record knows of
only because [LIT-196](../../../../literature.d/LIT-196.md) mentions them as adopters. There is no report for
either. They are exactly what the request was about — a system the record
names, with no document — and I had assumed them away by mapping them to the
nearest matching note.

Mapping them to [LIT-196](../../../../literature.d/LIT-196.md) would have been wrong twice: it is the *method's*
paper, and it is already [SOTA-180](../../../../practices.d/SOTA-180.md)'s `source:`, so the field would have said a
practice is implemented by its own evidence. Dropped from the field, kept in
the prose that already carried them.

**So the answer is two, not one, and they are a real class**: adopters known
only by somebody else's mention. Two is small enough to carry in prose, which
is what [ADR-043](../../../../decisions.d/ADR-043.md) prescribes until it is not — and if that class grows, the
scheme becomes worth building and this entry is where the count started.

## What I nearly lost

Typing the field collapses variants. `DiffuGPT` and `DiffuLLaMA` both become
[LIT-381](../../../../literature.d/LIT-381.md); `SWAN-GPT-1B` and `SWAN-GPT-8B` both become [LIT-209](../../../../literature.d/LIT-209.md). A reader who
needs to know it was the 8B can no longer get that from the field.

I checked before accepting it: every affected practice carries a
`## Known implementations` section naming the specific systems, and those are
untouched. That is [ADR-017](../../../../decisions.d/ADR-017.md)'s existing shape — the field is typed, the prose
is argument — rather than a new concession, which is why it is acceptable
here and would not be if the prose did not exist.

## The method note

I reported "population of one" to the user before finishing the residue by
hand, on the strength of two automated matchers. The matchers were good
enough to reshape the design and not good enough to support a count, and I
quoted the count.

The pattern is the same as the `THEORY-013` error earlier today: an assertion
made at the confidence of the evidence I wished I had rather than the
evidence I had. Different subject, same failure. Twice in one day is a
frequency worth writing down rather than a coincidence worth excusing.
