Twenty-three notes in the generative and vision corpus had been set aside as
out of scope by construction — twice, once by the issue and once by me, both
times from the tag rather than the paper. [ADR-020](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-020.md) removed that exit. This is
the first pass over them with the question [ADR-020](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-020.md) actually asks: does the
work contain an instruction one of the seven topics can express?

**Three do, and all three are claims that outgrow their domain.**

EDM ([LIT-075](../../record/literature.d/LIT-075.md)) contributes a preconditioning scheme, a training-noise
distribution and a loss weighting. A denoiser does a different job at every
noise level, so without adjustment the loss is dominated by whichever regime
has the largest raw magnitudes. Wrap the network in level-dependent scalings
so the target has unit variance everywhere, and sample the level from a
log-normal concentrated where there is something to learn rather than
uniformly. The shape generalizes past diffusion: when a network is
conditioned on a variable that changes the difficulty of its task, normalize
the target so the loss means the same thing at every value, and sample that
variable where the signal is.

Segment Anything ([LIT-096](../../record/literature.d/LIT-096.md)) contributes a three-stage annotation loop —
assisted-manual, semi-automatic, fully automatic, retraining between stages.
Nothing about segmentation. The claim is that when annotation binds, the
right structure is a staged loop whose escalation is driven by the current
model uncertainty, not a one-shot labelling contract.

Latent Diffusion ([LIT-062](../../record/literature.d/LIT-062.md)) contributes a compute-allocation argument:
likelihood models spend most of their capacity on imperceptible detail, so
strip it with a cheap model first. Two stages — learn a compact
representation, then model that — filed under model-architecture because
that is what it is.

**Eight carry no practice today, and the reasons split in a way worth noticing.** The
radiance-field and task-solution papers — Instant-NGP, K-Planes, 3D Gaussian
Splatting, NeuS2, OnePose, Gaussian Shell Maps, OWL-ViT, MiDaS. Two are worth
the specific reason rather than the generic one. 3D Gaussian Splatting is
consequential and carries no training instruction because there is no network
being trained; it is an optimization over primitives, and it stays as reading
because the radiance-field line is unintelligible without it. MiDaS has a
genuinely transferable half — mix many datasets under an objective invariant
to their differing scale conventions — but the note carries no evidence about
it beyond the released models, which is the [ADR-017](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-017.md) case: shipping is not
measuring.

**Four of those eight are the vocabulary's fault, not the paper's.** Where a
claim has no category to take, that is a shortcoming of the topic set rather
than evidence the literature is irrelevant — and Instant-NGP, K-Planes,
OnePose and Gaussian Splatting are all declined on exactly that. The first
three carry representation and encoding claims and the record's encoding
practices are scattered (RoPE in `attention-techniques`, quantization formats
in `systems-optimization`); the fourth optimizes explicit primitives rather
than network weights, with densification and pruning schedules the record has
no comparable entry for. Written up as gaps to close, not as papers turned
away. The first draft of this entry said the seven topics "cannot express"
them, which reads as a verdict on the work and is the wrong way round.

**A workflow note.** The first draft closed each of the eleven notes with the
same paragraph explaining that it had been read under [ADR-020](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-020.md). The lint
reported ten unacknowledged citations of a `Proposed` decision, which was the
correct complaint about the wrong thing: ten identical directives would have
been the fix, and eleven identical paragraphs were the actual defect. Removed
them; the fact belongs here once, which is what a curation entry is for. The
guard caught a repetition problem by way of a reference problem.

**What is left.** The generative half still has ten bare notes, and they are
the harder ones: DDIM and DPM-Solver are sampler algorithms, which is the
inference-shaped gap [ADR-020](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-020.md) explicitly declined to open a topic for;
Progressive Distillation and Consistency Models are training procedures that
buy inference speed and probably do carry practices; Textual Inversion and
DreamBooth are personalization methods that sit beside LoRA; ControlNet and
T2I-Adapter are conditioning architectures; DALL-E 2 and Imagen are model
reports where [ADR-017](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-017.md) applies, though Imagen has a real capacity-allocation
finding underneath it. Each needs a reading, and none of them is settled by a
tag.
