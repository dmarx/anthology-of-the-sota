# LIT-135: Qwen3.8-27B

Qwen Team, Alibaba (2026) —
[Qwen3.8-27B model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B) and the
release post at qwen.ai (`blog?id=qwen3.8`, whose own title is
"Qwen3.8-Max: A New Bar for Coding and Cowork"). No paper; the third
`url:`-only source in the record.

## Key takeaways

- Released 14 August 2026 under Apache 2.0: 27.78B parameters, dense, with
  a 27-layer vision encoder for image and video, a 262,144-token native
  context extensible to 1M, and a multi-token-prediction head shipped in
  the checkpoint for EAGLE-style speculative decoding.
- The language model is a 64-layer, 5120-hidden hybrid: 48 Gated DeltaNet
  linear-attention layers (48 value heads, 16 query/key heads, 128-dim) and
  16 gated full-attention layers (24 query heads, 4 KV heads, 256-dim) —
  three linear layers to one global, so only 16 layers carry a KV cache.
  `full_attention_interval` is 4 and `layer_types` spells the pattern out
  literally; `attn_output_gate` is true and `output_gate_type` is `swish`,
  which is where [SOTA-134](../practices.d/SOTA-134.md) lands in this checkpoint. Rotation is partial
  (`partial_rotary_factor` 0.25) with an mRoPE section of [11, 11, 10] over
  a 10M theta.
- The configuration is the same as Qwen3.6-27B's. Fetched from the Hub and
  compared directly, the two `config.json` files differ in exactly one key —
  `transformers_version`, 4.57.1 against 5.8.0.dev0 — which is a stamp of
  the library that serialised the file, not a fact about the model. Every
  architectural field is identical: `model_type`, the 64 entries of
  `layer_types` and their 3:1 pattern, hidden size, head counts and
  dimensions on both attention paths, `attn_output_gate`, vocabulary,
  `max_position_embeddings`, the MTP layer, and the whole vision block.
  What changed in four months is post-training. The 3.8 card re-evaluates
  both in one harness — Claude Code, with the baselines re-run on its own
  refined benchmark — and reports Terminal Bench 2.1 (Terminus) 73.0 vs
  63.4, SWE-bench Pro 61.7 vs 53.5, QwenSWEBench 79.0 vs 49.3, with smaller
  gains on plain reasoning. QwenSWEBench is in-house.
- Thinking mode is on by default and can be disabled per request.
  `reasoning_effort` takes `xhigh`, `medium` or `low`, and the default is
  `xhigh` — the deepest of the three, described as being for complex tasks
  demanding thorough analysis — so the out-of-the-box configuration is the
  expensive one. `preserve_thinking` is likewise on by default, carrying
  reasoning context forward from earlier messages.

## Standing in the anthology

Every architectural number above is read from the checkpoint's own
`config.json`, not from coverage of it; the benchmark comparisons are the
model card's own re-evaluation of both generations in one harness, which is
the card's claim rather than an independent one.

Filed as the current dense instance of the design Qwen3-Next introduced
([LIT-136](LIT-136.md)) and Qwen3.5 and 3.6 carried: Gated DeltaNet ([LIT-137](LIT-137.md)) for three
layers in four, gated attention ([LIT-138](LIT-138.md)) for the fourth. It is the second
independent adopter of the 3:1 hybrid in the record, with a different
linear module from Kimi's ([LIT-133](LIT-133.md)), which is what let [SOTA-132](../practices.d/SOTA-132.md) stop
calling that replication missing. The pretraining recipe for this
checkpoint is still not published, and nothing here is sourced to the card
beyond the architecture — but the generation's design work now is
([LIT-152](LIT-152.md)), for a different model in the same family.

**No practice of its own: a model report is adoption, not evidence
([ADR-017](../decisions.d/ADR-017.md)).** It describes a recipe in full and isolates none of it, so what
it supplies is that somebody shipped these choices — which is a claim about
the field, and belongs in the `consensus_note:` of the practices whose
designs it carries. It is cited that way by the hybrid-interleave and multi-token-prediction practices, and it is the sharpest adoption datum in the record for the claim that a generation's gains can come entirely from post-training: the architecture is identical to its predecessor four months earlier.
