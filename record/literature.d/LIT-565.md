# LIT-565: FLUX-Reason-6M & PRISM-Bench: A Million-Scale Text-to-Image Reasoning Dataset and Comprehensive Benchmark

Fang, Yu, Duan et al., CUHK, HKU, BUAA and Alibaba (2025) —
[ARXIV-2509.09680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09680)

## Key takeaways

- **The dataset:** 6M images generated by FLUX with 20M English and Chinese
  descriptions, grouped by six characteristics, each with a step-by-step
  "Generation Chain-of-Thought" description. 15,000 A100 GPU-days to curate
- **The benchmark:** PRISM-Bench, seven tracks including a long-text track
  built from the GCoT prompts, scored for alignment and aesthetics by VLM
  judges. 19 models evaluated
