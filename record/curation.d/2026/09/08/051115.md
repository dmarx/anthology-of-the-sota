The four notes deferred from the scope-correction pass, now read. All four
were migration stubs whose bullets named the area rather than the method.
Two attributions were checkable and one was wrong.

**GPTQ ([LIT-081](../../record/literature.d/LIT-081.md)) sources a practice.** Error-compensating one-shot
post-training quantization: round column by column and push each rounding
error into the columns not yet done, using second-order information from a
calibration set. 3–4 bits at 175B in about four GPU-hours, where reaching
that before had meant quantization-aware training. Filed as the *approach*
rather than the artifact — the specific search and ordering have been
improved on many times since, and what converged is the shape.

**Three notes carry no practice, and now say so.** This is the distinction
worth recording, because "no practice" covers two quite different situations
and the record could not previously tell them apart:

- **P-Tuning v2 ([LIT-055](../../record/literature.d/LIT-055.md)) is a decision.** The record recommends low-rank
  adaptation for the same job, and recommending both would be recommending
  neither. But the note stays `Active` because the comparison is the content:
  deep prefix tuning is competitive on *quality* and lost on *deployment
  shape* — a prefix occupies context at every layer forever, where a low-rank
  update folds into the weight and disappears. A corpus holding only the
  winner cannot say that.
- **The lottery-ticket pair ([LIT-019](../../record/literature.d/LIT-019.md), [LIT-039](../../record/literature.d/LIT-039.md)) is a gap, and a different
  gap than it looks.** The record holds no sparsity or pruning practice at
  all, which is not a scope decision — sparsity is expressible in
  `model-architecture` and there is live work on it. But these two notes are
  not what would close it. LTH's procedure costs a full dense training run
  before producing anything, so as an instruction it reads "train the model,
  then train a smaller one." And Evci et al. is the deflation: a rewound
  ticket re-learns the solution the dense run already found rather than
  discovering a good sparse architecture. The record needs both, precisely so
  the hypothesis is not carried unqualified.

**The attribution error is the interesting one.** [LIT-039](../../record/literature.d/LIT-039.md) was filed as
Frankle. It is a lottery-ticket paper, and Frankle wrote the lottery-ticket
paper, so the guess is nearly right and completely wrong — Evci et al. This
is the same generator as [LIT-029](../../record/literature.d/LIT-029.md)'s fabricated RMSNorm bullet and [LIT-082](../../record/literature.d/LIT-082.md)'s
Askell: describing a work from its subject produces text that is plausible in
every respect except being true. Three instances now, all from one migration.
