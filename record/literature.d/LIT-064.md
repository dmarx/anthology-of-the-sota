## Standing in the anthology

**Carries no practice, and this is a reading rather than an assumption.**

Instant-NGP's contribution is a multiresolution hash encoding — an input representation that trades hash collisions for a small, GPU-friendly table, which is what makes the training fast. The multiresolution hash encoding trades hash collisions for a small GPU-resident table, and that is what makes the training fast rather than any change to the network. **No practice yet, and the reason is a gap in the topic vocabulary rather than in the paper**: this is an input-encoding claim, and the record's encoding practices are currently scattered — RoPE sits in `attention-techniques`, quantization formats in `systems-optimization`. Filing it well needs that settled first.
