### Added

- **Analyzing and Improving the Training Dynamics of Diffusion Models** —
  `LIT-720`. Karras, Aittala, Lehtinen, Hellsten, Aila and Laine (2023),
  `2312.02696`. Filed against a measured gap: the record holds six practices about
  averaging weights and, before this note, **no document in it mentioned post-hoc
  EMA or said the averaging length could be chosen after the run.**

  **Post-hoc EMA.** Keep two power-function averages during training
  (`σ_rel` 0.05 and 0.10), store both in each periodic snapshot, and afterwards
  reconstruct the average for any length by least-squares fit over the stored
  profiles. Reconstruction error falls as **`O(1/n⁴)`** in snapshot count; a few
  dozen snapshots is "virtually perfect". It works **retroactively** from a single
  stored average per snapshot at reduced accuracy, so runs that already finished
  can have their averaging length swept.

  **The result that reaches outside diffusion (Fig. 12).** With the averaging
  length pinned at 13%, varying the learning-rate decay across
  `t_ref ∈ [30k, 160k]` moves FID **by up to 72%**. Sweeping the length post hoc
  puts the whole bracket **within 10% of the optimum**.

  Also carried: why a power function rather than an exponential (a long
  exponential weights near-random initial parameters; a power profile stretches
  with training time and gives `θ(0)` weight zero); the `σ_rel` parameterization;
  the optimum differing between configurations, narrowing as the architecture
  improves, and drifting longer through training; different weight tensors wanting
  different lengths, worth **10% FID** in Config B (7.24 → ~6.5) from a *very
  short* length in one case and a *very long* one in another; and the
  magnitude-preserving architecture, FID **1.91** on ImageNet-512 without
  guidance, with EDM2-S at 2.56 on 102 Gflops and 63 NFE against VDM++'s 2.99 on
  555 Gflops and 256 NFE.

- **`SOTA-432`**, `Active`/`unreplicated` — maintain the two averages and
  snapshot them, so the averaging length is a post-hoc sweep. `Active` despite one
  group because the reconstruction is arithmetic rather than an empirical
  regularity; `unreplicated` because the payoff — that sweeping it changes which
  other hyperparameters matter — is measured once, in one setting, on FID.

- **`THEORY-111`**, `Proposed` — a hyperparameter can look important because
  it is entangled with an averaging length you froze. A sensitivity sweep
  attributes interactions to the knob being moved, and the averaging length is an
  unusually bad thing to hold fixed: chosen pre-run, rarely reported, with an
  optimum that tracks architecture, duration and the other hyperparameters. The
  `promote_when` asks for the same 2×2 outside diffusion on a non-FID metric, and
  notes it can be run on finished runs.

### Changed

- **`SOTA-415` v2 — qualifies one of its two reasons for uniform averaging.**
  "It has no hyperparameter" described the scheme, not the choice: the practice's
  own next bullet is a heuristic for tuning a window. The hyperparameter is
  avoidable rather than unaffordable, and `SOTA-432` is how. Recommendation,
  status and consensus unchanged.

- **`SOTA-408` v2** — its "the extra epochs are real compute" objection is a
  property of its method, not of weight averaging: `SOTA-432` accumulates
  inside the run and pays storage. Different settings, never compared.

### Measured, not closed

**Nothing here is measured outside ImageNet-512 diffusion**, while the record's
other averaging practices are about ResNet classification, fine-tuned CLIP models
and language-model pretraining. The reconstruction arithmetic is
setting-independent; the entanglement claim is not.

**Per-tensor averaging lengths are open**, explicitly left unexplored by the
authors — and in their final configuration the per-tensor gain vanished, which
suggests it may be a diagnostic for an unfinished architecture rather than a knob.
