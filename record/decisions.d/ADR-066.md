# ADR-066: Two subtopics under generative modeling

## Context

The owner asked how the anthology covered flow methods, then for the gaps
to be filed, and suggested the large catch-all topics might take a subtopic
or two. The count bears that out. `generative-modeling` carries 169
documents, 115 of them as primary, and its blurb ("diffusion, samplers,
text-to-image, conditioning and control") holds GANs, tokenizers, video
systems, evaluation and flow theory alike. `inference-optimization` carries
89, and few-step generation is split between the two: progressive
distillation and consistency models sit under one, DMD2 and CausVid under
both, DPM-Solver under the first only.

The flow-methods batch adds about twenty more documents to exactly this
seam, so a reader asking "what does the anthology hold on flow matching"
or "on one-step samplers" would have to read the whole of
`generative-modeling` to find out.

## Decision

Add two words to `topics`, in the secondary-only section beside
`tiny-models` and `capability-thresholds`:

- **`flows-and-transport`**: generative models as learned transport between
  distributions — flow matching, rectified flow, stochastic interpolants,
  normalizing flows and neural ODEs, and the paths, couplings and time
  schedules they are trained on. Gaussian diffusion is the special case
  ([THEORY-106](../theory.d/THEORY-106.md)), so a diffusion paper takes it only when its argument runs
  through the flow or transport formulation.
- **`few-step-generation`**: getting a sample in one or a few network
  evaluations — progressive, distribution-matching and adversarial
  distillation, consistency, flow-map, shortcut and mean-flow models, and
  fast ODE solvers.

**They are subtopics by convention, not by mechanism.** A document carries
one beside its parent topic, never as its first tag, so `primary_topic` and
every chain invariant that compares it are unchanged. Nothing checks this;
it is the same unchecked convention `tiny-models` lives under.

tag, so no primary topic moved:

  Flow Matching" ([LIT-678](../literature.d/LIT-678.md)), SiT ([LIT-447](../literature.d/LIT-447.md)), SD3 ([LIT-449](../literature.d/LIT-449.md)), Score SDE
  ([LIT-722](../literature.d/LIT-722.md)), FLUX.1 Kontext ([LIT-573](../literature.d/LIT-573.md)), Movie Gen ([LIT-626](../literature.d/LIT-626.md)) and Wan
  ([LIT-619](../literature.d/LIT-619.md)); [SOTA-263](../practices.d/SOTA-263.md), [SOTA-265](../practices.d/SOTA-265.md), [SOTA-266](../practices.d/SOTA-266.md), [SOTA-392](../practices.d/SOTA-392.md) and [THEORY-106](../theory.d/THEORY-106.md).
- `few-step-generation`: Consistency Models ([LIT-093](../literature.d/LIT-093.md)), progressive

  [SOTA-206](../practices.d/SOTA-206.md), [SOTA-289](../practices.d/SOTA-289.md), [SOTA-392](../practices.d/SOTA-392.md) and [SOTA-394](../practices.d/SOTA-394.md).

The flow-methods batch filed in the same contribution takes them as well.

## Alternatives considered

- **Promote `topics` to a scheme**, so each term can name a parent. This is
  what luria recommends once values need a hierarchy, and it would let the
  views nest subtopics under their parent and the lint check the
  convention. It is a structural migration, needs a newer luria than the
  0.33.3 this record pins, and touches every document's tags. Worth doing
  if subtopics multiply; not for two.
- **Split `generative-modeling` into primary topics**, so that a flow paper
  no longer takes `generative-modeling` first. That moves the primary topic
  of dozens of documents and every chain that compares it, to fix a
  browsing problem that a secondary word fixes without moving anything.
- **One word, `diffusion-and-flows`.** Most of `generative-modeling` is
  diffusion, so the word would be nearly as large as its parent and browse
  no better.
