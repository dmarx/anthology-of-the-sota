Follow-up to the Wan filing in this PR. The Wan report's related work and
comparison tables name its predecessors and its competitors, and the record
held none of them. Nine were filed:

    VDM ([LIT-tmpvcgyq](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpvcgyq.md)) -> Video LDM ([LIT-tmpl3mo9](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpl3mo9.md)) -> SVD ([LIT-tmprf8ak](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmprf8ak.md))
    MAGVIT-v2 ([LIT-tmpmi3yo](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpmi3yo.md)) -> CogVideoX ([LIT-tmplthcn](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmplthcn.md)), HunyuanVideo ([LIT-tmpkcchp](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpkcchp.md)), Wan ([LIT-tmpbr2sl](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbr2sl.md))
    Movie Gen ([LIT-tmpsjfid](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpsjfid.md)), Step-Video ([LIT-tmpqns7l](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpqns7l.md)), LTX-Video ([LIT-tmp75yny](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp75yny.md))

## How they were read

Three delegated readers each read three full papers and returned an
extraction: numbers with table references, evidence type per claim, hedges,
lineage evidence, and what was skipped. I wrote the notes from those
extractions. None of the nine has a `NOTE`, and every LIT note says so and
says what was read. They are sources, not readings.

## What the line turned out to be

**The large reports are adoption. The evidence is in the small early
papers.** Movie Gen is the one exception: it ran controlled 5B ablations of
flow matching against diffusion, video against frame captions, and its
transformer block. HunyuanVideo reports no controlled ablation. Step-Video's
architecture comparisons are unquantified. Wan's three ablations are at 1.3B
on text-to-image. The controlled evidence for training on images is VDM,
Video LDM and SVD, all small. That is why [SOTA-tmp3b17n](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-tmp3b17n.md) cites those three
and counts the large reports under `consensus_note` ([DP-005](../design-principles.md#dp-5)).

**Every one of the ten has at least one [DP-010](../design-principles.md#dp-10) hedge, most of them in the
abstract.** The recurring shape is a scaling or "state-of-the-art" sentence
that the body does not carry:

- Wan's "scaling laws" has no curve.
- Movie Gen's scaling fit is image-only and overlaid, not fit.
- HunyuanVideo's "5×" has no experiment.
- CogVideoX's scalability is two sizes.
- Step-Video's "state-of-the-art" is ranked second in its own §9.3.

## The relation I withdrew

I first declared Step-Video `extends` DPO ([LIT-169](../../record/literature.d/LIT-169.md)) and Movie Gen `extends`
Llama 3 ([LIT-179](../../record/literature.d/LIT-179.md)). Each edge was bound, since they share a tag, but together
they produced **one unbound line**. The diffusion lineage, the preference
lineage and the language-model architecture lineage became one component
with no tag in common. [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md) allows two readings, and this was the second
one: the relation was wrong. Neither report is a successor to those papers.
Each borrows one component from them. Both are now plain citations in the
prose.

A trap for the next person: **deleting `extends` on one side is not enough.**
`link --fix` read the leftover `extended_by` on [LIT-169](../../record/literature.d/LIT-169.md) and [LIT-179](../../record/literature.d/LIT-179.md) and wrote
the `extends` back. Withdraw a relation by removing both ends, then run the
fixer.

## Not filed

  simulators"). openai.com returned 403 and web.archive.org refused the
  connection from this environment. It exists only as a `url:` source, and
  every report here cites it. It should be filed from a machine that can
  reach it.
- **Imagen Video, Make-A-Video, Emu Video, Open-Sora, AnimateDiff,
  CausVid / Self-Forcing.** These are real nodes of the line and were cut to
  keep the PR reviewable. Emu Video is the one Movie Gen says it builds on.
- **Candidate practices still unfiled.** The text-encoder question (T5 or
  umT5, multimodal LLM, or several encoders; four reports, four answers, no
  cross-test), full 3D attention over factorized attention (CogVideoX
  measured it, the rest adopt it), and flow matching over diffusion for video
  (Movie Gen alone). The last has one source and is the next one to file.
