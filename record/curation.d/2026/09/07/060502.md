Two items off [#18](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/18), both of which could have gone either way and both of
which came back mostly negative. Negative results are the ones that go
unrecorded, so they are recorded here.

## The spot-check found no misalignment

[#18](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/18) flagged that the benchmark figures in [SOTA-121](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-121.md) – [SOTA-128](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-128.md) came from a
PyMuPDF extraction of the Falcon-H1-Tiny blogpost's PDF export, and that
table extraction can silently misalign columns. The rendered post is
reachable now, so every figure the record attributes to it was checked
against the HTML tables.

They all hold, and the column assignments in particular hold:

- [SOTA-123](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-123.md)'s IFEval pair, `50.1 vs 40.8` before DPO and `66.1 vs 53.5`
  after, maps onto the post's four columns — SFT 40.77, SFT-DPO 53.47,
  SFT-pretrain 50.11, SFT-pretrain-DPO 66.08 — in the right order, which is
  exactly what a misaligned extraction would have got wrong.
- [SOTA-123](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-123.md)'s reasoning row (AIME24 6/30 vs 3/30, AIME25 9/30 vs 2/30,
  MATH500 0.4 vs 0.2), [SOTA-126](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-126.md)'s `40.8 to 53.5`, [LIT-119](../../record/literature.d/LIT-119.md)'s post-GRPO
  AIME24 75.0 and MATH500 94.0: all exact.
- The prose numbers too: [SOTA-125](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-125.md)'s 27-layer/512-hidden mid config against
  the 50-layer deep one, the "roughly a 2x decrease for training
  throughput", the reduction from 27 layers to 24 to land on 90M — all
  verbatim from the post's own config listings.

One thing did not survive. [SOTA-122](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-122.md) quoted "up to a 20% relative gain on
MMLU, BBH and GSM8K" and dropped the four words that follow it in the
source: **from random values**. At 90M those three benchmarks sit near
chance, so a fifth of the distance above chance is a much smaller claim than
a fifth of the score. That is not a transcription error of the kind [#18](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/18)
worried about — the table was fine — it is a summarisation error, which is
the failure mode reading a rendered table would never have caught.

## None of the Proposed practices moves

[#18](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/18) asked for the practices whose promotion condition was "the larger-scale
evidence lives in the preprint" to be re-examined now that the preprints are
readable. [SOTA-133](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-133.md) turns out to have been promoted to Active already. Of the
rest:

**[SOTA-136](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-136.md) (mHC)** was the interesting one. Half its condition asks for "a
production report at depth whose streams stay distinct", and DeepSeek-V4 is
the production report at depth — 1.6T, shipping mHC. Read in full, it does
not answer the objection. Its argument for the doubly-stochastic constraint
is that projecting onto the manifold bounds the spectral norm at 1, so the
residual transformation is non-expansive and both passes are numerically
more stable. That is the property [LIT-151](../../record/literature.d/LIT-151.md) grants and then turns around: a
mapping that can only contract can only erode what distinguishes the
streams. V4 reports no stream statistic, and *homogenize*, *diversity* and
*distinct* appear nowhere in it. So the condition is unmet by the report
most likely to have met it, and the practice now says so — because a reader
who knows mHC ships at 1.6T will otherwise assume shipping settled it.

**[SOTA-141](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-141.md) (decay to zero)** gained a sharper version of its own
consensus_note. DeepSeek-V4 decays to exactly 10% of peak, which is the
customary floor [LIT-147](../../record/literature.d/LIT-147.md) names in its first sentence and argues against. Not
a team that weighed decaying to zero and declined — a team that used the
default, at 33T tokens.

It also gained a trap worth naming. K3's schedule study *looks* like the
second half of [SOTA-141](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-141.md)'s promotion condition, which asks for "a second
group's schedule comparison with the peak retuned per schedule". K3 is
exactly that. But it runs the comparison "under a fixed minimum learning
rate", and the minimum is this practice's variable. A study can satisfy the
methodology a practice demands and still not be about it, and a promotion
condition written in terms of method rather than question is how you fail to
notice.

**[SOTA-122](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-122.md) (learnable multipliers)** and **[SOTA-144](../../record/practices.d/SOTA-144.md) (CompleteP)** both need
an independent group adopting the technique. Nothing in the four reports
read this session does. Searched, not found, and that is the whole result.
