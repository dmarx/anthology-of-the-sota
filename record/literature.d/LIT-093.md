# LIT-093: Consistency Models

Song et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2303.01469](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01469)

## Key takeaways

- Trains a model to map **any** point on a diffusion trajectory directly to its
  origin — self-consistency along the trajectory — so generation is **one**
  function evaluation. Multistep sampling stays available, so the compute/quality
  dial survives
- **Two training modes, and the second is the contribution.** Consistency
  *distillation* needs a pretrained diffusion model; consistency *training*
  needs none, which makes these an independent model family rather than an
  acceleration technique
- **One-step FID 3.55 on CIFAR-10, 6.20 on ImageNet-64**, beating prior
  distillation methods; trained in isolation they beat existing one-step
  **non-adversarial** models on CIFAR-10, ImageNet-64 and LSUN-256
- **A bias/variance schedule on the discretisation:** the training loss has less
  variance and more bias when `N` is small and the reverse when `N` is large, so
  `N` is *increased* during training on a schedule, with the EMA decay `μ`
  scheduled alongside it. Fast biased progress early, accurate noisy refinement
  late
- Zero-shot inpainting, colorization and super-resolution with no task-specific
  training. The authors flag that Theorem 3 is **vacuous for the `ℓ₁` norm** and
  prove a separate statement
