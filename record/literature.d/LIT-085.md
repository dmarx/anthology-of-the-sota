# LIT-085: Progress measures for grokking via mechanistic interpretability

Nanda et al. (2023) — [ARXIV-2301.05217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217)

## Key takeaways

- **Reverse-engineers the network rather than fitting a curve to its loss.** A
  one-layer transformer on `a + b mod 113` is shown to implement a **Fourier
  multiplication algorithm**: the embedding maps inputs to sines and cosines at
  a sparse set of key frequencies, attention and MLP combine them by
  trigonometric identities into `sin/cos(w_k(a+b))`, and the output matrices
  recombine. Addition by rotation on a circle
- The unembedding is approximately **rank 10** — five frequencies × sine and
  cosine — and **individual neurons are well approximated by degree-2
  polynomials of sines and cosines at a *single* frequency**, with the matching
  output direction carrying only that frequency. Computation is localised by
  frequency and mostly aligned with the neuron basis
- Progress measures defined *from* the recovered algorithm (restricted and
  excluded loss) show grokking is **three phases: memorization, circuit
  formation, cleanup** — continuous progress under an apparent discontinuity
- **Grokking disappears above about 60% data.** Sufficiently large data
  fractions generalize immediately; smaller fractions grok more slowly. The
  delayed generalization is a data-starved-regime phenomenon, not a fundamental
  one
- One task, `P = 113`, one layer, weight decay `λ = 1`, 40,000 epochs. The
  authors call the generalisation to emergence "a proof of concept"

## Standing in the anthology

Read — [NOTE-tmpmgnf7](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpmgnf7.md). The document is `Rejected` and this reading does not argue
with that: a 113-element modular-addition task on a one-layer transformer is not
a source for practice at this record's scale.

But `Rejected` and *worth reading* are different, which is what `ADR-002`'s
two-scheme split exists for. The reason to keep it is the data-fraction result:
**grokking is the most-cited mysterious training phenomenon of its period and it
goes away above 60% data.** Any claim about delayed generalization should know
that.

None of the takeaways it carried said what the network was found to be doing —
which is the result, and the thing that makes the progress measures definable at
all.
